Why are rainbows curved? 🌈 Because a rainbow is really part of a circle. Sunlight enters each raindrop, bends, splits into colours, reflects off the back of the drop and comes back out. Red light returns at about 42° from the point straight opposite the Sun, the shadow of your head. Only drops at that angle send colour to your eye, and all of them together form a cone with its tip at your eye: a circle. The ground hides the bottom, so you see an arc. From a plane or a mountaintop you can see the whole circle. A fainter second bow sits at about 50° with its colours flipped.

Sources: USGS Water Science School (with NCAR/UCAR); Arizona State University PHS 208 "Rainbows"; UNLV "Rainbow explication".
