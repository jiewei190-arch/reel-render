# rainbow: opening lines (pick marked ★)

1. ★ "Why are rainbows curved?" (4 words: the exact search question). Frame 0: a cyanotype drafting sheet titled THE RAINBOW. In the figure window a big spectrum bow fills the frame with rain falling, and the camera rides the arc. The title block reads STATUS: AN ARC? At the but-turn (2.0 s, "But a rainbow isn't really an arc. It's part of a circle, and everyone sees a different one.") the camera pulls back and the hidden lower half is drafted in as a dashed circle; then someone else's circle is drawn in phantom line beside it. STATUS changes to PART OF A CIRCLE.
2. "Every rainbow is secretly a full circle." (7 words). Strong, but it spoils the payoff and isn't the searched question.
3. "You and your friend never see the same rainbow." (9 words). A great re-hook, so I kept it for 46 s.

Why ★1: it's the question people type, and "it's a circle" is the twist by 2 s.
Re-hooks: 22.6 s "Here's the trick" (only drops at exactly 42° light up, then the cone); 41.8 s "from a plane you can see the whole circle"; 46 s "your rainbow isn't the one the person next to you sees".
Payoff 58.2 s: "So a rainbow is curved because it's a circle of light around your shadow, and the ground hides the rest." (the red CIRCLE stamp). Question: "Have you ever seen a full-circle rainbow? Yes or no?"
