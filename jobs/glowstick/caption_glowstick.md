Snap a glow stick and it lights up with no battery, no bulb and no wire. Inside the tube floats a thin glass vial of hydrogen peroxide. Bend it, the glass cracks, and the peroxide meets diphenyl oxalate. They build a very unstable molecule that falls apart into carbon dioxide and hands its energy to a dye, which gives it off as light. That's cold light: almost no heat. Warm it up and it glows brighter but quits sooner; cool it down and it lasts longer. 🎃
