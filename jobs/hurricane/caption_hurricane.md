A hurricane is a giant engine, and its fuel is warm ocean water. Warm sea (about 27 C / 80 F) evaporates, the moist air rises and condenses into storm clouds, and Earth's rotation bends the inflow into a spin. Winds of 74 mph make it a hurricane, with a calm eye inside an eyewall of the strongest winds.

Sources: NOAA "Hurricanes"; Wikipedia "Tropical cyclone".
