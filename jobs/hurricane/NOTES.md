# NOTES: hurricane

**Style: Cinematic 3D** (awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles, `styles/cinematic3d.md`): Three.js/WebGL scene with PBR ocean (rough/metal parameters), one strong warm key light (low sun), slow dolly and orbit camera moves, vignette and film grain, a colour grade that goes from warm dusk to stormy teal-grey as the storm builds.

**Why:** a hurricane is about scale, light and a camera that rises from the warm sea surface to a top-down view of the spiral and then dives toward the eye. A 3D camera lets the story follow the air upward. Procedural storm: about 1100 cloud sprites on spiral arms, plus an eyewall ring.

Look: dusk #f2a26b sky, teal ocean, white/grey clouds, labels as small amber chips. Inter. Voice af_heart 0.97, music crayon bpm 88 (leaf settings). Three.js r160 (MIT), vendored locally at `reels/vendor/three.module.js` (not committed to the repo; `npm i three@0.160.0`).
