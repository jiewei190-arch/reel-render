Do goldfish really have a 3-second memory? 🐟 Not even close. At the University of Plymouth, goldfish learned to work a lever that fed them only one hour a day, and they crowded it just before feeding time. In Israel, fish trained to a feeding sound reportedly came back to it months after release. In Japan, goldfish even learned to tell Bach from Stravinsky. Fish researcher Culum Brown says goldfish memories can last weeks, months, even years.

Sources: Gee, Stephenson & Wright, J. Exp. Anal. Behav. (1994); Shinozuka, Ono & Watanabe, Behavioural Processes (2013); Live Science (Culum Brown, Macquarie University).
