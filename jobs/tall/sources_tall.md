## Softened / attributed claims (report to the owner)
- "Almost 2 cm" = 19.3 mm average of 8 young adults in one 1985 study; said as "in one study".
- "About 23 discs" (Short) / "around two dozen" (long): counts differ (23 without the sacral disc, 25 with it).
- "A quarter" (Short) simplifies the 25-33% range; the long-form says "a quarter to a third".
- "3% taller" is attributed to NASA scientists as quoted in popular press (2013); I did not find the primary NASA paper.
- "Most of your height returns in the first half of the night" = about 70% (Tyrrell 1985).
- MRI 5-10% and StatPearls figures were read through search summaries because the pages blocked automated access; open them once before publishing.
- "Measure at the same time each day" and "the morning mark should sit a little higher" are conclusions drawn from the studies, not separate findings.
- Health topic: educational only, no medical advice.
