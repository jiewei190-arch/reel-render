You're shorter right now than when you woke up 📏 In one study, people lost almost 2 cm of height every day. The reason is the soft, water-filled discs in your spine: gravity squeezes them flatter all day, and they soak water back up while you sleep.

Sources: Tyrrell, Reilly & Troup, Spine 1985 (circadian variation in stature); StatPearls, Intervertebral Discs (NCBI); NASA scientists via Scientific American, 2013.
