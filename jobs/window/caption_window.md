That tiny hole at the bottom of a plane window isn't a flaw. Windows have three panes. The hole in the middle one lets cabin air into the gap, so the pressure pushing out lands on the outer pane, with the middle pane as a backup. It also lets moisture out so the window doesn't fog up.
