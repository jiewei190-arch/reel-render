How does a zipper lock two sides of fabric together? Each tooth has a rounded bump and a matching hollow. The slider's Y-shaped channel pushes the two rows together, one pair of teeth at a time, and the teeth snap in and spring back, so the zipper stays locked. Pull the slider the other way and the same Y pushes them apart.
