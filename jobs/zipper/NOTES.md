# NOTES: zipper

**Style: Papercut** (lemo-opuscar `styles/papercut-red/STYLE.md`, grammar only): every visible thing is cut paper laid on paper, with no strokes or gradients. Red paper is the hero on an indigo paper ground (different families so the red pops), paper grain in each piece, hand-cut edge wobble, a light rim upper-left and dark rim lower-right, tight soft shadows, inset yin contour on pieces, a red paper subtitle strip with serrated edge, and gold foil reserved for the slider/accents. Pieces step at 12 fps.

**Why:** a zipper is itself a thing made of small interlocking pieces, and a papercut look makes the bump-and-hollow snap-fit read like a craft diagram. I stayed with the suggested style.

Look: ground #1b2450-#2f3d82, red #d2201f, gold #f2b632, cream #f3e7cf; Bodoni Moda lettering. Voice af_heart 0.97, music crayon bpm 88 (leaf settings). Implemented as original canvas code, no project assets.
