Open question: why do we sneeze? The chain: an irritant (dust, pollen, a germ) in the nose → the trigeminal nerve → a signal to the brainstem, where scientists think a sneeze centre sits (marked as unconfirmed in humans) → eyes shut, deep breath (re-hook at 24.7 s), pressure, blast → irritants washed out. Then the wow: about one in four people sneeze in bright light (the photic sneeze reflex; 25.6% in a 2019 study), and nobody knows why ("one idea"). Then the re-hook at 50.4 s: does your heart stop?
