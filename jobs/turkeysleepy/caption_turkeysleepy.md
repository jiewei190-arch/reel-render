Does turkey really make you sleepy? Not by itself. Turkey has tryptophan, but about the same as other poultry like chicken (roughly a quarter of a gram per 100 g), and cheddar and nuts have even more. The real culprits are the whole plate: a huge meal, lots of carbs (stuffing, bread, potatoes, pie) and a drink or two. Carbs also release insulin, which helps the tryptophan you do eat reach your brain. So the turkey is innocent. Do you nap after Thanksgiving dinner?

Sources: Oklahoma State University (Darren Scott, FAPC); Boston University (Joan Salge Blake); Live Science; Texas Health Resources.
