Why does popcorn pop? Each kernel holds a little water (about 13-14.5%) inside a tough hull. Heat it and the water gets hotter than boiling, the starch turns to gel, and at about 180 °C the pressure reaches around ten times normal air pressure. The hull cracks, the steam bursts out and the gel puffs into foam that sets solid in hundredths of a second. The pop sound? High-speed video shows it lines up with the steam rushing out, not the crack. 🍿

Sources: Virot & Ponomarenko, J. R. Soc. Interface (2015); Hoseney et al., Journal of Cereal Science (1983); Purdue University Agriculture (Hamaker, 2020).
