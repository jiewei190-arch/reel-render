# Sources: lock (blueprint style)
- Pin tumbler mechanism (plug in housing, shear line, spring + driver pin + key pin stacks, key lifts pins to the shear line, wrong key leaves a pin straddling it): Britannica "lock" article; HowStuffWorks "How Locks Work"; Wikipedia "Pin tumbler lock".
- Cam at the back of the plug pulls the bolt: HowStuffWorks "How Locks Work".
- Oldest known pin locks ~ ancient Egypt/Mesopotamia, wooden: Britannica "lock". Reel says "thousands of years old", no exact date.
- Linus Yale Jr. patented the modern compact cylinder lock (1861 and 1865 patents): Britannica "Linus Yale, Jr."; reel says "in the 1860s".
- Not verified / illustrative: cut-depth digits and the exact pin counts (real locks use five or six pins, per the above).
