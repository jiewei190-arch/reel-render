Why do we blink so much? About 15 times a minute (American Academy of Ophthalmology), more often than your eyes strictly need for wetting. Each blink spreads tears across the eye, and on a screen your rate can drop to about 5-7 a minute. So scientists asked when we blink: people watching the same video blinked together, at calm moments like the end of an action or when the main character left the screen. A brain-scan study then found that right after a blink, attention areas dipped while the default mode network (a resting network) became more active, and simply blacking out the screen didn't do the same. One idea: blinks may work as tiny breaks for your attention. These are findings, not settled facts. 👁️

Sources: American Academy of Ophthalmology; Nakano et al., Proceedings of the Royal Society B (2009); Nakano et al., PNAS (2013, online Dec 2012); Osaka University press release.
