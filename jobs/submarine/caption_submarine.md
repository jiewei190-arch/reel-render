To dive, a submarine lets the ocean in, on purpose. 🌊 So how does it ever come back up? Vents open, air rushes out, seawater floods the ballast tanks and it slips under. Trim tanks make it hover, diving planes steer, and a blast of compressed air pushes the water back out to bring it home. Would you trust a bubble of air to bring you home?
