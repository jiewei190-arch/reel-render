A submarine dives by letting the ocean in, on purpose. 🌊 Vents open, air rushes out, seawater floods the ballast tanks and it slips under. Trim tanks make it hover, diving planes steer, and a blast of compressed air pushes the water back out to bring it home. Would you trust a bubble of air to get you back up?
