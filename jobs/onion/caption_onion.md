Why do onions make you cry? The onion isn't sad, it's defending itself. Cutting rips its cells open, so enzymes meet sulfur chemicals and make a stinging gas, syn-propanethial-S-oxide. The gas drifts into your eyes, your nerves warn your brain, and your tear glands flush it away.

Sources: Wikipedia "Lachrymatory factor"; Discover Magazine; The Conversation; Imai et al., Nature 2002.
