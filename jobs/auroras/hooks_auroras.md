Open question: what causes the northern lights? The chain is the Sun (the solar wind, CMEs) → Earth's magnetic field catches the particles and steers them to the poles (re-hook at 25 s) → they slam into the air 50-300 miles up and make it glow → the colour key (oxygen green or red, nitrogen blue and purple; the wow at 38-43 s). Then the timely twist: the 11-year cycle peaked in 2024 (re-hook at 46 s), and the May 2024 G5 storm reached places that rarely see auroras. Payoff: "the Sun's wind, painted on our sky by Earth's magnetic field".
