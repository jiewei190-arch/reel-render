Why 1: everyone who owns a cat has felt it, so the first frame is their own lap; line 2 turns it straight into the search question. Line 2 spends the nursing answer at frame 0; line 3 is a list hook with no image.
Open question: why do cats knead? The chain: newborn kittens press their mother's belly, which helps get the milk flowing (oxytocin); push, milk, warmth and safety arrive together (four notes fly in, re-hook at 24 s); the twist that grown-up cats never stopped (the NEVER STOPPED stamp, 28 s); neoteny. Theory #1 is comfort, with purring. Theory #2 (the room goes dark, re-hook at 43 s) is the scent glands in the paw pads releasing pheromones, ending in the MINE! stamp (the wow, ~54 s). Theory #3 is wild cats trampling grass into a bed. Payoff at 60.7 s: "a cat that feels safe, and maybe one that's claiming you". Then the vet check, the blanket tip, "Biscuits?", and the end card. Every explanation is marked as a theory or idea.
