## Softened / dropped claims
- "Cats see in about 1/6 of the light we need" (popular figure, sources disagree: 1/6, 6x, 7x): **dropped**; the script says the mirror "helps cats make the most of dim light".
- "130 times more light reflected" (Wikipedia, unclear wording): dropped.
- "Kittens' eyes often shine blue; grown cats yellow to green" follows Wikipedia's "young cats blue, shifting to yellow with age; adults light orange to green".
- "Many night animals ... dogs, owls, some spiders": Wikipedia says most spiders have a tapetum (in their lateral eyes) and many owls/night birds do; worded "some spiders".
- The eye diagram is simplified (retina cells as a row of capsules, tapetum as a band behind them); real cat tapetum covers only part of the back of the eye.
