Turn off the lights and at first you see nothing. Wait twenty minutes and the room slowly appears. 🌙 Your rods rebuild a light-catching pigment called rhodopsin, and you can end up as much as a million times more sensitive. One bright flash undoes it, which is why pilots and ship crews use red light. Could you sit in the dark for twenty minutes?
