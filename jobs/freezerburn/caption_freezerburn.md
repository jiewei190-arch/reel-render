What is freezer burn? It isn't a burn: your food is drying out. Frozen food is full of tiny ice crystals, and freezer air is very dry, so ice at the surface turns straight into vapor without melting first (sublimation). The surface dries into tough, leathery patches, and air creeps in and changes the color and flavor. It's safe to eat, just dry and tough. The fix: keep the air out. Wrap tightly, squeeze the air out of freezer bags, use small containers, and keep the freezer at 0°F or colder.

Sources: University of Tennessee food scientist Tong Wang (The Conversation); Texas A&M AgriLife Extension; NDSU Extension (citing USDA FSIS).
