The October moon can rise huge and orange over the hills, but it isn't really bigger and it isn't really orange. Near the horizon, moonlight crosses much more air, which scatters the blue away (the same reason sunsets look red). And the size? Your fingernail at arm's length covers it low or high, and photos show the same width: the giant moon is an illusion in your brain. Even astronauts in orbit see it, and scientists still can't fully explain why. 🌕🍂
