Why does your voice sound weird on recordings? When you talk, your voice reaches your ears two ways: through the air, and through your skull (bone conduction), which boosts the low, deep tones. So in your head you sound deeper and fuller. A microphone only hears the air path, so the recording sounds thinner and higher, and that's the voice everyone else hears. In a 2023 study, people picked out their own voice more easily when it played through bone-conduction headphones. Do you like your voice on recordings?
