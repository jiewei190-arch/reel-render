Why can't you tickle yourself? Your own fingers are just as soft. The difference is your cerebellum: it predicts the touch from your own movement and tells other brain areas to turn the feeling down. Add a delay between movement and touch, as researchers did with a robot, and the ticklishness comes back.

Sources: Scientific American (Blakemore, UCL); Blakemore, Wolpert & Frith 1998, Nature Neuroscience; Wikipedia "Tickling".
