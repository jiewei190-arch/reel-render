Why does your foot fall asleep? 🦶 It isn't sleeping. Sit on your leg too long and the pressure squeezes a nerve and the tiny blood vessels that feed it, so the signals between your foot and your brain get blocked and it goes numb. The worst part comes after you move: blood flows back, the nerve restarts, some fibres stay quiet while others fire too much, and that messy restart is pins and needles. It fades quickly. Now and then it's harmless; if it happens often, with no pressure, or with weakness, see a doctor.

Sources: NINDS (NIH) Paresthesia information page; Cleveland Clinic, "Paresthesia"; Dr. Robert Daroff, Case Western Reserve University, via NBC News/LiveScience.
