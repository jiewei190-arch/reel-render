# feetasleep: NOTES (EP 80)

- **Template:** #18 handdrawn "Japan heat wave" field sketchbook (demo 101), via styles/fieldbook (the module behind thermos and earpop). Picked to avoid the EP 71-78 families.
- **Why this template:** a body-notes field sketchbook fits a nerve explainer:
  - graphite drawings that draw on;
  - red and navy pencil hatching as the only fills (navy = numb, red = signal and tingle);
  - double-ruled measurement boxes as nerve-signal readouts;
  - the demo's row of day boxes as a pins-and-needles timeline strip.
- **Pages:**
  - title: a big graphite foot, hatched numb, with twitching toes and rising Zzz's beside a red hatched sun (frame 0). At the but-turn the Zzz is struck out and a red nerve draws up the leg;
  - "Nerves are wires": signal dots ride the nerve to a brain; a LEG block presses on it; a cross-section box shows the nerve bundle and its blood vessel squashing; the dots stop at the pinch and the brain gets a "?";
  - "The worst part comes after": a big cross-section un-squashes, blood flows back, and fibres restart (some quiet, some too loud), with a SIGNAL readout going off → ok → messy. Then the foot fizzes with red pin marks beside tingle / buzz / prickle boxes;
  - "Arms do it too": a sleeper on their arm, the hand hatched numb;
  - "Move.": the foot wiggling, and the PRESSURE → NUMB → MOVE → TINGLE → NORMAL strip with the current step circled; "FADES quickly";
  - "It never slept.": the signal restored to the brain; then a when-to-see-a-doctor box (now and then = harmless; often / no pressure / with weakness = see a doctor);
  - "Foot or arm?"; then the end card (centred lines, round END seal, half disc on the horizon).
- **Layout:**
  - label at y 280 and headline at y 386; drawings y 430-1200;
  - typed caption at y 1240, x 80-880;
  - nothing in the right column below y 840; the top-right band is kept clear for the badge.
- **Search question:** spoken at 0.35 s. Hook: 6 words. "But" at 2.2 s.
- **Re-hooks:** 21.9 s "Now the weird part"; 40.1 s "And it's not just feet".
- **Payoff:** 49.9 s. Question: "foot, or your arm?"
- **Length:** 70.1 s, lengthened from 68.1 s at main's request: voice af_heart at 0.93 and two extra breaths. Popup bed 96 bpm; music_gain 0.6, fx_gain 0.35.
