Why is snow white if ice is clear? There's no white in snow at all. A snowbank is mostly air, packed with tiny clear ice crystals. Light bounces off and bends through crystal after crystal, and since ice barely absorbs visible light, every colour comes back out together: white. A smashed windowpane does the same thing. But poke a deep hole in fresh snow and it glows blue, because deep snow soaks up a little more red light. ❄️
