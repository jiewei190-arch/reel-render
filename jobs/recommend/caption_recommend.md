Out of millions of videos, only a few dozen ever reach your screen. YouTube's engineers described it as a funnel: pull out a few hundred that viewers like you enjoyed, then score each one. Back in 2011 YouTube learned that a click doesn't mean you watched, so its 2016 system predicted watch time instead, and today surveys, shares, likes and dislikes count too. Instagram says its key Reels guesses are whether you'll watch to the end, like it or reshare it. So what does your feed think you are?
