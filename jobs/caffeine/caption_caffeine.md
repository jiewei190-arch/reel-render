Coffee doesn't give you energy. It fools your brain. While you're awake, a chemical called adenosine builds up and makes you sleepy by plugging into receptors on your brain cells. Caffeine is close enough in shape to fit those same receptors, but it doesn't trigger them, so the sleepy message never arrives. The tiredness doesn't go away, it's just blocked, and caffeine's half-life is about four to six hours.
