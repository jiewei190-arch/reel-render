# Hooks: coldtaste (template #23 Recipe card)

Search question (spoken at 4.3 s, typed into a search bar): "Why can't you taste food when you have a cold?"

1. **[PICK]** "With a cold, your tongue works fine. It's your nose that quits." (12 words; pattern 7, myth correction). Frame 0: "Tongue: fine. / Nose: off." in brush hand over a big strawberry with a "?", the flavor meter beside it draining from full to none.
2. "Pinch your nose, and a jellybean loses its flavor." (9 words; pattern 6, a demo you can try).
3. "Most of what you call taste is actually smell." (9 words; pattern 2).
