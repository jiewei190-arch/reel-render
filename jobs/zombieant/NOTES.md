**Template:** #19 handdrawn "Sketch music visualization" (demo 102, @dan_riverbendai; fetched with reels/fetch_demos.sh 102). New reusable module: reels/styles/sketchmusic/sketchmusic.js.
**Why:** the brief asked for an eerie-calm, strictly non-gory take. The demo's quiet pencil landscape under soft pastel skies and slow rain keeps a creepy topic gentle, and its "one line building the scene in real time" suits a story that unfolds step by step (nest -> wander -> climb -> bite -> stalk -> spores).

**Copied from the demo (frames studied at 0, 50, 105, 160, 220, 265, 280 s):**
- Thin dark pencil line that draws every object on screen with a leading pen tip; nothing pops in.
- Pastel watercolour washes laid in after the line, slightly off the line: lavender -> rose -> peach sky, pale lavender-grey ground, mottled paper.
- Perspective ground lines to a vanishing point (the demo's road), bare tall trees, the peach sun disc crossed by thin horizontal strokes.
- Glassy 3D raindrops drifting over the drawing and puddle rings; they become the humid air and are echoed by the lavender spores.
- The walker with the red umbrella (scene "it can't infect you"), the single warm accent colour (red) per shot.
- Old film: sepia cast, grain, gate flicker, dust specks, occasional scratch, soft heavy vignette; slow camera glides/push-ins; gentle cross-dissolves; copperplate script titles ("The Zombie Ant", like "Pétales Givrés"); the film dims toward dark at the end (kept at 55% so the end card stays readable).
- Captions: handwritten pen karaoke on a soft paper strip (the demo has no captions; channel rule).
