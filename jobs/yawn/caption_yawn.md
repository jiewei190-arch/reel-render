Why are yawns contagious? Scientists still argue. Just thinking about yawning can set one off. Dogs can catch human yawns (21 of 29 in one study, though later results were mixed), and some chimps yawn at videos of yawning chimps. Idea 1: brain cooling. With a cold pack on the forehead, people caught far fewer yawns (9% vs 41%). Idea 2: it's social, since yawns spread most among family. But the empathy link is contested: in 328 people, catching yawns tracked age, not empathy. 🥱
