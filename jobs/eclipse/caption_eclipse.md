The Moon can hide the Sun in the middle of the day 🌑 In a solar eclipse the Moon slides between us and the Sun; its shadow has a dark inner umbra (totality) and a fainter outer penumbra. In a lunar eclipse Earth's shadow falls on the Moon, which glows dim red. Why not every month? The Moon's orbit is tilted about 5°. Safety: solar eclipses need real eclipse glasses, not sunglasses.
