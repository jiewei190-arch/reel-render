Your air fryer has never fried anything. 🍟 It's a tiny countertop convection oven: a heater, a fan, and air whipping around at up to 200 °C. Fast air strips away the thin "blanket" of still air around your food, the outside dries, and the Maillard reaction turns it golden. So: fryer, or very small oven?

Sources: Wikipedia "Air fryer", "Convection oven" (boundary layer), "Maillard reaction"; Philips Airfryer launch 2010, inventor Fred van der Weij (via Wikipedia).
