Why do wet dogs smell so bad? 🐕💧 It's not really the dog, it's who lives on it. A dog's skin and fur are home to normal, healthy bacteria and yeast, and a Texas A&M vet study found furry skin carries the richest mix of microbes. Those microbes give off tiny smelly molecules. On a dry dog they mostly stay put, but water knocks them loose from the fur, and as it evaporates it carries them up to your nose. In one small pilot study, wet dog hair had 22 key odor compounds vs 16 when dry: some mushroomy, some fruity or floral... and some fecal.

Sources: American Chemical Society, Reactions (2015); Rodrigues Hoffmann et al., PLoS ONE (2014), Texas A&M College of Veterinary Medicine; Young et al., WSAVA Congress (2002).
