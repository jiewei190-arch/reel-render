When you hit search, Google doesn't go out and search the web. It already did that. Crawlers follow links all day, and everything they find goes into an index of hundreds of billions of pages. Google compares it to the index at the back of a book. Your words pull up lists of pages, and the pages that match get ranked by meaning, relevance, quality, usability and context, all in a fraction of a second.
