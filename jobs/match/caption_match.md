Strike a match and fire appears, but half of the chemistry isn't on the match, it's on the box 🔥 The head holds the fuel (sulfur, antimony sulfide) and the oxygen supply (potassium chlorate). The striking strip holds red phosphorus: friction heats a trace of it, it ignites, and that sets off the head. Which everyday thing should we explain next? 👇
