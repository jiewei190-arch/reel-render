# NOTES: sleep
- Style: **Hand-drawn** (awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles `styles/handdrawn.md` recipe: wobbly pencil/ink lines on off-white paper redrawn every few frames (line boil), ink black plus one accent colour, no gradients/3D/gloss, hand-lettered labels). Why: sleep is an intimate, quiet topic, and a sketchbook look with a single dreamy indigo accent fits it better than a UI-like style; wobbling line boil makes static diagrams (the night's sleep graph, brain, heart) feel alive.
