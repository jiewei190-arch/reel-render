# NOTES: barcode
- Style: **Kinetic typography** (awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles `styles/typography.md` recipe: bold type, huge words that snap on the beat, strict grid, 2-3 colours with one electric accent, hard cuts, no imagery). Same as the suggestion. Why: a barcode is already a typographic object (black/white stripes and digits), so giant snapping words plus real UPC-A geometry make the hook "it holds a number, not a price" land fast, and hard cuts fit a step-by-step "laser, signal, digit, check digit" explanation.
- Palette: ink #0c0c0c, paper #f3efe4, electric red #ff2d1f. Face: Anton (OFL) for slams, JetBrains Mono for captions/maths.
- Bars drawn are a real, valid UPC-A (036000291452) built from the standard L/R patterns; the check-digit maths on screen is the Wikipedia example.
- Voice af_heart 0.97, music crayon 88 bpm. Closing line is the exact required phrase, spoken and on the end card.
