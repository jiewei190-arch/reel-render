# NOTES: orbit
- Style: **Cinematic 3D** (awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles `styles/cinematic3d.md` recipe: Three.js PBR materials, one strong warm key light with soft shadows, slow dolly/orbit camera moves, film grain, colour grade). Same as the suggestion. Why: orbit is a spatial idea (curvature, sideways speed, a ball that keeps falling around a planet), so a real 3D planet, cannon, station and astronaut let the camera show it from the right angle, and the slow pull-back to the full globe sells "it never lands".
- Original code (t_orbit.js): procedural Earth texture + clouds + Fresnel atmosphere, Newton's-cannon trails from an actual inverse-square integration, PBR rocket/ISS/astronaut, soft shadow key light, grain overlay + soft-light grade + vignette, dips to black at scene cuts. No depth of field (not feasible in the software renderer).
