A hurricane is a giant heat engine, and its fuel is warm ocean water. The sea surface needs to be at least about 27 °C (80 °F). Warm, moist air rises, cools and condenses into storm clouds, releasing the heat that powers the storm. Earth's rotation turns the inflow into a spin: counterclockwise north of the equator, clockwise south of it. When sustained winds reach 74 mph it is officially a hurricane, with a calm eye inside an eyewall of the strongest winds. Which part surprised you most?
