# microwavelight: opening lines (pick marked ★)

1. ★ "You can see through this door, but microwaves can't get out." (11 words, main-session edit). Frame 0: the sketchbook page, the painted kid peeking at the glowing microwave with popcorn already jumping, and the handwritten "WHY THE MESH?". By the but-turn the camera pushes in on the door and a loupe shows the mesh holes large ("Look closer: tiny holes.").
2. "Your microwave door is a sieve, and light is the only thing small enough to get through." (17 words). Gives the answer away and runs long.
3. "That little dotted screen on your microwave door is doing serious physics." (12 words). Fine, but it has no tension.

Why ★1: it states the paradox (see-through but sealed) in the first sentence, matching the visual of the glowing window.
