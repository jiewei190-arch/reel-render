Why does a microwave have a metal mesh on the door? 🍿 The oven's waves are about 12 cm long, and the mesh holes are only a few millimetres across, much too small for them to squeeze through, so the metal bounces them back inside (the idea behind a Faraday cage). Visible light's waves are less than a thousandth of a millimetre, so they slip right through and you can watch your food. Bonus: the trapped waves make hot and cold spots about 6 cm apart, which is why the plate spins.

Sources: Swinburne University of Technology; University of Arizona Optical Sciences outreach; The Open University (OpenLearn).
