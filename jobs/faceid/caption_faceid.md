Your phone doesn't look at your face. It looks at its shape. Face ID projects thousands of invisible infrared dots, reads where they land to build a depth map, and turns that into numbers that stay on your device. A printed photo has no depth, so it can't fool it.

Sources: Apple Support (About Face ID advanced technology); Apple Platform Security guide (Face ID and Touch ID security).
