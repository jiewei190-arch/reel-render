Why do eggs turn solid when you cook them? 🍳 Egg white is almost 90% water with folded-up proteins floating in it. Heat makes the proteins unfold (denature), then the loose chains grab each other and tangle into a mesh that traps the water, so clear and runny turn white and solid. The white starts to set around 63 °C and the yolk around 70 °C. Can you uncook one? In 2015, chemists refolded one egg-white protein with a vortex machine. Not the whole egg, but it's a step toward cheaper proteins for medicine.

Sources: Exploratorium (Science of Cooking); Harold McGee, On Food and Cooking; UC Irvine News (2015); USDA FoodData Central.
