Nobody has ever predicted a major earthquake. Not the USGS, not any scientist. A real prediction needs the when, the where and the how big, and nobody can nail all three. In 1985 scientists forecast a quake at Parkfield, California before 1993. It arrived in 2004.

What we can do is warn you seconds before the strong shaking arrives.

Sources: USGS (Can you predict earthquakes?; Parkfield experiment; foreshock probability; ShakeAlert fact sheet), NPR (7 Feb 2023), NASA Earth Observatory, Caltech.
