Why do we get hiccups? 😮 Your diaphragm (the muscle under your lungs) suddenly jerks, and about a thirtieth of a second later your vocal cords snap shut: "hic!". It's a reflex loop through the vagus and phrenic nerves. What hiccups are for is still a mystery: one idea says they help babies burp out swallowed air; scientists also suspect they're a leftover from gill-breathing ancestors, like tadpoles. The cures? There's no proof they work for everyone. Hiccups lasting more than 2 days? See a doctor.

Sources: Howes, BioEssays (2012); Straus et al., BioEssays (2003); NHS.
