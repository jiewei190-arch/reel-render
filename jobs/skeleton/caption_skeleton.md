Your skeleton isn't spooky, it's alive and busy right now. You were born with around 300 bones that fuse into 206. They hold you up, guard your brain, heart and lungs, and let your muscles move you. Inside some bones, soft marrow is a factory for red cells, white cells and platelets. Your bones are a calcium bank, and their cells keep tearing down old bone and building new: about 10% of an adult skeleton every year. 💀🎃

Sources: Cleveland Clinic "Skeletal System"; MedlinePlus "Bone Marrow Diseases"; Healthline "How Many Bones Does a Baby Have?"; Wikipedia "Bone" and "Bone remodeling".
