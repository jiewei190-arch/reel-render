# darknight: opening lines (pick marked ★)

1. ★ "Why is the night sky dark if there are so many stars?" (12 words: the exact search question). Frame 0: a shadow-theatre night: a dark hide sheet pricked with star holes (the lamp shines through them), a jointed watcher puppet on a hill looking up, cut-letter plaque "WHY IS THE NIGHT SKY DARK?". The but-turn at 3.5 s ("But if space went on forever...") fills the sheet with more and more stars, and sight lines from the watcher's eye each end on a star.
2. "The dark night sky proves the universe had a beginning." (10 words). Overstates it ("proves"), and it gives the answer away.
3. "In an endless universe, the night sky would be blinding." (10 words). A good re-hook, kept as phrase 2 instead.

Why ★1: it's the question people type, and it carries the paradox in the hook itself.
Re-hooks: 15.4 s the dust guess; 21.8 s "Here's the real answer: the universe has an age."; 38.2 s "And there's a second reason: space is expanding."; 49.2 s the Big Bang glow in microwaves.
Payoff 55.8 s: "So the night sky is dark because the universe had a beginning: most starlight hasn't arrived yet, and expanding space stretches the rest." Question: "Did you know the dark sky is a clue the universe had a beginning? Yes or no?"
