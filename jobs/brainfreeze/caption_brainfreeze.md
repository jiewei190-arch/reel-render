Why do you get brain freeze? 🍦 Your brain isn't freezing. Brain tissue has no pain sensors. The cold hits the roof of your mouth, and the leading idea is that the blood vessels there tighten, then swing wide open. In one lab study, an artery at the front of the brain widened fast as the pain hit. The trigeminal nerve carries the signal, and your brain blames your forehead: referred pain. Fix: press your tongue to the roof of your mouth.

Sources: Serrador et al., Experimental Biology 2012 (abstract); Kaczorowski & Kaczorowski, BMJ (2002); Cleveland Clinic; Houston Methodist.
