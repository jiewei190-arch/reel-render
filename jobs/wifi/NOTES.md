# NOTES: wifi

**Style: Generative & neon** (awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles, `styles/generative.md`: procedural particles/fields, dark ground, bold 3-4 colour neon palette, motion locked to the beat).

**Why:** the topic is an invisible wave. A procedural dot field whose dots are displaced and coloured by the wave (cyan = crest, magenta = trough) makes the wave visible, so the "wall barely dims it" and "metal reflects it" payoffs are literally visible. 88 bpm pulse on the end beat.

Look: ground #05060f, cyan #19f0ff, magenta #ff2fd0, lime #b6ff3d, amber wall #ffb347. Font Inter. Voice af_heart 0.97, music crayon bpm 88 (leaf settings). Hand-written 2D canvas scene (t_wifi.js), rendered with the repo engine.
