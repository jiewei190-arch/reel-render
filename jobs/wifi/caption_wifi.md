Your WiFi goes through walls. How? It isn't pushing anything solid. It's radio waves, about 12 cm from peak to peak, and a wall is barely in their way. Each wall just takes a small share, and metal, stone and water take the most. The 5 GHz band is faster but reaches less far.

Sources: Wikipedia "Wi-Fi" and "Microwave oven"; basic physics (c = f x wavelength).
