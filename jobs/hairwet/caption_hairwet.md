Why does wet hair look darker? Water doesn't dye it, it traps the light. Dry hair is rough, so light scatters every way and plenty bounces back to your eyes. Add water and a thin film coats every strand: light scattered off the hair hits the water's surface at a steep angle and bounces back inside, getting more chances to be absorbed. Water also lets more light slip into the strand. Less light comes back, so the same hair looks darker. On wet sand, measured reflection dropped to about half of dry sand. And the shine? The smooth film reflects like a tiny mirror. 💧

Sources: Lekner & Dorf, "Why some things are darker when wet," Applied Optics (1988); NC State University ECE (Prof. Michael Kudenov).
