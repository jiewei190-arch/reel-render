Why does hair turn gray? Your hair doesn't turn gray; it grows in gray. Color comes from melanin, made by pigment cells (melanocytes) at the root, and each strand's color is set as it grows. Those pigment cells are refilled by stem cells in the follicle. A 2023 study in mice found that these stem cells normally shuttle between two spots, and with age more of them get stuck where they can't make pigment. Hydrogen peroxide building up in aging follicles is another clue. In mice, stress made nerves release noradrenaline, which used up the pigment stem cells within days. Whether it works the same way in people is still being studied. Your genes set the timing.
