Why does aluminum foil have a shiny side and a dull side? ✨ Blame the factory. Foil is rolled thinner and thinner between polished steel rollers, and on the last pass two sheets go through together so the thin foil doesn't tear. The outsides touch the polished rollers and come out shiny; the insides only touch each other and come out matte. Does the side matter for cooking? Reynolds and Germany's aluminium industry association both say no. The exception is non-stick foil: its coating is on the dull side.

Sources: GDA (German aluminium industry association) foil factsheet; Reynolds Kitchens.
