How do hand warmers get hot with no battery? The recipe is on the maker's own safety sheet: more than half iron powder, plus water, a pinch of salt, activated charcoal and vermiculite. Tear the pack open, air gets in through the cloth, and the iron starts to rust. Rusting always gives off heat; fine powder and salt just make it fast enough to feel, for hours. The clicky reusable kind is different: a liquid holding more dissolved salt than it normally could, which crystallises and releases stored heat when you click it. 🔥🧤

Sources: Kobayashi Healthcare safety data sheet (air-activated heating packs); University of Silesia (Prof. Robert Musioł); Minnesota State University, Mankato (Dr. Marie Pomije, via KTTC); BBC Science Focus.
