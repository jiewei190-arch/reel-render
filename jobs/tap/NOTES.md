# NOTES: tap

**Style: Retro / terminal** (awesome-opus-5-5-video-styles, `styles/retro.md`: CRT terminal, amber phosphor, scanlines, ASCII/line art, type-on text with a blinking cursor, no modern gradients).

**Why:** the story is a short machine conversation (power up, hello, check, one-time code). A terminal log with typed-on lines and inverse-video highlights fits it naturally, and a single phosphor colour keeps it graphic and calm.

Look: ground #0b0802, amber #ffb000 with dim/hot tones, VT323 font, scanlines, vignette, slow bloom band, slight flicker. Voice af_heart 0.97, music crayon bpm 88 (leaf settings).
