A contactless card has no battery. So how does it pay when you tap? The terminal's coil makes a magnetic field that flips about 13.5 million times a second, and it powers the card's chip through a tiny wireless "transformer". Every payment uses a one-time code, so a recorded signal can't be replayed.

Sources: Wikipedia "Near-field communication" and "Contactless payment"; Apple Pay security overview; EMVCo.
