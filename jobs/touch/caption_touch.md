Your phone screen doesn't feel your touch. It senses your electricity. Under the glass is an invisible grid of wires. Every crossing holds a little charge, and your finger disturbs it. The phone finds the dip and works out where you are. That's also why many gloves fail.

Sources: Wikipedia "Touchscreen"; US patent literature on mutual-capacitance touch sensing.
