Why can you get sunburned on a cloudy day? ☁️ Because what burns you is light you can't see. Sunburn comes from ultraviolet (UV) light: UVB is the main one that burns the top layer of your skin, and UVA goes deeper and gets through clouds and even windows. Thicker clouds block more UV, but not all of it, and you can't feel UV, so a cool, grey day can still have strong UV. That's why health agencies say to check the UV index, not the temperature: at 3 or higher, protect your skin. UV also bounces off water, sand and concrete, and fresh snow can reflect up to 80% of it. Clouds hide the sun, not the UV.

Sources: CDC Sun Safety; US EPA UV Index Scale; Skin Cancer Foundation; WHO; NOAA Climate Prediction Center.
