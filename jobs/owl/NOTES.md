# owl: How owls fly almost silently

- Template: #4 crayon-book (lemo-opuscar/styles/crayon-book), using the in-house re-implementation reels/styles/crayon (approved for flamingo). Duration 66.5 s, af_heart 0.97, crayon 88 bpm, new mix2.py.
- Why: a Halloween night picture-book page (moon, stars, owl, mouse) fits the template's own demo poster ("The Moon Can't Sleep"), and colouring as the action lets each wing trick be drawn on in turn.

## Copied from the template (STYLE.md, demo source, poster)
- Wax crayon on toothy warm paper: wax only sticks to the tooth peaks, pressure sets coverage, zig-zag hatching that overshoots outlines, boiling lines stepped at 12 fps.
- One watercolour per film: an ultramarine night wash over indigo crayon sky bands; white-wax stars resist the wash (the demo poster's night sky).
- A moon with a sleepy face, numbers written in the sky (child logic, like the poster's "1 2 3 4 5"), hand-lettered Gaegu titles, the book's printed words in Patrick Hand on a ragged paper knock-out patch at the bottom (never a dark box).
- Limited crayon box with an indigo-plum outline (never black); characters as a skilled adult imitating a child (big round eyes with a white catchlight, mitten-simple shapes).
- Substitution animation: the owl's two key wing drawings alternate on the beat; colouring is the action (the comb, the fringe and the velvet are drawn in as each trick is named).
