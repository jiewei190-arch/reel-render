An owl can swoop right past you and you won't hear it. 🦉 Its wings have three tricks: a comb-like front edge, a soft fringed back edge that breaks up the turbulence, and a velvety surface that soaks up sound. Together they cut the noise above about 2,000 Hz, below what its prey can hear. What would you make quieter with owl feathers?

Sources: Wikipedia "Owl"; University of Cambridge, "The owl and the wind turbine".
