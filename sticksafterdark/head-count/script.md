# Sticks After Dark #3: "Head Count" (9:16, ~65 s, Instagram cut)

Original story. Narrator: Kokoro am_fenrir, slow (0.88), low, close, deadpan. Captions ON (1-3 words, punch word alone, digits for numbers).
Look: Sticks After Dark kit. Night woods + campfire + tents (set built inside this project: scenes/shots/_camp.js).
ONE accent: campfire orange (#ff9a3c). '##' lines are long silent beats; '## 2.4' is the held frame before the twist. Instagram cut: 60-70 s.

## NARRATION
Five of us went camping. Way out in the woods. No signal.
I'm the one who worries, so I made a rule.
Every night by the fire, I counted heads.
One. Two. Three. Four. Five.
Night one, five.
Night two, five. Everyone made fun of me.
##
Night three, the fog rolled in.
The fire got low. Someone told a ghost story.
I counted, like always.
One. Two. Three. Four. Five.
##
Six.
##
Everyone laughed. They said I counted someone twice.
So I counted again, slowly. Five.
##
Good.
##
I felt stupid. I went back to my spot.
Later, lying in my tent, I went over it again.
Five of us. Four friends, and me.
## 2.4
And I never count myself.
## 1.6
Follow for more.
