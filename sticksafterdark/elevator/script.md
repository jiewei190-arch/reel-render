## NARRATION
I work late. Most nights, I'm the last one out.
Last night, I got in the elevator on the ninth floor and pressed lobby.
On eight, it stopped. The doors opened.
Nobody there. Just a dark hallway.
The doors closed.
On seven, it stopped again. Nobody.
Six. Five. Four.
Every floor, the doors opened. Every floor, nobody got on.
I figured it was broken.
I stared at my phone and tried not to look into the hallways.
The light flickered.
The elevator got slower. Heavier.
The cable groaned, like it was carrying more than just me.
##
Lobby. The doors opened, and I stepped out.
##
Behind me, the doors started to close.
I glanced back at the mirror on the elevator wall.
