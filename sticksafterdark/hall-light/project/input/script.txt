My little brother is scared of the dark.
So every night, we leave the hall light on.
It's an old yellow bulb. It hums. You get used to it.
Last month it burned out, and he screamed until Dad changed it.
So we never let it go out.
Tonight, at midnight, it clicked off.
No flicker. No pop. Just, click.
The hum stopped. The whole house went quiet.
I waited for my brother to scream.
He didn't.
I got up to turn it back on.
My hand was on the doorknob when, from his room, he whispered:
Don't. It turned it off so you'd come out.
I let go of the doorknob.
I got back in bed. I pulled the blanket over my head.
And I lay there, listening to the quiet, until morning.
Then I remembered.
