# Sticks After Dark #5: "Voicemail" (9:16, 60-70 s cut)

Original story. Narrator: Kokoro am_fenrir, slow (0.88), low, close, deadpan (kit tools/voice.py).
The [WHISPER] line is the NARRATOR'S OWN VOICE (am_fenrir) turned into a breathy whisper and played back
through a small phone speaker (project-local `tools/voice_vm.py`; the kit is untouched).
Look: Sticks After Dark kit. Bedroom at night, ONE accent: cold blue phone/moon light (#8fb2ff).
'##' lines are long silent beats; '## N' = a beat of exactly N s ('## 3.2' is the held frame before the twist).
Numbers are SPOKEN in words; captions show digits ("3:12 a.m.", "3:11") via story/captions.json.

## NARRATION
##
I always sleep with my phone on silent.
So it was the light that woke me. One new voicemail.
From my own number.
Three twelve a.m.
I thought it was a glitch. Spam. I almost deleted it.
I pressed play.
## 2.6
At first, just breathing. Slow. Close to the phone.
Like someone lying very still, trying not to make a sound.
Then my own voice, whispering.
[WHISPER] Don't get out of bed. Don't look under it. It's under there.
## 3.0
I didn't move.
I played it again, to be sure. It was me. It was definitely me.
But I never said that.
## 2.5
I checked the time.
## 3.2
It was three eleven.
## 2.6
Follow for more.
