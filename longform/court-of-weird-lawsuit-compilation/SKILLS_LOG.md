`docs/voice_court_of_weird.md` (branch `claude/skills-shorts-queue`), copied to `~/.claude/youtube/voice.md` for this

| Skill | Used? | What it concretely changed |
|---|---|---|
| yt-script (`hookscore.py`) | used | Six cold-open candidates scored 34-38, all WEAK (the tool rewards second-person address, which a narrated true story does not use; same result as on the Shorts). Top two: "What does a court give a man who asks fifty-four million dollars for one pair of pants?" (38, The Question) and the gold rooster line (35). Changes made: the question is kept as the planted open question (line 5 of the cold open, repeated before case 1); the tool's SPECIFICITY/STAKES note moved "fifty-four million dollars" up from line 3 to line 2. Each case's first line was also scored (33-38): the lowest, "Number one. The pants." (28), was rewritten to "Number one. The fifty-four million dollar pants." (33). Adding a "because..." stakes clause to the tomato and burrito lines LOWERED them (36 to 27, 34 to 26, "cut the half of the sentence that answers itself"), so those stayed short. Every beat has an on-screen note (VISUALS.md), per the skill's "a talking head with nothing to look at is a podcast". Runtime by the skill's 150 wpm figure would be 13.7 min; the repo's own 2.45 words per second gives 14.0. |
| youtube-script | used | The setup, tension, payoff shape per segment set the length of each case (46-80 s, payoff in the last two lines) and its rule "end each payoff with the next segment's setup" produced the re-hook line that closes every case. Its minute map (a beat at 3:00, 6:00, then every 3-5 min) decided the order: the monkey at 3:12, the haunted house at 5:47, Satan at 7:36, the rooster at 9:27, the tonal turn ("in these, people got hurt") at 10:24. No banned words; no greeting; the title's payoff is stated inside the first 30 seconds. |
| yt-package (`title.py`) | used | Twelve titles: all scored 94 alone because every one ran past the 40-character mobile cut. That finding produced the pick: the working title shortened to exactly 40 characters, "14 Real Lawsuits That Sounded Like Jokes", which scores 100 with the thumbnail text "$54M FOR PANTS". The owner's working title with the true count scores 98 and is the backup. A rooster title with "ROOSTER v. USA" scored 80 (thumbnail repeats the title) and was dropped. Thumbnail text held to three words. |
| youtube-packaging (+ VISUAL-CHECKS.md) | used | Five title + thumbnail pairs with image prompts (PACKAGING.md), a named backup, and the truth gate, which removed: "15" (the video has 14 cases), "won millions" for the coffee case, and "the X-Men are not human" as a flat claim. Thumbnails are object-led (pants, rooster, house), no faces of real people. |
| youtube-virality-gate | used | 1 Title trigger PASS (gap: jokes that got real verdicts). 2 Thumbnail adds PASS ($54M pants is not in the title). 3 First 5 seconds PASS (judge crying over pants at 0:00, the sum by about 0:06, no greeting). 4 Promise kept PASS (14 cases, each with its verdict; count matches the title). 5 Retention shape PASS (a verdict every 46-80 s; stakes rise to injuries in the last three). 6 Only you PASS (every case real, with citation and a primary-source table; the format's reference channel uses hypotheticals). 7 Format proven PASS (docs/FORMAT_RESEARCH_2026-11-23.md, format 3). 8 Creator wants it: NOT SCORED, the owner's call. Its fix that was applied: gate 4 forced the title number down from 15 to 14 when a case was cut. |
