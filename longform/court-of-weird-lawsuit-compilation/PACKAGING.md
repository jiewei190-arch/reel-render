## Description (paste-ready; no tags, no hashtags)

A judge asked for $54 million over one pair of pants. A man sued Satan. The United States put a solid gold rooster on trial. Fourteen real lawsuits that sounded like jokes, and what each court actually decided.

Fair to both sides: the hot coffee burns were severe and the award was cut by the judge; the forfeiture ruling sent the case back, and we could not confirm what finally happened to the cash; the haunted house ruling let the buyer seek his deposit back, it did not find that ghosts exist.
