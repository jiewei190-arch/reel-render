# VISUAL NOTES: GTA 6 Sold $3.79 Billion In A Day. Nobody Knows Its Cost

For the `doc` kit, theme `biz` (3D shots from `biz3d`). One row per beat: the words that start it, what the viewer sees,
and the scene type. Every number on screen is a number the narration says at that moment.

**Hard rules for this video (stricter than usual, because the subject is a live, heavily protected brand)**
- **No game footage, no screenshots, no trailers, no box art, no logos, no fonts or colours that imitate the series,
  no characters or their likenesses, no map of the game world.** Nothing from the 2022 leak, in any form.
- The game is shown only as a plain hero `box` prop labelled "GAME 6" (and "GAME 5" for its predecessor) in neutral
  colours, plus the title as plain text. Consoles are grey boxes labelled "CONSOLE".
- People: only the kit's generic executives. One name card is allowed: "Strauss Zelnick · CEO, Take-Two". The hacker,
  the analysts and the Rockstar executive are never named or drawn as likenesses (generic silhouettes only).
- No real stock-ticker artwork or exchange logos. Charts are the kit's own.
- Every `quote` and `document` scene uses wording from `sources.md`; re-read the page before the render.
- Keep the on-screen footnote "Checked Nov 24, 2026" on every scene that shows a launch number.
