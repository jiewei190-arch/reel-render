Series: THE RULES (rules horror). Same look and format as #1/#2. CAPTIONS ON (owner rule 23).
Setting: an ordinary family house during a quiet end-of-the-world night. Every phone on the street gets the same emergency alert.
Outside there is a white, warm light that looks like sunrise but is not (never shown directly: only light under the door, through
gaps in the cardboard, on faces). Narrator = Fenrir. [MOM] = a warm mother's voice through the front door: slightly too slow, too calm,

## OPEN

## RULE 1
2. Rule one. Stay inside. Cover every window.

## RULE 2
3. Rule two. If it looks like morning outside, it isn't. Don't look.

## RULE 3
4. Rule three. If someone knocks, count your family before you answer.

## THE NIGHT

7. Then a knock.
8. [MOM] Sweetheart? It's Mom. The sun's up. Come and see.
9. Mom is asleep upstairs. You're almost sure.

## COUNT

11. Four.
12. [VOICE] Five.
