## 3. Patterns (provisional)
- **Topics that win:** physics/"what would happen" (falling through Earth, Jupiter) and animals/nature (sharks, mosquitoes, gills, top 28 d at 2.7K). Absurd real laws/history are a solid #2 (beards, guinea pigs, Coca-Cola).
- **Topics that lose:** finance/opinion on this channel (tipping 49; Nvidia 1,274, below median). Finance belongs on Block Money.
- **Titles:**
  - Specific factual claims with a proper noun ("Russia Once Charged You for Having a Beard", "Switzerland Made Owning One Guinea Pig Illegal") sit at or above the median.
  - Vague teasers ("Can you pick up this spoon?", "You probably didn't see this coming…") sit at the bottom.
  - The newer title style (Title Case, specific claim) is the better one. Keep it.
- **Hooks, first frames, length, loop rewatch:** **not enough data yet.** This needs % viewed and stayed-vs-swiped per Short.
