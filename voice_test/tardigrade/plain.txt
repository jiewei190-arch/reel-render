In 2007, scientists strapped tiny animals to the OUTSIDE of a satellite.
No suits. No oxygen. Just open space for ten straight days.
The radiation out there is a thousand times stronger than on Earth.
When the satellite came home, they checked the bodies... and the animals were alive.
Some even laid eggs.
They're called tardigrades. Half a millimeter long, lives in moss, looks like a gummy bear with eight legs.
And now it's the first animal in history to survive outer space... naked.
Which means the toughest astronaut ever... is this guy.
