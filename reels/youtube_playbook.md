# YouTube Shorts playbook for howitreallyworkstudio (researched Oct 26, 2026)

## How Shorts distribution works (vidIQ 2026 guide; Buffer summary of YouTube's Shorts team)
- Explore/exploit: each Short is shown to a small seed audience first; if they engage it goes wider.
- Main signal: Viewed-vs-Swiped-Away (Analytics > Content > Shorts), then average view duration/retention, engagement (likes, comments, shares) and viewer surveys.
- Since Mar 31 2025 a play or replay counts as a view with no minimum; "Engaged views" are what count toward monetization.
- First 1-3 seconds decide it. Loops add views. Quality > frequency; posting time matters little (check Audience tab).
- A 35B-view study cited by vidIQ found 13 s and 60 s Shorts do best. Buffer's own data: 50-60 s does well. Ours are 66-87 s: test shorter cuts.
- Keep the intended viewer consistent so YouTube can learn who likes the channel. Channels with 200+ Shorts see steady growth.
## Titles, hashtags, thumbnails
- Title max 100 chars, ~first 50 show in feed: put the hook/keyword first. Be accurate; no keyword stuffing.
- Description first ~100 chars visible. First 3 hashtags show above the title. Use 3-5 relevant hashtags at the bottom; #Shorts optional. >60 hashtags = all ignored.
- Custom thumbnails for Shorts: upload on DESKTOP Studio (Content > pencil > Thumbnail), 1080x1920 (9:16). Not available in the mobile app.
## Growth tactics (practitioner opinion, not data)
- Related-video link, pinned comment with question/CTA, spoken+visual CTA in last 2-4 s, open-loop endings, consistent topic/vibe, post long-form too.
## Monetization (YouTube Help)
- 500 subs + 3 public uploads/90d + (3M Shorts views/90d or 3,000 watch hrs/12m): fan funding. 1,000 subs + (10M Shorts views/90d or 4,000 hrs/12m): ad revenue (Shorts pool, creator gets 45%).
- Typical Shorts RPM ~$0.01-0.35 per 1,000 views. Beware "inauthentic/mass-produced" policy: keep each reel original.
## Our plan
- Audience: curious beginners 13-35 into science/tech/nature. Keep every title a curiosity gap that is still accurate.
- Same reel on IG + YT (clean file). Test titles/thumbnails; check VVSA and AVD each Sunday and cut the weakest openings.
