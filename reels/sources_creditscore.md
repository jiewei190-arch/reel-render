## Dropped or unverified

- Credit inquiries count for 12 months but stay on the report for two years (FICO page). Read, but cut for length.
- The "report card" analogy is an analogy, not a claim.
- The fetched pages give no formula for turning factors into points, so the script says only that the model "weighs" the factors.
- The weights are FICO's. The script names "the FICO model" for them; other scoring models may weigh factors differently (FTC and FICO both say so).
- Not in any fetched page and not used: income is not part of the score, FICO's market share, any specific utilization percentage.
