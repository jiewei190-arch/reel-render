## Dropped or unverified
- Charlotte lecture PDF (webpages.charlotte.edu) was blocked by robots rules; nothing read, not used.
- Temperature line is a garnet-inclusion thermometry mode (~1,200 C), not a direct diamond measurement; the script says "near" to match.
- Pressure of about six gigapascals is a mode for lithospheric diamonds, not a full range.
- The 20 to 30 mph eruption speed and the graphite point come from one article (Smithsonian Magazine); single-source.
- Depth sources disagree (Smithsonian "about 100 miles"; opentextbc 200 to 250 km; Nimis about 190 km). Script uses "more than one hundred miles," which fits the two higher figures.
- Britannica also says 1 to 3.3 billion years and depth more than 120 km; not used because the Smithsonian range is enough.
- Lab-grown (HPHT and CVD) methods: only retailer pages appeared in search; not fetched, not used.
- Not used: "stable only at depths greater than about 100 km" (EBSCO), optional detail.
