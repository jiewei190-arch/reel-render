# Cozy 2D kit + Episode 1: "Mango's 2AM Kitchen" (new Instagram account, owner: "this cute shit... more niche")

Goal: a NEW, ORIGINAL cozy-cute 2D series for a brand-new Instagram account. Format inspiration (NOT to copy): viral wholesome "midnight cravings" reels from another creator. We copy only the FORMAT/pacing below, never their characters, room designs, art, logo, music or captions. Our character, world and palette must look clearly different.

## Reference format (what makes it work, described by MAIN)
- No voiceover. One caption pinned at the top the whole time ("When the midnight cravings hit..."), cozy lo-fi music + ASMR cooking sfx (sizzle, bubbles, crack, pour, slurp, can open).
- ~55 s: 0-5 s dark blue dorm at night, everyone asleep, the hero wakes up hungry. 5-35 s close-up secret cooking on a tiny hot plate: ingredients go in one by one, top-down pot "money shots" (bubbling broth, melting cheese slice, egg), each ingredient = a satisfying beat. 35-48 s eating: slurp, sip a drink, happy blush, spicy red face. 48-55 s twist: roommates smell it and peek over; final wide shot of the dorm glowing warm.
- Soft hand-drawn pastel look: wobbly brown outlines, flat warm fills, soft grain, warm lamp light vs. cool blue night.
