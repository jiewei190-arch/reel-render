## Description
In 410, for the first time in about 800 years, an enemy army was inside the city of Rome. How did the greatest empire on Earth end up there? Follow one Roman family's silver coin through three hundred years: one very nervous emperor after another, money that melted into copper, an empire too big to defend, the Huns, the disaster at Adrianople, and the Gothic commander Rome hired and then refused: Alaric. Plus the emperor and his chicken, and the last emperor of the West, who just... retired. And then the twist most people never hear: half of the Roman Empire didn't fall in 476 at all.
