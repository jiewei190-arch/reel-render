**Title (49 chars):** What If You Shrank to the Size of an Ant? 🐜

**Description:**
What if you shrank to the size of an ant? You could fall off a skyscraper and walk away... but one raindrop could trap you. 🐜💧

Tiny bodies barely fall: weight shrinks with volume, air resistance only with surface area, so an ant drifts down at a few km/h while a full-size human hits around 200 km/h. But at that size, surface tension is as strong as gravity is for us.
