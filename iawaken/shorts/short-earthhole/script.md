# What If You Jumped Into a Hole Through the Earth?  (I.Awaken Short, W1, slug `short-earthhole`)

9:16 vertical, 20-30 s, Kokoro am_fenrir (speed 0.95), narrator only, captions on, classic I.Awaken look (style "dusk").
HERO = "you" in the mustard hoodie. Structure: HOOK -> BUILD-UP -> PAYOFF -> closer, hard cut.
Thought experiment: a hole pole to pole (no spin sideways push), with the air pumped out, walls that never melt.

## Narration (exact text sent to the TTS; ~70 words)

**HOOK (0-3 s)**
What if you jumped into a hole straight through the Earth?

**BUILD-UP (~3-17 s)**
You fall. Faster every second.
But then the rock starts to glow.
The heat and pressure keep rising.
Near the core, it's about as hot as the surface of the Sun.
And it gets weirder.

**PAYOFF (~17-28 s)**
At the center, you're weightless, racing at eight kilometers per second.
Then you slow down, and about thirty-eight minutes later, you pop out on the other side.
With no air to stop you, you fall back. Forever.
Would you jump?
