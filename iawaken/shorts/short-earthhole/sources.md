# Sources: What If You Jumped Into a Hole Through the Earth?

| Claim in the script | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Falling pole to pole through the Earth takes ~38 min (realistic density model PREM: 38 min 11 s; uniform Earth: 42 min 12 s) | Alexander R. Klotz, "The gravity tunnel in a non-uniform Earth", *American Journal of Physics* 83, 231 (2015). arXiv: https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.1342 ; news summary: https://phys.org/news/2015-03-fall-hole-earth.html ; https://www.livescience.com/50312-how-long-to-fall-through-earth.html | Script says "about thirty-eight minutes" (one-way trip, surface to opposite surface). |
| You speed up all the way to the center, then slow down symmetrically and arrive at the other side at rest; with no friction it oscillates forever | Gravity train / gravity tunnel: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gravity_train ; Klotz 2015 (above) | Simple harmonic-like motion; ideal case with no air and no friction. |
| Peak speed at the center about 8 km/s | Klotz 2015 (above), phys.org summary quotes speeds "over 8 kilometres per second" (uniform-Earth value is sqrt(gR) ≈ 7.9 km/s) | Script says "eight kilometers per second". |
| Weightless at the center | Shell theorem: at the centre the mass pulls equally in all directions, net gravity 0 (any physics text; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shell_theorem ). In free fall you would feel weightless the whole way; at the centre gravity itself is zero. | |
| Inner core about 5,400 °C, about as hot as the surface of the Sun | NASA-associated / STScI ViewSpace "Did You Know: Earth's Internal Energy": https://viewspace.org/video_library/videos/738 ; Smithsonian: https://www.smithsonianmag.com/smart-news/the-center-of-the-earth-is-as-hot-as-the-sun-43631207/ (Anzellini et al., *Science* 2013, ~6,000 °C at the inner-core boundary) | Estimates range ~5,000-6,000 °C, so "about as hot as" and "~5,400 °C". |
| Pressure at the center ~3.6 million times surface air pressure (~360 GPa) | Encyclopaedia Britannica, "Earth's core": https://www.britannica.com/science/core-Earth ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Earth%27s_inner_core | Chip only: "PRESSURE x3,600,000". |
| (Context, not narrated) the deepest hole ever drilled is ~12.2 km (Kola Superdeep Borehole) | https://www.britannica.com/place/Kola-Superdeep-Borehole | Optional chip only if it fits. |

Assumptions shown on screen: pole-to-pole tunnel (no Earth-rotation sideways push), air removed, walls that survive the heat.
