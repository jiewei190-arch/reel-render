# What If You Went to Space Without a Suit? (I.Awaken Short, slug space-nosuit)
Topic bank #6 (origin/iawaken/topic-bank, iawaken/topic-bank/TOPICS.md). Pillar: WHAT IF (science), myth-busting the movies.
Narrator: Kokoro am_fenrir, speed 0.95, deadpan storyteller (`whatif/voice/fenrir.py`, short gaps).
Spoken text = the numbered lines only. "## beat" = a beat of silence before the punchline.
Cast: HERO (mustard hoodie) is "you". Cameos: DEV (beanie + phone, waiting for the explosion), MINA (red scarf + stopwatch,
times the 10 seconds), DR LIN (lab coat, runs the 1966-style chamber test). The 1966 volunteer is never named.
No gore: no swelling, no bleeding. Danger reads as a comic gasp, fizzing cartoon bubbles, eyes closing, a rescue.

1. You have about ten seconds. Use them.
2. You just stepped into space. No suit.
## beat
3. But you don't explode. That's a movie thing.
4. You don't freeze solid either. Space is basically a giant thermos.
5. The spit on your tongue, though?
## beat
6. That boils.
7. In 1966, a NASA test volunteer felt it bubbling... right before he blacked out.
8. Because about ten seconds in, your brain runs out of oxygen.
9. Lights out.
10. That volunteer? They pumped the air back in, in under ninety seconds.
## beat
11. He woke up fine.
12. Subscribe for more.
