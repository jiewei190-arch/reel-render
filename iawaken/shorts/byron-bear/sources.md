Not said (unverified or disputed): where the bear lived. Neither page says it lodged in his rooms, and the topic bank
notes a Trinity scholar calls the bear lodging with him "highly improbable"; so the Short never shows or says the bear
living in his rooms (the bear is shown in the court, at the gate, and in Byron's comic fellowship daydream). Also not
said: the bear later going to Newstead Abbey ([W] states it without a source).
