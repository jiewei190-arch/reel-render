# Banned From Having a Dog, He Got a Bear (I.Awaken Short, slug byron-bear)
Narrator: Kokoro am_fenrir (0.95), deadpan storyteller. "## beat" lines = a beat of silence before the punchline.
Target ~30 s. Loop: the last shot is the "NO DOGS" gate again with the bear strolling in = frame 1.
1. His college banned dogs. So he got a bear.
2. According to legend, the rules said nothing about bears.
3. This is the poet Lord Byron, a student at Trinity College, Cambridge.
4. Dogs? Not allowed.
## beat
5. Bears? Apparently fine.
6. In 1807, he wrote to a friend: I have a new friend, the finest in the world.
## beat
7. A tame bear.
8. They asked him what he planned to do with it.
## beat
9. He said it should sit for a fellowship.
10. Cambridge is still telling the story two hundred years later.
11. Subscribe for more.
