His college banned dogs. So he got a bear.
According to legend, the rules said nothing about bears.
This is the poet Lord Byron, a student at Trinity College, Cambridge.
Dogs? Not allowed.
Bears? Apparently fine.
In 1807, he wrote to a friend: I have a new friend, the finest in the world.
A tame bear.
They asked him what he planned to do with it.
He said it should sit for a fellowship.
Cambridge is still telling the story two hundred years later.
