# What If You Jumped Into a Hole Through the Earth? (I.Awaken Short, Toon 3D remake, slug `short-earthhole-3d`)

Remake of `short-earthhole` (2D, branch `iawaken/short-earthhole`) in the approved Toon 3D style (kits/toon3d).
9:16, 1080x1920, 24 fps, 29.75 s. Same voiceover, word timings and shot timings as the 2D original (shots s001-s018),
plus the closer "Subscribe for more." (the Short answers its question, so playbook rule: "Subscribe for more.").
Thought experiment shown on screen: a pole-to-pole tunnel, air removed, walls that never melt.

## Narration (unchanged, Kokoro am_fenrir, speed 0.95)
**HOOK** What if you jumped into a hole straight through the Earth?
**BUILD-UP** You fall. Faster every second. But then the rock starts to glow. The heat and pressure keep rising.
Near the core, it's about as hot as the surface of the Sun. And it gets weirder.
**PAYOFF** At the center, you're weightless, racing at eight kilometers per second. Then you slow down, and about
thirty-eight minutes later, you pop out on the other side. With no air to stop you, you fall back. Forever. Would you jump?
**CLOSER (new)** Subscribe for more.  (voiced alone with `whatif/voice/fenrir.py --gap 0 --comma 0 --lead 0`, input
`voice/closer.md`, output `voice/closer_fenrir.wav`, 1.19 s; spliced into the voiceover at 28.10 s; the old hold after
"Would you jump?" (27.66-28.50) is trimmed to 28.05)
