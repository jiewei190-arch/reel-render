This man sold the Eiffel Tower. Twice.
He didn't own it.
Paris, nineteen twenty-five. Victor Lustig poses as a French government official.
He tells scrap dealers the city will tear the tower down. Seven thousand tons of metal. For sale.
One dealer, André Poisson, pays up.
Lustig vanishes.
According to the story, Poisson was too embarrassed to tell anyone.
So Lustig came back, and ran the exact same scam. Again.
This time, a dealer went to the police.
Lustig just... left the country. Again.
