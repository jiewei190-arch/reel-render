Australia declared war on birds.
And lost.
The army brought machine guns.
The emus brought... running.
It's 1932. About twenty thousand emus are eating the wheat in Western Australia.
So the soldiers mount a gun on a truck.
The emus are faster than the truck.
Thousands of rounds later, the commander reported nine hundred and eighty-six birds.
Out of twenty thousand.
The army went home. The emus stayed.
Final score: Emus, one. Army, zero.
