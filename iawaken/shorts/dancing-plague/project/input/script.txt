In fifteen eighteen, a whole city couldn't stop dancing.
The doctors' cure?
More dancing.
Strasbourg, July. A woman named Frau Troffea starts dancing in the street.
For days.
Then dozens join her. According to chronicles, maybe four hundred.
Doctors blame hot blood. So the city builds a stage...
and hires a band.
It made things worse. So they banned music.
Historians think it was stress: famine, sickness and fear, spreading person to person.
And it all started with one step.
