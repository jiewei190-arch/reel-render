## Channel: Law, weird true court cases
- Real cases only: lawsuits, trials and verdicts with court records or solid news coverage. Name the case (e.g.
  "Pearson v. Chung"), the year and the place.
- The arc: the absurd claim, who these people were, how it escalated, the courtroom twist, the verdict, and what
  happened after (appeals, fallout, the law it changed).
- **Never legal advice.** Never tell viewers what they can sue for or how. Explain what the court decided and why, as
  part of the story.
- Be fair to both sides; say "alleged" for anything not proven in court. Private people who became public only
  through the case: use the names in the records, without mockery.
- Great topics: absurd damages, animals or objects on trial, laws used in bizarre ways, tiny claims that became huge,
  courtroom moments that went viral, cases that changed everyday rules.
