## Channel: Scams & Hacks, true cyber heists
- Real scams, hacks and breaches with public sources (DOJ/FTC/FBI press releases, court records, major news, security
  research reports). Name the case, the year and the amount.
- The arc: the target and what was at stake, the trick, how it unfolded, the moment it was discovered, how they got
  caught (or didn't), and **"how to spot it"**: 2-3 simple warning signs viewers can use.
- **Story level only. Never operational detail**: no step-by-step methods, tools, code, or anything that would help
  someone repeat the crime. Explain the idea ("they faked invoices from a real supplier"), not the how-to.
- Victims are never the joke. Say "allegedly" for anything not proven in court.
- Great topics: business-email scams, romance and crypto scams, SIM swaps, famous data breaches, ransomware that shut
  down a city or hospital, social-engineering heists, scams aimed at regular people right now.
