## Channel: Justin clips, weird true history
- Real history only: events, people and facts with solid sources (encyclopedias, museums, academic or major-news
  sources). Give the year and place. If historians disagree or a popular version is a myth, say so; busting the myth
  can be the story.
- The arc: the strangest fact first, then how it happened, the twist nobody expects, and why it still matters or what
  became of it. Shorts: one story, one twist. Long videos: one big story with chapters, or a countdown of connected
  stories under one theme.
- Respect real tragedies: deaths, wars and disasters get a serious tone; no jokes at victims' expense.
- Great topics: bizarre events, odd inventions, strange laws of the past, unbelievable coincidences, people who did
  something nobody would believe today, myths everyone believes that are wrong.
