## Channel: Business, costly mistakes and collapses
- Real companies and real decisions, with dates and dollar figures from reliable sources (filings, major business
  press, books by named authors). Company names may be spoken; on screen they appear only as plain text, never as logos.
- The arc: the company at its peak, THE decision (or the opportunity they passed on), why it seemed smart at the time,
  what happened next, the collapse or the missed fortune in today's dollars, and the one lesson anyone can use.
- **No investment advice:** never tell viewers to buy, sell or avoid a stock or company.
- Be fair: people in these stories made reasonable-looking calls with what they knew; show why, then show the cost.
- Great topics: deals turned down, products killed too early or too late, one bad acquisition, expansion that sank a
  chain, a founder's single choice, "worth X today".
