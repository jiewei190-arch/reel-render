# Sources (checked Oct 26, 2026)
- Max Planck Society press release (MPE), Aug 12, 2026: https://www.mpg.de/26904239/three-black-holes-discovered-in-a-young-galaxy
- Paper: Übler et al., "BlackTHUNDER: Evidence of three massive black holes in a z~5 galaxy", Astronomy & Astrophysics 2026, DOI 10.1051/0004-6361/202557419
- Coverage: Universe Today, EarthSky, Science Times (Aug 2026)
Facts used: galaxy J0148-4214, z=5.02, >12.5 billion ly, ~1.2 Gyr after Big Bang, JWST NIRSpec-IFS; two central BHs ~620 ly apart (projected), third ~5,500 ly from centre; masses ~80M, ~0.6M, ~2M solar; smallest accreting above Eddington; central pair expected to merge within a few hundred million years; third may be recoil remnant or migrating inward; "first evidence" of three active BHs in one distant galaxy.
Notes: announcement is from Aug 12 (older than the xlsx "this week" framing). Visuals are artist's concept, schematic, not scale.
