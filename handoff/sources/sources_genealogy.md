# Sources: How investigative genetic genealogy solved the Golden State Killer case (2018)

Each claim is one plain sentence under 25 words, with its source URL(s) and a confidence tag.
"Verified by 2 sources" means two independent outlets or agencies report the same fact.
"Single source" means only one source was found, so use it with care.

## Claims

1. Between 1974 and 1986, a California attacker was linked to at least 12 murders and more than 50 rapes.
   - Sources: https://www.axios.com/facts-on-the-golden-state-killer-a665ff93-d682-464c-9c1c-b18260a6aa42.html ; https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/golden-state-killer-suspect-joseph-deangelo-charged-with-four-more-decades-old-murders
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources

2. Investigators collected DNA from several of the murder scenes.
   - Sources: https://www.axios.com/facts-on-the-golden-state-killer-a665ff93-d682-464c-9c1c-b18260a6aa42.html ; https://www.ktvu.com/news/records-dna-from-tissue-led-to-golden-state-killer-arrest
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources

3. Investigator Paul Holes uploaded the crime-scene DNA profile to GEDmatch, a public genealogy website.
   - Sources: https://ktvu.com/news/critical-genealogist-in-golden-state-killer-case-speaks-out-about-her-role ; https://abc11.com/post/how-dna-from-family-members-helped-solve-the-golden-state-killer-case-da/3402829/ ; https://www.lamag.com/citythinkblog/dna-golden-state-killer/ (names GEDmatch)
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
   - Note: ABC11 says the DNA was entered into "a genealogy database" and does not name the site. LA Mag names GEDmatch.

4. The search returned more than 1,000 genetic cousins of the crime-scene profile.
   - Source: https://ktvu.com/news/critical-genealogist-in-golden-state-killer-case-speaks-out-about-her-role
   - Confidence: single source

5. Each match shows how much DNA two people share, which estimates how closely they are related.
   - Sources: https://www.idahofallsidaho.gov/DocumentCenter/View/8869/GG-Fact-Sheet-for-Media-Final-062118 ; https://pub.parabon.com/Parabon_Snapshot_Scientific_Poster-ISHI_2018.pdf
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources

6. Genealogists then build family trees for matched relatives using census, vital records and obituaries.
   - Sources: https://www.idahofallsidaho.gov/DocumentCenter/View/8869/GG-Fact-Sheet-for-Media-Final-062118 ; https://pub.parabon.com/Parabon_Snapshot_Scientific_Poster-ISHI_2018.pdf
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
   - Note: the Parabon poster supports the family-tree step. The record types come from the Idaho Falls fact sheet.

7. The family-tree work narrowed the list of possible killers to six men who lived in California.
   - Sources: https://ktvu.com/news/critical-genealogist-in-golden-state-killer-case-speaks-out-about-her-role ; https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/just-science-dna-just-golden-state-killer (NIJ, a DOJ agency)
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources

8. Investigators checked DMV records for the six men, and only one had the eye color linked to the killer.
   - Sources: https://ktvu.com/news/critical-genealogist-in-golden-state-killer-case-speaks-out-about-her-role ; https://nij.ojp.gov/library/publications/just-science-dna-just-golden-state-killer
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
   - Note: the DMV detail comes from KTVU only. The NIJ page confirms the eye-color point.

9. Investigators followed DeAngelo and collected a tissue he discarded in his trash.
   - Sources: https://www.ktvu.com/news/records-dna-from-tissue-led-to-golden-state-killer-arrest ; https://www.axios.com/facts-on-the-golden-state-killer-a665ff93-d682-464c-9c1c-b18260a6aa42.html
   - Confidence: verified by 2 sources

10. Lab testing matched the discarded DNA to the original crime-scene samples.
    - Sources: https://www.lamag.com/citythinkblog/dna-golden-state-killer/ ; https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/golden-state-killer-suspect-joseph-deangelo-charged-with-four-more-decades-old-murders
    - Confidence: verified by 2 sources

11. Police arrested Joseph James DeAngelo at his Sacramento County home on April 24, 2018.
    - Sources: https://www.cbsnews.com/amp/news/golden-state-killer-suspect-joseph-deangelo-charged-with-four-more-decades-old-murders ; https://abc11.com/post/how-dna-from-family-members-helped-solve-the-golden-state-killer-case-da/3402829/
    - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
    - Note: ABC11 says "Tuesday," which matches April 24, 2018. KTVU places the home in Citrus Heights, which is in Sacramento County.

12. In June 2020, DeAngelo pleaded guilty to 13 murder counts in a plea deal that avoided the death penalty.
    - Sources: https://abc7.com/post/golden-state-killer-admits-to-dozens-of-rapes-murders/6280795/ ; https://www.wdbo.com/news/national/california-golden-state-killer-pleads-guilty-more-than-dozen-murders/wyV4ksNzdHlmoDN32AwcFK/
    - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
    - Note: ABC7 says the deal spared him the death penalty. WDBO gives the June 29, 2020 date and the 13 counts.

13. Privacy critics argue that relatives who uploaded their DNA never agreed to police searches.
    - Sources: https://www.ksl.com/article/46560537/how-a-utah-assault-case-upended-the-cutting-edge-dna-website-that-caught-the-golden-state-killer ; https://www.lamag.com/citythinkblog/dna-golden-state-killer/
    - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
    - Note: KSL quotes Judy G. Russell on informed consent. LA Mag says the relative "may never have known law enforcement would check the database."

14. In 2019, GEDmatch changed its rules so police searches require users to opt in.
    - Sources: https://www.ksl.com/article/46560537/how-a-utah-assault-case-upended-the-cutting-edge-dna-website-that-caught-the-golden-state-killer ; https://www.oxygen.com/crime-time/gedmatch-new-policy-users-manually-opt-in-law-enforcement-access-to-dna
    - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
    - Note: the sources disagree on the month. KSL was published May 28, 2019 and describes the change as already made. Oxygen dates the new policy to August 4, 2019. Use "2019" only.

15. Positive identifications are confirmed with traditional STR DNA testing before an arrest.
    - Sources: https://www.idahofallsidaho.gov/DocumentCenter/View/8869/GG-Fact-Sheet-for-Media-Final-062118 ; https://pub.parabon.com/Parabon_Snapshot_Scientific_Poster-ISHI_2018.pdf
    - Confidence: verified by 2 sources
    - Note: Parabon is a commercial lab, so its poster is a secondary source for this step.

## Excluded as unverified or conflicting

- The "62 rapes" figure (WDBO) conflicts with the "50 rapes" figure from the same article. Claim 1 uses "more than 50," which Axios and CBS both report.
- Exact GEDmatch policy-change month (see claim 14).
- ABC11 says the DNA was plugged into a genealogy database about six days before the arrest. No second source confirmed this timing, so it is left out.
- Discarded-item DNA was "plugged back into" the database (ABC11). Only LA Mag and CBS confirm a direct comparison against the crime-scene sample, so this version is left out.
- Specific crime-scene details (for example, the semen sample) are left out to keep the reel non-graphic.
- No primary FBI or DOJ press release was located in this search. The NIJ page is the only federal source used, and it was only partly readable.

## Best hook (3 lines)

A killer went unidentified for decades, until a public DNA website turned up a distant cousin.
Investigators then built family trees from that one match and narrowed thousands of names to six men.
A tissue from a trash can finally tied one man to the crime scenes.
