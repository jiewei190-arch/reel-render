# Sources (spin)
- Wikipedia "Semicircular canals" (fetched): three canals (lateral, anterior, posterior) filled with endolymph; hair cells embedded in gelatinous cupula; on head rotation the endolymph lags by inertia, bending stereocilia; fluid catches up within ~10 s of constant motion; when a turn ends, the ducts signal rotation in the opposite direction.
- Not in that article (widely known, hedged in the script): the dizzy/nystagmus feeling after stopping, spotting technique used by dancers/skaters. Visual fluid timing is illustrative (about a second per cycle, not the real ~10 s).
- Hedge: "dizziness has many causes. This is the spinning kind."
