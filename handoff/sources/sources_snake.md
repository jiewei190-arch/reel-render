# Sources (snake)
- Toho University press release: https://global.toho-u.ac.jp/research/pr-1658/ (Current Biology, Oct 19 2026, "Locomotor kinematics and head stabilization in rolling snakes", Kojima et al., DOI 10.1016/j.cub.2026.08.057)
Verified from it: genus Pseudorabdion (dwarf reedsnakes, SE Asia); peak rolling speed 1.72-3.69 m/s, about 4x peak lateral undulation; body does not spin as one unit; roll on one side then a "stroke" swings an arched body section past the head; side alternates left/right; head stays aligned with travel, height relatively stable esp. faster; method multi-angle video; 20 cm wide racetrack.
Hedges: study sample size not given in release; escape purpose is the known/described use, not directly tested; head stability is "relatively" stable; applies to these snakes only.
