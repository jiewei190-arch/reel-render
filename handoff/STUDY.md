# Studying the accounts (owner, Oct 28)

Owner: "What I meant by growth is how we can grow together as a team, not just keep working non stop, we all need a break. The other meaning of growth is how we can grow the accounts together, how we can study it and learn what we're doing good and bad and grow that."

## The pace from now on
- Production pauses once the in-flight reels are finished (EP 63, 84, 99, 100, 102, 103, 104). That fills every slot through Nov 17, about 3 weeks of posts. Then the workers rest.
- Buffer target: **2-3 weeks, not more.** A bigger lead makes topics go stale and means lessons reach new reels late.
- Production restarts after the first study (Nov 2) at a pace that matches posting (about 5 reels a day across the team), and new topics, styles and hooks follow what the numbers say.

## What we study (per reel, per platform)
Read each reel at least 48 h after it posts. Log it in handoff/metrics.csv.
- **Reach:** views, and where they came from (For You / Shorts feed / search / profile).
- **Hold:** average watch time, average % watched, swipe-away rate (YouTube "stayed to watch"), and where the retention curve drops.
- **Response:** likes, comments, shares, saves.
- **Growth:** follows gained from that reel.

Every reel also has tags we already know: topic area, template, hook pattern (hooks_<n>.md), length, posting slot, weekday, platform. The study compares numbers across those tags.

## How the numbers get here
The accounts are touched only through the owner's PC session (Claude in Chrome), with the owner's OK. That session reads TikTok Studio / Analytics, YouTube Studio and Instagram Insights, and writes the numbers into handoff/metrics.csv. It only reads; it never changes or deletes anything. The prompt is in handoff/ANALYTICS_PULL.md.

## What we do with them (weekly, Mondays, starting Nov 2)
Written to handoff/STUDY_LOG.md:
1. Top 3 and bottom 3 reels per platform, and for each one *why*: where people left, what the hook was, what the topic was.
2. What's working and what isn't, by tag. Only call a pattern once there are at least ~5 reels on each side of the comparison; say "too early" otherwise.
3. **One or two changes** for the next week: topics to make more of, a hook pattern to drop, a template to rest, a length or slot to try. These go into GROWTH.md / LESSONS.md and into the briefs the workers get.
4. Questions viewers asked in comments become topic ideas (handoff/topics_backlog.md).
5. Plus the owner's own scores and notes from Reel Dailies.
