# Caption: fog (hand-drawn ink style)
Spooky fog isn't smoke 👻 Dry ice turns straight from solid to gas, chills the air above warm water, and the vapor condenses into tiny floating droplets. A fog machine heats a glycol or glycerin fluid into vapor that condenses the same way. Both are just tiny droplets hanging in the air. Safety first: never seal dry ice in a container, keep the room ventilated, and wear insulated gloves.
