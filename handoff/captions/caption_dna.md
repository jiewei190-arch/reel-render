## dna
How does a distant cousin's DNA catch a killer? 🧬
Five steps: crime-scene DNA → upload the profile to a public genealogy website (GEDmatch) → the site shows how much DNA people share, which estimates how closely they're related → genealogists build family trees from census, vital records and obituaries → a lab confirms the match.
In the 2018 Golden State Killer case that narrowed the list to six men in California. One report says the search returned more than a thousand genetic cousins.
Privacy critics say relatives who uploaded their DNA never agreed to police searches. In 2019 the site made police searches opt-in.
