# Hiccups reel caption (cartoon style, af_heart)
A hiccup is a tug and a slam 😮 Your diaphragm suddenly spasms and jerks down, then the glottis (the gap between your vocal cords) snaps shut. That snap is the "hic." A nerve loop (the vagus and phrenic nerves) sets it off, and often there's no clear cause. Why we have hiccups at all? Nobody knows for sure. And the famous cures (holding your breath, cold water, sugar, a paper bag) are unproven. If hiccups last more than two days, ask a doctor.
