## Sources (see sources_electricity.md for full URLs and confidence tags)
- Drift speed ~23 µm/s at 1 A in a 2 mm copper wire: Wikipedia "Drift velocity", cross-checked with Georgia State HyperPhysics (hyperphysics.gsu.edu/hbase/electric/ohmmic.html)
- Signal travels at roughly the speed of light: HyperPhysics (single source)
- Energy flows through the space around the wires: Univ. of Illinois Van Physics (van.physics.illinois.edu/ask/listing/20654); arXiv 1207.2173
- Steam, turbine, generator, magnet in a coil: U.S. EIA (eia.gov/energyexplained/electricity/how-electricity-is-generated.php)
- Higher voltage = lower current; losses = I²R; step-up transformers: University of Calgary Energy Education (energyeducation.ca/encyclopedia/Electrical_transmission); Wikipedia "Electric power transmission"
- About 6% of US electricity lost in transmission/distribution: Energy Education citing EIA (single source)
