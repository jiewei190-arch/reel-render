# reactor: How a nuclear power plant really works

**Title:** A nuclear power plant is basically a very fancy kettle ⚛️

**Caption:**
A nuclear plant boils water into steam that spins a turbine, just like a kettle, except the heat comes from splitting uranium atoms. Here is how the chain reaction, the control rods, the moderator and the fuel really work, and why, according to the World Nuclear Association, a power reactor cannot explode like a nuclear bomb.
