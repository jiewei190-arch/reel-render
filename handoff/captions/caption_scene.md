## Title (YouTube)
How a Crime Scene Really Works (Locard's Principle Explained)

## Caption
Every time two things touch, something moves between them. 🔍

That's Locard's principle, and it's why investigators secure a crime scene, photograph and sketch it before anything is moved, develop hidden fingerprints, and keep a signed chain of custody for every piece of evidence.

Fun fact: researchers say it's impossible to prove that no two fingerprints are identical.

Real procedures vary by agency and case. Follow for more of how it really works.
