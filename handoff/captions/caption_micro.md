# Microwave reel caption (Thu Oct 30, 1:00 PM)
Your microwave does NOT cook food from the inside out 🔥 It's a myth. The waves make water molecules spin, spinning makes friction, friction makes heat, but the waves only reach about 1 cm into most foods. The middle warms up later as heat spreads. (Depth varies by food.)

Tip: stir it and let it sit a minute. Did you know this? 👇 Follow for more.
