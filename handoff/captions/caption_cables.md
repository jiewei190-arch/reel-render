# cables: How the internet crosses the ocean

**Title (YouTube, <=100 chars):** Sharks DON'T cut the internet. Here's what does 🌊

**Caption:**
Everyone blames sharks, but fishing gear and ship anchors cause about 65 to 75 percent of undersea cable breaks (ICPC). Here's how the internet really crosses an ocean: pulses of light in glass fibers, boosted by repeaters, powered from the shore.
