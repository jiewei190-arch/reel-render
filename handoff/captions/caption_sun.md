# sun: How the Sun really works

**Title:** The sunlight on your face took up to a MILLION years to escape the Sun ☀️

**Caption:**
The sunlight on your face was made in the Sun's core. Estimates say it took 100,000 to about a million years to escape, then only 8 minutes 20 seconds to reach you. Here is how the Sun really works: fusion at about 15 million degrees, gravity vs pressure, and a very long random walk out. (These are estimates and sources differ.)
