# How a lie detector really works (polygraph)

**Title (YouTube):** The lie detector doesn't detect lies #Shorts

**Caption:**
The lie detector doesn't detect lies. It records breathing, heart rate, blood pressure and sweat, and a person has to interpret what those changes mean. In 2003 the National Academies called the evidence "scanty and scientifically weak." Subscribe for more of how it really works.
