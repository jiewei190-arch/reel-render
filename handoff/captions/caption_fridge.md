# fridge: How a refrigerator really works

**Title:** Your fridge doesn't make cold

**Caption:** Your fridge doesn't make cold. It moves heat. A refrigerant loops through four parts: the compressor squeezes it hot, the condenser dumps that heat into your kitchen, the expansion valve drops its pressure so it turns colder than your food, and the evaporator boils it, soaking up heat from inside. That's why the back of a fridge is warm, and why leaving the door open warms your room instead of cooling it.
