# Dizzy reel caption (Thu Oct 30, 7:00 PM)
Why you get dizzy after spinning 🌀 Inside your inner ear are 3 tiny loops filled with fluid. When you stop, the fluid keeps moving for a moment (like tea in a cup), so your ears say "spinning!" while your eyes say "still." That mismatch is the dizzy feeling.

Dancers and skaters use "spotting" (eyes on one spot, head snaps around late). Dizziness has many causes, this is just the spinning kind.
