# plane: How airplanes really fly

**Title:** Planes DON'T fly because of a longer top ✈️ (NASA says so)

**Caption:**
You were probably taught that air has to race over a longer top surface to meet the air underneath. NASA says that "equal transit time" idea is wrong: air over the top actually reaches the back edge first, and many wings (like symmetrical ones) don't even have a longer top. Here is how a plane really flies: the wing turns the air downward, the air pushes back, and lower pressure on top describes the same lift. Plus the four forces (lift, weight, thrust, drag) and what a stall is.
