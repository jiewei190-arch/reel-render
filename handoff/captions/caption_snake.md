This snake's escape plan is to become a WHEEL 🐍 Scientists just showed how a tiny dwarf reedsnake rolls away at up to ~3.7 m/s (about 4x its slither speed) while keeping its head steady. No axle, no extra joints, just a clever new way to move. (This is about these snakes, not every snake!)
