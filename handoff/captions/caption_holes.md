# Caption — "One galaxy. Three giant black holes." (yellow)
One galaxy. THREE giant black holes 🕳️ Astronomers used the James Webb telescope to spot them in a galaxy we see as it was over 12 billion years ago, and two of them may soon merge. Black holes in plain English: 1) gravity so strong nothing escapes, 2) "active" ones glow because they're eating gas, 3) Webb split the light into colors to find them. The authors call it evidence, not the final word.
