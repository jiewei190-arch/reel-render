# Noise-cancelling reel caption (Sat Oct 31, 1:00 PM) - teal
Noise-cancelling headphones don't just BLOCK sound. They fight it with MORE sound 🎧 Tiny mics listen, a chip flips the wave upside down, and when the two meet your ear, up cancels down. It works best on steady hums (airplane engines) and struggles with sudden sounds like voices.
