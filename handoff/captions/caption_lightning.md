# How lightning really works

**YouTube title:** A lightning bolt is 5x hotter than the Sun's surface ⚡ #Shorts

**Caption:**
Lightning is about 5x hotter than the surface of the Sun, but the flash you see is only the last step. Here's how it really works: charge builds in the cloud, a stepped leader heads down, a streamer rises up to meet it, and the return stroke makes the flash. Count the seconds between flash and thunder, then divide by 5 for the distance in miles.
