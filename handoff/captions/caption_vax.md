How a vaccine really works 💉

A vaccine is a practice run for your immune system. It shows your body a safe version of a germ's antigen (a weakened or killed germ, a piece of one, or instructions to make one), so your body builds antibodies and memory cells without the disease. Meet the real germ later and the response is much faster. Protection can take weeks, and some vaccines need several doses.

Sources: CDC "Explaining How Vaccines Work", WHO "How do vaccines work?", NIAID "Vaccine types".
