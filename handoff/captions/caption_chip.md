## Caption (Instagram + YouTube description)
Your phone's chip starts as sand 🏜️➡️📱
Silicon is purified, grown into a giant crystal, sliced into wafers, then printed with light through masks, layer after layer. A S M L says a modern chip can have up to 100 layers, and the finest ones use extreme ultraviolet light at 13.5 nanometers.
Plus: the "5 nanometer" in chip names is a marketing name. It no longer measures any physical feature on the chip.
