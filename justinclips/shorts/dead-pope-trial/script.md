# A Pope Put a Dead Pope on Trial (Justin clips Short #10, slug dead-pope-trial)
Narrator: Kokoro am_fenrir, deadpan storyteller. "## beat" lines = a beat of silence before the punchline.
1. A pope once put a dead pope on trial.
## beat
2. The dead pope lost.
3. Rome, eight ninety-seven.
4. Pope Stephen the Sixth has his predecessor, Formosus, dug up.
5. He'd been dead for nine months.
6. They dress him in his papal robes, and sit him on a throne.
7. Wait. How does a dead man defend himself?
8. You see, a deacon stands next to him and answers for him.
## beat
9. Badly.
10. Verdict: guilty. Everything he did as pope? Cancelled.
11. Months later, a new pope cancelled the verdict...
## beat
12. and banned putting dead people on trial.
13. Subscribe for more.
