## Dramatised (cartoon, not claimed as fact)
Flight caps and goggles, the salute, the stare-down between birds, the "Committed" stare, the stamps, the money bag, the shrugs,
the feather, the Navy officer's entrance, the rolling electronic box. The chalk doodle and the screens are schematic. The real
screens showed a projected image; the sea-and-ship picture stands in for "the target". The peck sound is synthesized, not a recording.
The scientist, officials and officer are generic toons, not likenesses of real people.
