## Fixes made while checking (also in STATUS.md)
- First draft line 2 said "up to 18 centimeters long": only Wikipedia (W2, "3–18 cm") gave it, no second source loaded → replaced by the "thousands of times its body weight" claim (W2 force + PC + NG).
- "Scientists compare it to a bullet": Wikipedia compares the *acceleration* (.22 LR from a handgun), Nat Geo the *speed* (.22 caliber bullet). The line says only "compare it to a bullet fired from a handgun" so it fits both; the on-screen chip says "≈ like a bullet".
- No temperature number is claimed: no loaded source gives a figure for the shrimp's bubbles (W3's "several thousand Kelvin" is general cavitation physics). The video says "a tiny flash of light" only.
- "83 km/h" is Wikipedia's figure for *O. scyllarus*; Nat Geo's 75 ft/s (~82 km/h) and Patek & Caldwell's "over 20 m/s" agree.
- Not loaded from the cloud: Nature 428:819 (login wall), UC Berkeley 2004 news (404), Smithsonian / Smithsonian Ocean / PNAS (403), BBC.
