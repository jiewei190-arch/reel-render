## Changed from the owner's kit (also noted in STATUS.md)
- "by 1903" became "around 1903", because the sources give 1903 or 1904.
- "Coca-Cola STILL uses coca leaves" became "Coke reportedly STILL uses coca leaves", because the company neither confirms nor denies it.
- The crate label "MERCHANDISE NO. 5" and the "guarded" facility were dropped. Wikipedia says the identities of Merchandise 5-9 "are a matter of debate", and no reputable source describes guards. The crates say "COCA LEAVES" instead.
- The Pope shot moved to the "sold as medicine" line, so it plays next to the medicine and tonic claims. Its on-screen text says "coca WINE", so it is not read as Coke.
