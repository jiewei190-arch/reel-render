## Narration (exact VO, as voiced after the source check)
1. The biggest swarm ever recorded was twelve trillion bugs. Then it vanished.
2. 1875. Rocky Mountain locusts swept across the American West.
3. One swarm was estimated at about 198,000 square miles. Bigger than California.
4. Around twelve and a half trillion insects. Guinness calls it the greatest concentration of animals ever recorded.
5. They ate crops, wood, leather... even the clothes off people's backs.
6. But less than thirty years later... they were gone.
7. The last one ever collected was found in 1904.
8. So what wiped out trillions of locusts?
9. One theory: farmers. Their breeding grounds were near streams and rivers in the Rockies.
10. Settlers plowed, irrigated and ran cattle on that exact land... and probably destroyed the eggs.
11. Today, their remains are still found... frozen inside a glacier in Montana.
