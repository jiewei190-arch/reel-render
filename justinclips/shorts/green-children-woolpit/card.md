The Medieval Legend of the Green Children of
 Woolpit
 TARGET EMOTION: mystery / WTF
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: two medieval kids with green skin standing at the mouth of a cave, villagers recoiling.
 Caption: "12th century legend". First line: "A medieval legend says two children with green skin
 appeared in an English village, speaking a language nobody knew."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 According to a 12th-century legend from Woolpit, Suffolk, two green-skinned children, a brother
 and sister, were found near the village speaking an unknown language and refusing all food except
 raw broad beans. The boy sickened and died, but the girl adapted, lost her green colour, and
 eventually explained they came from a land where the sun never shone and everything was green.
 Historians call it a legend or garbled folklore; no one has ever settled where the story came from.
