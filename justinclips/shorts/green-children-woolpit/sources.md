## Hedges kept / notes
- **Legend framing (owner's card):** line 1 "A medieval legend says...", top caption "a 12th-century LEGEND", line 10 "Historians call it folklore, or a garbled real event". The video never says it happened.
- **Where they were found:** the card's frame 1 says "the mouth of a cave". The article says they were found **beside a wolf pit** (William); the cave is in Ralph's version of how they got lost. The video uses the wolf pit (said in line 3, labelled on screen) and shows no cave.
- **"Everything was green"** is Ralph of Coggeshall's version (William says she called it St Martin's Land); it is told as the girl's story inside a legend.
- **"Died around the time of his baptism"** follows the article's lead; the body says "before or soon after baptism".
- Sources of the legend: William of Newburgh (c. 1189) and Ralph of Coggeshall (c. 1220), per the article. Not in the video.
