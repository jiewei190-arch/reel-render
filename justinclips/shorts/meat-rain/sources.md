## What is shown (tone guards)
- The meat is drawn as clean, pink cartoon steak chunks with a cream fat edge: comic, never bloody or gross. Nobody is hit or hurt.
- Vultures are comic toon birds: they get startled, turn green for a second, a single cartoon chunk drops, then they fly off fine.
- All characters are original Toon 3D beans (farm wife, two farmers, a scientist). Period clothes are simplified.
- "Throw up" is said once, plainly; no vomit is shown beyond one falling cartoon chunk.
