# The Secret Cinema Under Paris — script & shot list

**EMOTION:** WTF
**LINE 1:** "In 2004, Paris police stumbled onto a secret movie theater underground." (11 words)
**CAPTION (top, frame 1 → 3.5 s, 2 lines, centre-top):** "2004" / "a secret CINEMA under PARIS" (gold: 2004, CINEMA; red: PARIS)
**Frame 1:** pitch-dark limestone tunnel; two police flashlight beams already sweeping, landing on rows of red cinema
seats, a big screen and a little bar. Already moving.
**Target:** 40-48 s · Toon 3D · Chatterbox D2 · 1080×1920, 24 fps

## Narration (exact VO)
1. In 2004, Paris police stumbled onto a secret movie theater underground.
2. Deep in the catacombs, beneath the Trocadéro.
3. The catacombs hold the remains of more than six million people.
4. And down there, they found a giant cinema screen, seats for an audience, and projection equipment.
5. Film reels of recent thrillers and film noir classics.
6. But here's the part that gets weirder...
7. A fully stocked bar. And a complete restaurant, with tables and chairs.
8. Electricity and a phone line had been brought in from an unknown location.
9. The security system? A recording of barking dogs.
10. A secretive group called the U.X. took responsibility.
11. When police came back to investigate, it had all been dismantled. Left behind, a note... "Do not search."

Line 11's held beat ("a note... Do not search.") lands the payoff in the last ~2 s. The last shot is the same dark tunnel
and the same two flashlight beams as frame 1, now on an empty floor and the note; the cut back to frame 1 (beams finding
the cinema) loops.
