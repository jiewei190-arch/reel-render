Police Found a Secret Movie Theater Under Paris
 TARGET EMOTION: WTF
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: police flashlights revealing a full cinema, red seats and a bar, deep inside dark tunnels.
 Caption: "2004". First line: "In 2004, Paris police exploring the catacombs stumbled onto a fully
 equipped underground movie theater. A secretive group said it was theirs."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 In 2004, police discovered a fully equipped movie theater in the catacombs beneath the Trocadero
 in Paris: a giant cinema screen, audience seating, projection equipment, reels of thrillers and film
 noir classics, a stocked bar, and a complete restaurant with tables and chairs. A secretive group
 called les UX took responsibility. The catacombs hold the remains of more than six million people,
 and this was happening down there.

 "The Catacombs of Paris: The Empire of Death" (youtube.com/watch?v=pQJmn6z3h5Q); no
 dedicated video on the secret cinema in the results checked.

 TARGET SEARCH QUERY
