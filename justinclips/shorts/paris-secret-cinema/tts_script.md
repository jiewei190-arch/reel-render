# paris-secret-cinema narration (spoken = numbered lines)
1. In 2004, Paris police stumbled onto a secret movie theater underground.
2. Deep in the catacombs, beneath the Trocadéro.
3. The catacombs hold the remains of more than six million people.
4. And down there, they found a giant cinema screen, seats for an audience, and projection equipment.
5. Film reels of recent thrillers and film noir classics.
6. But here's the part that gets weirder...
7. A fully stocked bar. And a complete restaurant, with tables and chairs.
8. Electricity and a phone line had been brought in from an unknown location.
9. The security system? A recording of barking dogs.
10. A secretive group called the U.X. took responsibility.
11. When police came back to investigate, it had all been dismantled. Left behind, a note... "Do not search."
