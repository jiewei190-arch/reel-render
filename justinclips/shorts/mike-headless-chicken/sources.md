# Sources: Mike the Headless Chicken

Re-verified Nov 15 2026 (WebSearch + WebFetch of Wikipedia; the BBC page refused automated fetches, so its claims are checked through Wikipedia, which cites it, and through Guinness and Mental Floss) against the owner's kit (`justinclips/briefs/funny-kits-nov15_owner.pdf`).

## Every claim, sourced
- **Lived 18 months without a head**: Wikipedia, "Mike the Headless Chicken": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mike_the_Headless_Chicken ("a male Wyandotte chicken that lived for 18 months after he was beheaded"). Guinness World Records, longest surviving headless chicken: https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/74471-longest-surviving-headless-chicken
- **1945, a farmer (Lloyd Olsen of Fruita, Colorado); the axe missed the jugular and left most of the brain stem**: Wikipedia ("On September 10, 1945, farmer Lloyd Olsen of Fruita, Colorado..."; "The axe removed the bulk of the head, but missed the jugular vein, leaving one ear and most of the brain stem intact"). BBC News, "The chicken that lived for 18 months without a head" (2015): https://www.bbc.com/news/magazine-34198390
- **"Miracle Mike", toured, 25 cents a look**: Wikipedia ("put on display to the public for an admission cost of 25 cents"; other name "Miracle Mike"). Mental Floss: https://www.mentalfloss.com/posts/mike-the-headless-chicken-sideshow-oddity
- **He made Life magazine**: Wikipedia ("was featured in Time and Life magazines"); Guinness news (Life, Oct 22, 1945): https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2024/2/the-unbelievable-tale-of-the-chicken-that-lived-headless-for-over-a-year-764825 ; LIFE gallery: https://www.life.com/animals/life-with-mike-the-headless-chicken-photos-of-a-famously-tough-fowl/
- **Fed with an eyedropper**: Wikipedia ("He fed him a mixture of milk and water via an eyedropper").
- **One night they misplaced the eyedropper, and Mike choked (Phoenix motel, 1947)**: Wikipedia ("Mike started choking on his mucus in the middle of the night"; "The Olsens had inadvertently left their feeding and cleaning syringes at the sideshow the day before"); Guinness ("choked to death one night in an Arizona motel").
- **Fruita still throws him a festival every year**: Wikipedia ("an annual 'Mike the Headless Chicken Day' ... the third weekend of May since 1999").

## Kept hedges / simplifications
- What was left behind: Wikipedia and the BBC say the *syringes* (feeding and cleaning) were left at the sideshow. The owner's kit and many retellings say "eyedropper". The VO keeps the brief's "misplaced the eyedropper" and stays vague on details, as the kit asked ("keep it to choked, eyedropper lost"). Some sources give a different cause (the severed trachea couldn't take in enough air). The video says "choked" once and shows nothing.
- The beer bets in town (family account) were **cut**, not claimed.
- "Scientists say he could have lived for years" was **cut** (not needed for the payoff).
