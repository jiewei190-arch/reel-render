Britain's Plan to Build an Aircraft Carrier Out of Ice
 TARGET EMOTION: LOL / WTF
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: a cartoon aircraft carrier made of glowing ice, planes taking off, baffled sailors staring.
 Caption: "made of ice". First line: "Britain really planned to fight U-boats with an aircraft carrier
 made of frozen wood pulp, and it almost got built."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 During WWII, Britain seriously developed Project Habakkuk: a proposed aircraft carrier built from
 pykrete, a mix of ice and wood pulp. The design called for a 600-metre (about 1,969-foot) hull that,
 had it been built, would have been the largest ship ever. Lord Mountbatten reportedly
 demonstrated pykrete's strength to Churchill and Roosevelt's staffs by firing his pistol at a block of
 it; the bullet ricocheted around the room. A prototype was tested on Patricia Lake in Canada before
 rising costs and longer-range aircraft killed the project before construction ever began.
