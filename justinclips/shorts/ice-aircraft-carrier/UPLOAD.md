## Description
In WWII, Britain seriously planned Project Habakkuk: a giant aircraft carrier made of pykrete, frozen water mixed with wood pulp, to hunt U-boats in the mid-Atlantic. At 600 metres it would have been the biggest ship ever. Mountbatten reportedly shot a block of it and the bullet bounced off. A small prototype was built on Patricia Lake in Canada, but rising costs and longer-range planes sank the idea in 1943. The prototype took three summers to melt.
