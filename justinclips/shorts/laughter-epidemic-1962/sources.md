# Sources — laughter-epidemic-1962
- Wikipedia, "Tanganyika laughter epidemic": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tanganyika_laughter_epidemic — began 30 Jan 1962 at a girls' mission boarding school in Kashasha; 95 of 159 pupils affected; attacks from a few hours to 16 days; school closed 18 March 1962; spread to Nshamba (~55 mi) with 217 affected; ~1,000 affected overall; 14 schools closed; mass psychogenic illness; faded ~18 months after it began.
- Rankin & Philip (1963), Central African Journal of Medicine, "An epidemic of laughing in the Bukoba district of Tanganyika".
