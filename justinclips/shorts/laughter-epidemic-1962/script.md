# The Laughter Epidemic of 1962 — script

**EMOTION:** WTF
**LINE 1:** "In 1962, laughing shut down fourteen schools."
**CAPTION (top, 0-3.5 s):** "nobody could stop it"
**Frame 1:** a 1960s classroom, a few students laughing uncontrollably while a teacher freezes mid-sentence; chalk drops. Already moving.
**Target:** ~42 s

## Narration (exact VO)
1. In 1962, laughing shut down fourteen schools.
2. January 30th. A girls' boarding school in Kashasha, in what is now Tanzania.
3. Three students started laughing... and couldn't stop.
4. Within weeks, it spread to 95 of the school's 159 students. Some attacks lasted hours. Some lasted days.
5. In March, the school had to close.
6. But it didn't stop there...
7. It spread to a village fifty-five miles away. Two hundred and seventeen people were affected.
8. Then more villages. More schools. About a thousand people in total. Fourteen schools shut down.
9. No drugs. No gas. No poison. Doctors called it mass hysteria.
10. It took about eighteen months to finally fade away.

## Tone / accuracy guard
- It was distressing (laughing mixed with crying, fainting, restlessness): don't play it purely for laughs; the WTF is that it spread.
- Respectful, non-caricatured depiction of Tanzanian students and villagers.
