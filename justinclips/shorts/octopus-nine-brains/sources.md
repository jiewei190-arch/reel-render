# Sources: The octopus has nine brains and blue blood

Every claim is checked against Wikipedia plus at least one more reliable source (museum, magazine, encyclopedia).
Quotes are copied from the pages (fetched Nov 27, 2026).

## Notes
- "Nine brains" is the NHM's own popular framing (one central brain + a "mini brain" of nerve tissue in each arm), not a formal anatomy count. The script uses it the same way.
- Line 5: Wikipedia has no sentence saying the brain can override the arms (closest line quoted); the claim rests on the NHM quote ("top-down control"). Scientific American agrees: "some kind of mixture of localized and top-down control might be at work."
- "Blue" is the colour of the oxygenated copper form (deoxygenated blood is nearly clear); the script says "the blood is blue", which is how both sources describe it.
- Copper is better only in cold, low-oxygen water. Hemocyanin is generally *less* efficient per amount of blood (Wikipedia, Hemocyanin: "roughly one-fourth as efficient as hemoglobin"). Line 9 keeps that condition.
