# Sources — The Cat Mayor of Alaska (Stubbs, Talkeetna)

Re-verified Nov 15 2026 (WebSearch + the Wikipedia article source) against the owner's PDF sources (Wikipedia, AP News, CNN).

## Every claim, sourced
- **Stubbs was the (honorary) mayor of Talkeetna, Alaska, from 1997 until his death in July 2017, about 20 years** — Wikipedia, "Stubbs (cat)" (term July 18, 1997 – July 21, 2017): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stubbs_(cat) · AP via The Guardian, "Feline sad: the cat who was 'mayor' of Alaskan town for 20 years dies" (Jul 23 2017): https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2017/jul/24/stubbs-cat-mayor-alaskan-town-talkeetna-20-years-dies-margarita · CNN, "Honorary Alaska 'mayor,' Stubbs the cat, dies at 20": https://www.cnn.com/2017/07/23/us/mayor-cat-stubbs-dies-at-20/index.html
- **"Voters reportedly wrote in Stubbs"** — widely reported write-in story (UPI 2012, Houston Chronicle 2012, ABC7, CBC — CBC dates it 1998), cited in Wikipedia. Hedge kept: NPR noted "the tiny town has no real mayor, so there was no election", and an Alaska Dispatch News opinion writer called the story false. The VO says "reportedly"; the payoff says "Allegedly".
- **"It was honorary" / the town has no real mayor** — Wikipedia ("His position was honorary, as the town is only a historic district"); NPR, Jul 18 2012: https://www.npr.org/2012/07/18/156961189/following-up-on-feline-mayor-story-from-tuesday
- **His office was the general store** (Nagley's General Store used as his "mayoral office") — Wikipedia, citing CNN, "Mayor of Alaska village walks on four paws" (Jul 17 2012): http://www.cnn.com/2012/07/17/us/alaska-cat-mayor
- **Catnip water from a wine glass** ("water mixed with catnip out of a wine or margarita glass", every afternoon, at a nearby restaurant) — Wikipedia, citing CNN 2012, Alaska Dispatch 2013, Reuters 2017; NY Daily News 2012 ("drinking water out of a wine glass laden with catnip every afternoon").
- **30 to 40 tourists a day** — Wikipedia, citing NY Daily News, "Cat has been mayor of Alaska town for 15 years" (Jul 17 2012).
- **Survived a dog, a fryer and a BB gun** — dog attack Aug 31 2013: CNN, "Stubbs the cat, Alaska town mayor, mauled by dog": http://www.cnn.com/2013/09/04/us/alaska-cat-mayor-attacked/index.html · BBC (Sep 5 2013): https://www.bbc.com/news/world-us-canada-23971537 · BB guns and the deep fryer ("which was switched off and cold at the time"): Wall Street Journal, J. Carlton, "Mayor of Alaskan Town Is a Cat" (Oct 16 2013): https://www.wsj.com/articles/mayor-of-alaskan-town-is-a-cat-1381889957, as summarised by Wikipedia.
