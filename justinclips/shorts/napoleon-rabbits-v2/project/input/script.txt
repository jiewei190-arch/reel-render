Napoleon once lost a battle. To rabbits.
After a peace treaty, his chief of staff threw a rabbit hunt.
Hundreds of rabbits. Some accounts say thousands.
The cages opened... and the rabbits charged. At Napoleon.
Tame farm rabbits. They thought he had food.
A general wrote that the conqueror of conquerors... retreated.
He fled to his carriage. Some reportedly hopped in after him.
