## Hedges kept / notes
- **"wrecked its London station"**: Wikipedia says "badly damaged ... and rendered unusable"; the engineer's report said "demolished". The office building and platforms survived, so the video says "wrecked", not "destroyed".
- **"87 years"**: as Wikipedia's lead states; the last train ran 11 April 1941, five days before the raid (the video does not claim the raid hit a running train).
- **"every new grave cut into an old one"**: the Royal Commission's finding (1842), shortened.
- **"cemetery bar"**: the cemetery stations' licensed refreshment rooms ("bar" on screen as "REFRESHMENTS"). The driver's destination is "home" (Wikipedia: "back to London").
- **Religion split in hearse vans**: Wikipedia notes the Anglican/Nonconformist split of hearse vans "was little used in practice"; the video states the split as the rule the trains were built with, which the article states for the trains and the compartments.
- **Golfers**: the article gives no motive; the video only says they dressed up as mourners to ride to the golf course.
