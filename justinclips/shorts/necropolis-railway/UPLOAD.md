## Description
From 1854 to 1941, the London Necropolis Railway carried coffins and mourners 23 miles from London to Brookwood Cemetery. The dead rode one-way, the carriages were split by religion and class, golfers disguised themselves as mourners to reach a golf course, and in 1867 the driver got so drunk at the cemetery bar that the fireman drove the train back. A 1941 air raid wrecked its London terminus, and it never ran again.
