# Owner topic card #6 (from "JustinClips 20 More Niche Topics", Nov 20 2026)

London Had a Train Line Just for Dead People
 TARGET EMOTION: WTF / LOL
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: a Victorian steam train where the carriages carry coffins instead of passengers. Caption:
 "the corpse train". First line: "For 87 years, London ran a railway whose only cargo was corpses
 and the people burying them."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 The London Necropolis Railway opened in 1854 to carry coffins and mourners 23 miles out of the
 overcrowded city to Brookwood Cemetery. It had separate cars for the dead and the living, split by
 religion and social class, so no one's body had to share with the wrong crowd. It ran until its
 London terminus was wrecked in a 1941 air raid, and it never ran again.
