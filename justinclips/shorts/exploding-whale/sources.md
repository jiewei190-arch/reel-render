## Checked and changed or left out
- **"The official later got a park named after him":** not supported. The park is named for the **event** (W1: "in honor of the incident"), and no source names it after the engineer. W1 says instead that "Thornton was promoted to the Medford office several months after the incident". The video says "the town named a park after **it**" and never names the engineer.
- **"Nobody hurt":** W1 is silent on injuries and SR says "without any serious injuries", so the line says "Nobody was seriously hurt."
- **The car:** W1 says "damaged" and SR, W2 and MF say "crushed"/"crushing". "Crushed" is used (three sources), and the flattened car is a cartoon exaggeration of that.
- Not used: the engineer's name, the TV crew and reporter (the 1970 news film is not shown or named), the car's make and owner, the 800 ft distance and the bulldozer burial.
