In 1970, Oregon blew up a whale.
An explosives guy said 20 sticks would do it.
They used... 20 cases.
The news said the blast blasted blubber beyond all believable bounds.
One chunk reportedly flew a quarter mile... and crushed a brand new car.
Most of the whale... was still there.
Oregon now buries whales. And named a park after this one... Exploding Whale Memorial Park.
