## Narration (exact VO)
1. In 2006, scientists trapped a few crows for a study.            *(0:00–0:04, flat — let the hook text work)*
2. One of them wore a caveman mask. Big mistake.                   *(half-beat after "mask")*
3. For the next 17 years, anyone in that mask got screamed at and dive-bombed on sight.  *(pace picks up)*
4. It gets worse. Crows that were never trapped joined in. They learned the hate by watching.  *(mid-video hook)*
5. Then they taught it to their babies. A generational grudge.    *(comedy beat)*
6. The scientists tested a second mask to compare. They picked Dick Cheney.  *(straight face)*
7. The crows were completely fine with Dick Cheney.               *(deadpan — the quote line)*
8. The feud only ended in 2023, after the last crow from 2006 died.  *(slow, almost serious)*
9. So if a crow is staring at you right now… what did you do in 2006?  *(loop line → frame 1)*
10. Subscribe for more.                                            *(closer, < 1.5 s)*
