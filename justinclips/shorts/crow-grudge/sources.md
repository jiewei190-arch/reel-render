# Sources — The Crow Grudge

The real study: **John Marzluff's team at the University of Washington (Seattle)**. Starting in 2006 they trapped and banded crows while wearing a rubber "caveman" mask; the crows then scolded, mobbed and dive-bombed anyone in that mask — and kept it up for years. A **Dick Cheney mask** was the neutral control, and the crows largely ignored it. Never-trapped crows learned the reaction by watching, and parents passed it to their young. By **September 2023** — ~17 years on, about a year after the last 2006-trapped crow died — no crow scolded the mask on Marzluff's walk.

## Every claim, sourced
- **2006 start, caveman mask, UW Seattle, crows scold/mob the mask; Dick Cheney = the neutral control the crows ignored** — Science (AAAS), David Malakoff, "Is That a Caveman or Dick Cheney? Crows Know the Difference." https://www.science.org/content/article/caveman-or-dick-cheney-crows-know-difference
- **Crows recognise individual human faces and remember them; the masked-trapping experiment** — The New York Times, "Friend or Foe? Crows Never Forget a Face, It Seems" (26 Aug 2008). https://www.nytimes.com/2008/08/26/science/26crow.html
- **Social learning: never-trapped crows learn the dangerous face by watching peers, and young crows learn it from parents (horizontal + vertical learning)** — Cornell, Marzluff & Pecoraro, "Social learning spreads knowledge about dangerous humans among American crows," *Proceedings of the Royal Society B* 279:499–508 (2012). doi:10.1098/rspb.2011.0957 — https://royalsocietypublishing.org/doi/10.1098/rspb.2011.0957
- **The 17-year span and the grudge fading out by 2023 (peaking ~year 7, then tapering after the original crows died)** — Urban@UW, "Crows hold grudges against individual humans for up to 17 years." https://urban.uw.edu/news/crows-hold-grudges-against-individual-humans-for-up-to-17-years
- Background book by the lead researcher: John Marzluff & Tony Angell, *Gifts of the Crow* (Free Press, 2012) — the face-recognition work in long form.

The individual characters, the crayon "WANTED" poster, the crows "taking notes," one crow briefing others with a speech bubble, the bench gag, the "2006" gravestone, and the phrase "generational beef/grudge." ## Softened / noted
This is a single long-running campus study; some of the later figures (e.g. the exact year-by-year decline) come from data Marzluff has described publicly but not all fully peer-reviewed. The video says "according to the study" in spirit and keeps every hard number to the sources above.
