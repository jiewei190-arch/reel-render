1. In two thousand six, scientists trapped a few crows for a study.
2. One of them wore a caveman mask. Big mistake.
3. For the next seventeen years, anyone in that mask got screamed at and dive-bombed on sight.
4. It gets worse. Crows that were never trapped joined in. They learned the hate by watching.
5. Then they taught it to their babies. A generational grudge.
6. The scientists tested a second mask to compare. They picked Dick Cheney.
7. The crows were completely fine with Dick Cheney.
8. The feud only ended in twenty twenty-three, after the last crow from two thousand six died.
9. So if a crow is staring at you right now... what did you do in two thousand six?
