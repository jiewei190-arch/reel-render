## Notes and wording choices
- "numbers meant weak, letters meant strong" simplifies a linear scale: each character is one step of standard deviation
  (blank = 0-1, digits 1-9, A-Z = 10-35). Letters are the strong readings; the chart in the video shows the real values 6-14-26-30-19-5.
- "red circle": the video draws the circle in red marker, as the famous printout scan shows; none of the sources read states the ink colour, so no line says "red".
- "over 10,000 tweets": Wikipedia says "approximately 10,000", Space.com says "more than 10,000". The reply was aimed at three nearby stars,
  not exactly at the Wow! signal's patch of sky (Wikipedia: "None of the targeted stars is within the possible region from which the Wow! signal came"), so the line says "replied anyway", not "replied to the source".
- "listening for alien signals": Big Ear was running a SETI search in 1977 (Wikipedia, bigear.org); the video never says the signal was alien.
- The 2024 hydrogen-cloud idea is shown as a hypothesis ("a hypothesis", "MAYBE?", "UNCONFIRMED"). The arXiv abstract names the trigger as possibly
  "a magnetar flare or a soft gamma repeater"; the video shows a generic flaring star, not a claim about which object.
- Not used: "red ink" (unsourced), the golf course (sources differ on Big Ear's demolition year: bigear.org 1998, Space.com 1997), the hydrogen-line frequency (cut for length).
