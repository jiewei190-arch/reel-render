# What If You Fell Into a Black Hole? (Justin clips, Toon 3D + BEANMEN effects)
A what-if / thought experiment, told the way NASA (Goddard, J. Schnittman's 2024 black-hole plunge visualization) and Wikipedia explain it.
Voice: **Chatterbox D2** (built-in voice, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3) via `whatif/voice/chatterbox_d2.py --gap 0.3 --comma 0.15 --lead 0.15`,
then `projects/fall-into-black-hole/tighten_vo.py` (atempo 1.03, pauses capped 0.32 s). 11 lines, 130 words incl. the closer.
Numbers are spelled out for the TTS ("four million", "twelve point eight"); captions (`input/script.txt`) show digits (4 million, 12.8).
Top caption 0-3.5 s, 2 lines centre-top: **WHAT IF** (gold) you fell into / a **BLACK HOLE?** (red).
