## Description
From 1976 to 1989, a sharp tapping sound — 10 taps a second — blasted across shortwave radio worldwide, drowning out broadcasts, ham radio and even aviation. Listeners called it the Russian Woodpecker. It was a giant Soviet over-the-horizon radar, and its antenna still stands, abandoned, in the woods near Chernobyl.
