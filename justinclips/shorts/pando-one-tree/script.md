# Pando: This Forest Is Actually ONE Tree (Justin clips, Toon 3D + BEANMEN effects)
Nature-fact Short in the Immortal Jellyfish style. Voice: **Chatterbox D2** (built-in voice, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3) via `whatif/voice/chatterbox_d2.py --gap 0.3 --comma 0.15 --lead 0.15`,
then `projects/pando-one-tree/tighten_vo.py` (atempo 1.04, pauses capped 0.32 s). 11 lines, 124 words incl. the closer.
Numbers are spelled out for the TTS ("forty-seven thousand", "six thousand", "eighty thousand", "nine to sixteen thousand"); captions show digits.
Top caption 0-3.5 s, 2 lines centre-top: this whole **FOREST** (gold) / is **ONE TREE** (red). Age is hedged: the 80,000-year claim is called out as too old, the newer range is "maybe".
