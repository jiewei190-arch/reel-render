The Town Where Fish Reportedly Rain From Storms
 TARGET EMOTION: WTF
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: villagers holding umbrellas while fish lie scattered across a flooded street. Caption:
 "every year". First line: "Residents of this town in Honduras say fish have been turning up on the
 ground after storms for over a hundred years."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 In Yoro, Honduras, the lluvia de peces, or rain of fish, is a reported phenomenon where fish are
 found on the ground after heavy storms. Residents say it happens every year in the rainy season
 and has for more than a century, and the town holds a festival for it. The strange part: the fish don't
 appear to actually fall from the sky, and nobody has confirmed how they get there.
