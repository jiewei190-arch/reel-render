## Hedges kept / notes
- "Town": Wikipedia says "Yoro, Honduras" (Yoro is both a town and a department); the festival is held by "locals of the department of Yoro". The video says "this Honduran town … It's Yoro" (the card's wording) and "locals" for the festival.
- "Every year" / "over a hundred years" are always attributed to residents ("residents say", top caption "THEY SAY IT RAINS FISH").
- "A big storm" = Wikipedia's "large storms"; "mid-year rainy season" verbatim.
- "About 45 miles" = Wikipedia's "about 72 km (45 mi)"; the card reads "~45 MILES".
- Card "1800s": Father Subirana was in Honduras 1855–1864; the legend itself gives no date.
