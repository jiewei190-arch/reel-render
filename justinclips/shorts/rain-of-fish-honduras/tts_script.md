# rain-of-fish-honduras narration (spoken = numbered lines). TTS respelling: "Yuvia de Pesses" = Lluvia de Peces.
1. Residents of this Honduran town say it rains fish. Every year.
2. It's Yoro. Locals call it the Yuvia de Pesses, the rain of fish.
3. In the mid-year rainy season, a big storm rolls in...
4. and when it clears, there are fish lying on the ground.
5. Residents say it's happened for over a hundred years.
6. Since 1998, there's even a yearly festival for it, with a parade.
7. But here's the strange part. The fish don't appear to fall from the sky.
8. One theory is waterspouts. But the nearest ocean is about 45 miles away.
9. Another: fish from a nearby river live underground, and heavy rains wash them up.
10. And a local legend says a priest prayed to feed the poor... and fish fell.
11. Nobody has confirmed how they get there. So Yoro waits for the next storm.
