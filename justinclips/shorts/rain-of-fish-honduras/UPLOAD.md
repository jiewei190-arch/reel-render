## Description
In Yoro, Honduras, residents say that after big storms in the mid-year rainy season, fish turn up lying on the ground, and that it has happened for over a hundred years. Locals call it the Lluvia de Peces, the "rain of fish", and have held a yearly festival for it since 1998. The strange part: the fish don't appear to fall from the sky. Waterspouts from the Atlantic (about 45 miles away) and river fish washed up from underground water are the theories offered; a local legend credits Father Subirana's prayer. Nobody has confirmed how they get there.
