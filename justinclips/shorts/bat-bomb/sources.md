## Hedges kept / notes
- **"The plan: a million bats"** — the planned/proposed scale (Adams's proposal). Tests used thousands of bats; the video never says a million were used.
- **"About two years. About 2 million dollars."** — both said with "about"; figures as reported (Wikipedia, Flying).
- **Cancellation reason.** Said plainly per MAIN's tone guard. Sources: Smithsonian and Wikipedia tie the end of the program to the atomic bomb; Wikipedia also reports that Adm. Ernest King cancelled it on hearing it would not be combat-ready until mid-1945, and that the atomic link is how Adams himself explained it. This nuance is in this file, not in the 25 s video.
- **"Set their own air base on fire."** The fire hit the auxiliary air base / test range at Carlsbad Army Airfield (sources differ on how much burned). The video says "on fire", the top caption says "burned down their own base" (MAIN's wording; Task & Purpose's headline says "burned down one of its own bases").
- Name: the Army project was handed to the Navy in Aug 1943 and named Project X-Ray; the video uses the name for the program as a whole, as all sources do.
