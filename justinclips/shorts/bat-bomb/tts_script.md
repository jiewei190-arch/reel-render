# bat-bomb narration (spoken = numbered lines)
1. The U.S. military built a bomb out of bats.
2. World War Two. Project X-Ray.
3. The plan: a million bats, each carrying a tiny timed firebomb.
4. Drop them over Japan. They'd roost in attics... and then ignite.
5. In May 1943, armed bats escaped during a test...
6. and set their own air base on fire.
7. About two years. About 2 million dollars.
8. It was canceled in 1944. The atomic bomb project took priority.
9. The bat bomb was never used in combat.
