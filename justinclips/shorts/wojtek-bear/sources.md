| 1 | A bear served in the Polish army in WWII | [IWM]: "Wojtek, a Syrian brown bear, was the mascot of the 22nd Polish Artillery Support Company ... of the Second Polish Corps." [W]: "officially drafted into the Polish Army as a private". |

| 9-10 | So he was enlisted as a private | [W]: "To get around this restriction, Wojtek was officially drafted into the Polish Army as a private". |
| 11 | 1944, Monte Cassino, **reported** to have carried crates of artillery shells | [IWM]: "He is reported to have helped move artillery ammunition at Monte Cassino". | 13 | Promoted to corporal; retired to Edinburgh Zoo | [W]: "This service also earned him promotion to the rank of corporal"; "Following demobilisation on 15 November 1947, Wojtek was given to Edinburgh Zoo". Not said / not shown (tone guards): no fighting, no weapons fired, nobody hurt; Monte Cassino is shown only as far-away hills at
dusk with crates and trucks. Cigarettes are not mentioned. His death (1963) is not part of the Short. All soldiers, the British
officer and the zoo keeper are original toon characters, not portraits. No photos.
