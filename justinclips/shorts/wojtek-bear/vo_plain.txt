A bear served in the Polish army in World War Two.
He had a rank. And a serial number.
In nineteen-forty-two, Polish soldiers in Iran adopted a bear cub.
They named him Voytek.
His favourite drink? Beer.
His hobby? Wrestling the soldiers.
Then the British ship to Italy said: no pets allowed.
Wait, how do you get a bear on a troop ship?
You see, he wasn't a pet.
He was a private.
In nineteen-forty-four, at Monte Cassino, he is reported to have carried crates of artillery shells.
So his company made its badge a bear carrying a shell.
He was promoted to corporal, and retired to Edinburgh Zoo.
