# Sources: the War of the Bucket (Battle of Zappolino, 1325)

Checked Nov 16 2026 (web search + page fetches). Quotes from Italian sources are translated.

## Every claim, sourced
- **The legend says the war was fought over a bucket**: that is the popular story. Wikipedia "War of the Bucket" calls it largely incorrect: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/War_of_the_Bucket ("war was declared because Modena had captured the Bolognese castle of Monteveglio"). TheCollector: the bucket was "likely taken as a trophy, not the cause of war": https://www.thecollector.com/medieval-legend-war-bucket/ . The VO opens with "**Legend says**..." and then "The truth is pettier." It never claims the bucket caused the war.
- **1325, Bologna vs Modena, Battle of Zappolino**: Wikipedia "Battle of Zappolino": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battle_of_Zappolino ; Italian Wikipedia gives 15 November 1325: https://it.wikipedia.org/wiki/Battaglia_di_Zappolino (other sources say 13 Nov, so the video only says "1325").
- **About 32,000 Bolognese vs about 7,000 Modenese; Modena won**: Wikipedia "War of the Bucket" (about 32,000 vs about 7,000). Italian Wikipedia breaks it down as about 30,000 infantry + 2,500 cavalry vs 5,000 infantry + 2,800 cavalry. The VO says "about"; the top caption's "about 39,000 soldiers" is the sum of the two approximate figures.
- **After winning, they reportedly went to Bologna's gates and took a bucket from a well as a trophy**: Italian Wikipedia (the Modenese stayed outside Bologna's walls for days, held mock races, and took the bucket home as a trophy; it came from a well that still exists under a manhole cover outside Porta San Felice). TheCollector, above. Hedged with "reportedly" in the VO and on screen.
- **Modena still has the bucket**: City of Modena (UNESCO site pages): "Originally housed inside the Ghirlandina Tower", now in the Camerino dei Confirmati in the Palazzo Comunale (town hall): https://archiviopatrimoniomondiale.comune.modena.it/en/plan-your-visit/municipal-headquarters . TheCollector: "where it can still be seen to this day". A copy is shown in the Ghirlandina tower. The video shows the town hall, not the tower.
- **About 700 years later**: 1325 to 2026 = 701 years.

## Kept hedges
- "Legend says" for the bucket-caused-the-war story.
- "about" on both army sizes and on 700 years.
- "reportedly" on the march to the gates and the well.
- Casualties (sources range from about 2,000 to 3,000+) are left out entirely.

## Context not used in the VO
- Modena was Ghibelline (backed by the Emperor), Bologna Guelph (backed by the Pope) (Wikipedia, Battle of Zappolino).
- Alessandro Tassoni's mock-heroic poem "La secchia rapita" (written 1614-15, published 1622) made the bucket famous (Wikipedia; City of Modena).

## Dramatised (comedy, not claimed as fact)
Both armies charging at a bucket on a rock, the generals pointing at it, the LEGEND / PETTIER stamps, the losers running off in a dust cloud (nobody hurt), the sleeping guard, the thief's grin, the confetti parade, the tourist. Army colours (blue = Modena, red = Bologna) are toon colours, not real heraldry. Every character is an original toon.
