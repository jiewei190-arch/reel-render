# Owner topic card #17 (from "JustinClips 20 More Niche Topics", Nov 20 2026)

The Race Around the World With 1908 Cars
 TARGET EMOTION: LOL / wow
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: a rickety 1908 race car being dragged through Siberian mud while a crowd of villagers
 watches. Caption: "around the world". First line: "In 1908, six cars raced from New York to Paris
 through mud, snow and Siberia, in an era when roads barely existed."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 The 1908 New York to Paris Race sent six cars around the world at the dawn of the automobile.
 The American Thomas Flyer won after 169 days, covering about 16,700 km, after the German
 team was penalized 30 days for shipping its car part of the way by rail. The 26-day winning margin
 is still the largest in any motorsport event. Only three of the six teams finished at all.
