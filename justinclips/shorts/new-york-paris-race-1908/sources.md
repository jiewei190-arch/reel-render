## Hedges / notes
- **"Largest in motorsport history."** The article states it flatly, with no "reportedly" and no footnote on that sentence. The card attributed it "per the race record". The video hedges one step: "still **called** the largest in motorsport history", and the stamp reads "LARGEST WIN MARGIN / IN MOTORSPORT". Nothing stronger is claimed.
- **26 days vs 30 days.** The Germans arrived 4 days earlier and got a 30-day penalty: 30 − 4 = 26, which matches the article's margin.
- **"Around the world."** It's the card's framing, and the race's common nickname ("Race Around the World"). The cars crossed the oceans by ship, which the video shows (steamer).
- **"About 16,700 kilometers"** = the article's "approx 16,700 km" for the Thomas Flyer. Other sources quote ~22,000 miles for the full journey including ships; the video uses only the article's figure.
- The progress "measured in feet" line was left out (the article tags it {{Citation needed}}).
- "Thomas Flyer" (Thomas Motor Company, defunct 1919) is named in narration only. No logos or badges are drawn.
