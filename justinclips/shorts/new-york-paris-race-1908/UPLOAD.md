## Description
In 1908, six cars left Times Square to race from New York to Paris, with almost no roads ahead. They drove on railway tracks, were rerouted from Alaska by steamer to Japan, and sank into Siberian mud. The Germans reached Paris first but got a 30-day penalty, so the American Thomas Flyer won after 169 days and about 16,700 km. Only three of the six cars finished, and the 26-day winning margin is still called the largest in motorsport history.
