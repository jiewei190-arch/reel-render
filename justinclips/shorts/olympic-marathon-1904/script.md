## Narration (exact VO)
1. The 1904 Olympic marathon winner was fed rat poison.
2. St. Louis. Thirty-two runners. Dusty roads, traffic, even people walking their dogs on the course.
3. The first man across the finish line was Fred Lorz. The crowd cheered.
4. But he'd ridden in a car for miles. When it broke down, he jogged the rest of the way. Disqualified.
5. The real winner, Thomas Hicks, was barely alive.
6. His trainers kept giving him strychnine, mixed with brandy and raw eggs, to keep him going.
7. Strychnine is a poison.
8. But wait, it gets better...
9. A runner from Cuba stopped to eat some apples. They were rotten. So he took a nap. And still finished fourth.
10. Only fourteen of thirty-two runners finished. The winning time was the slowest in Olympic history.
