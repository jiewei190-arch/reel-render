## Narration (exact VO)
1. In 1876, it rained meat in Kentucky.
2. March third. Bath County. Between eleven and noon.
3. A farmer's wife, Mrs. Crouch, was making soap on her porch when chunks of meat started falling from a clear sky.
4. For several minutes, it was, in her words, "snowing meat."
5. A local grocer said the smell was like a dead body.
6. But it gets weirder...
7. Two men actually tasted it. They guessed lamb, or deer.
8. Scientists tested samples. Some were muscle, some cartilage, some lung tissue.
9. So where did it come from? One popular theory: a flock of vultures, throwing up mid-flight.
10. But nobody ever proved it. To this day, the Kentucky meat shower is still a mystery.
