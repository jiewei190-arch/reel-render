# Sources — The Greenland shark may live around 400 years

Checked 27 Nov 2026. Every claim has Wikipedia plus at least one more reliable source (the 2016 *Science* paper's abstract on PubMed,
a 2018 *Scientific Reports* paper, a 2025/26 open-access paper that restates the 2016 results, or the Florida Museum of Natural History).
Quotes are copied from the pages.

## Fixes and hedges (also in STATUS.md)
- Brief: "can live around 400 years" → "may live around 400" (estimate: 392 ± 120). The ± 120 is said out loud in line 6.
- Brief: "grows ~1 cm a year" → "less than one centimeter a year" (DEVINE "<1 cm yr−1"; W "0.5–1 cm per year").
- Brief: "matures ~150 years old" → "about 150" (W, HEART; NIELSEN gives at least 156 ± 22).
- Brief: "eye-lens radiocarbon dating study 2016" → kept. The sample size (28 female sharks) is in the paper but not in Wikipedia, so the video does not say it.
- Not used (only one source or gory): the eye parasite, the toxic meat, "slowest fish" without the "for its size" qualifier.
