A flower once cost more than a house.
Holland, the 1630s. Everyone wanted tulips.
The rarest ones had wild flame stripes... caused by a virus.
People traded bulbs still in the ground... on paper... in taverns.
One bulb reportedly sold for 10,000 guilders.
A craftsman made about 300 a year.
Then, in February 1637... the market crashed.
But a historian who checked the records... couldn't find anyone who went bankrupt.
