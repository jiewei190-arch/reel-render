## beat
2. And people bought it.
3. New York, August {1835|eighteen thirty-five}. The Sun runs a front-page scoop.
4. It says the famous astronomer John Herschel has seen life on the Moon. Through a giant new telescope.
5. First, animals like bison. Then, unicorns.
6. Then beavers, walking on two legs, carrying their babies.
7. And finally... people. With bat wings.
8. Wait. Did Herschel really see that?
## beat
9. You see, Herschel was in South Africa, and had no idea.
10. A writer at the Sun made it up. To poke fun at people, and to sell papers.
11. According to one account, the story sold {60,000|sixty thousand} copies as a pamphlet.
12. And the Sun? It never admitted it was fake.
