2. At least, that's the classic account.                                     *(attribution; "*there is another version" on screen)*
3. A Greek soldier's dog ran across the Bulgarian border, and he chased after it.
4. A Bulgarian sentry saw him... and shot him.                               *(said plainly; shown only as a flash + birds off the ridge)*
5. The Greek version is different: Bulgarian troops crossed over and attacked a Greek outpost.   *(the competing Greek version)*
6. But here's where it gets wild. Greece gave Bulgaria 48 hours: apologize, punish those responsible, and pay 2 million francs.   *("But" turn)*
7. Then Greek troops marched into Bulgaria, toward the town of Petrich.
8. Bulgaria appealed to the League of Nations... which ordered a ceasefire hours before the attack.
9. Greece had to pull out and pay Bulgaria 45,000 pounds.
10. Ten days. Dozens of lives lost... and history calls it the War of the Stray Dog.   *(sober beat, then payoff in the last ~2 s → loop)*
