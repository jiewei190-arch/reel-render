## Hedges / notes
- **Attribution kept.** Line 1 says the soldier "accidentally started a war"; line 2 immediately frames it as "the classic account", and line 5 gives the competing Greek version. The dog story is never stated as settled fact.
- **Dates.** The article body gives October 18 for the shooting; the infobox gives the conflict as 19–29 October. The video only says "1925" and "ten days", which both fit. (The card said "Oct 19–29" for the conflict and "October 1925" for the incident.)
- **Petrich.** Some 1925 newspapers reported the town captured; Wikipedia says the ceasefire came hours before the attack on the town. The video says "toward the town of Petrich", never "took it".
- **"Shot him"** follows Wikipedia's wording ("shot the Greek soldier"). Contemporary cables (cited by Wikipedia) report a Greek captain and a sentry shot dead; the video does not give a Greek death count.
- **"Dozens of lives lost"** is a deliberately conservative summary of 50 Bulgarian dead (plus a Bulgarian estimate of 121 Greek dead).
- **"2 million francs"** = French francs (Wikipedia); the on-screen list says "2,000,000 FRANCS".
