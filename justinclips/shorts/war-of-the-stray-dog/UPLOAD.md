## Description
In October 1925, according to the classic account, a Greek soldier chased his runaway dog across the Bulgarian border and was shot by a Bulgarian sentry. The Greek version says Bulgarian troops raided a Greek outpost instead. Greece sent an ultimatum, then troops toward the town of Petrich. It lasted ten days and cost dozens of lives, and it is remembered as the War of the Stray Dog (the Incident at Petrich).
