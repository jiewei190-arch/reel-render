# Owner topic card #7 (from "JustinClips 20 More Niche Topics", Nov 20 2026)

Greece and Bulgaria Went to War Over a Dog
 TARGET EMOTION: LOL
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: a confused dog trotting across a border line while two armies glare at each other behind
 it. Caption: "over a dog". First line: "In 1925, a soldier chasing his runaway dog accidentally
 triggered an international war, at least according to the classic account."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 In October 1925, according to the classic account, a Greek soldier ran after his dog across the
 Bulgarian border and was shot; the Greek version blames a Bulgarian raid on an outpost instead.
 Greece invaded the border town of Petrich in retaliation, Bulgaria appealed to the League of
 Nations, and the League ordered Greece to withdraw and pay about 45,000 pounds in
 compensation. The whole affair lasted ten days, and history remembers it as the War of the Stray
 Dog.
