- **"Viewers called asking how to grow their own"**: kept, said as "viewers called in" and "some wanted to know how to grow their own".
  "Hundreds" is Wikipedia only, so no number is spoken.
- **Left out (Wikipedia only; the other sources fetched don't have it, and the BBC and CNN pages couldn't be fetched):** "an estimated 8 million
  viewers", the "mild winter / bumper crop" line, and CNN's "biggest hoax that any reputable news establishment ever pulled".
- The narrator is not named in the VO ("one of Britain's most trusted broadcasters"), and on screen he is a generic toon presenter with no likeness.
- The "black-and-white TV" look is a period cue for 1950s television, not a claim about the footage.
