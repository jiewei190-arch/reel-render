The shortest war in history lasted 38 minutes.
Zanzibar, 1896. A new sultan seized the throne. Britain said: gone by nine in the morning.
At nine, the warships opened fire. The royal yacht sank almost at once.
At nine thirty-eight, the flag came down. The sultan fled to the German consulate.
Around 500 defenders were killed or wounded. Britain had one injured sailor.
Still the shortest war ever recorded.
