1. The shortest war in history lasted 38 minutes.
2. Zanzibar, 1896. A new sultan seized the throne. Britain said: gone by nine in the morning.
3. At nine, the warships opened fire. The royal yacht sank almost at once.
4. At nine thirty-eight, the flag came down. The sultan fled to the German consulate.
5. Around 500 defenders were killed or wounded. Britain had one injured sailor.
6. Still the shortest war ever recorded.
