## Line-by-line
**1. "A masked stranger hijacked Chicago TV in 1987. Twice."**
- [1] "On November 22, 1987, two television stations based in Chicago, Illinois, were hijacked two hours apart from one another..."
- [3] "The Max Headroom signal hijacking occurred on the night of November 22, 1987..."
- [2] "On 22 November 1987, sports anchor Dan Roan of Chicago's WGN-TV News Network was narrating..."

**2. "The nine o'clock news. The sports report... and then, this."**
- [1] "The first intrusion took place at 9:14 p.m. during the sports segment of WGN-TV's The Nine O'Clock News."
- [2] "...sports anchor Dan Roan of Chicago's WGN-TV News Network was narrating..." (the Bears highlights)

**3. "A man in a rubber mask and sunglasses, dressed as an 80s TV character."**
- [1] "...a person wearing a Max Headroom mask and sunglasses appeared."
- [2] "rubber-masked imposter"
- Max Headroom is a 1980s TV character. The name is not said or shown in the video (brief); the narration says "an 80s TV character".
- Note: sunglasses are in [1] only; [2] and [3] don't mention them.

**4. "Behind him, a swiveling sheet of metal, mimicking the character's fancy background."**
- [1] "...rocked erratically in front of a semi-rotating corrugated metal panel that mimicked the real Max Headroom's geometric background effect"
- [3] "a corrugated iron panel swiveling in the background"
- [2] (WTTW) "a randomly gyrating panel of corrugated metal"

**5. "No voice. Just a loud buzz. Then engineers switched frequencies, and he vanished."**
- [1] "The perpetrators were unable to broadcast any audio; the only sound viewers heard was a loud buzz."
- [2] "As a low buzzing sound belched from thousands of televisions throughout Chicago..."
- [1] "...cut off when engineers at WGN changed the frequency of the signal linking the broadcast studio to the station's transmitter atop the John Hancock Center."
- [2] "WGN-TV's on-site technicians neutralized the 'pirate' transmission by switching to an alternate transmitter." (same outcome, different wording)
- Duration of the first intrusion is NOT stated in the video: [1] gives both "nearly 17 seconds" (lead) and "about 30 seconds" (body).

**6. "The sportscaster, live: Well, if you're wondering what's happened... so am I."**
- [1] "Well, if you're wondering what's happened, so am I"
- [2] "Well, if you're wondering what happened… so am I."

**7. "But here's the part... two hours later, he came back. On another station, during Doctor Who."**
- [1] "...were hijacked two hours apart from one another..."; the second intrusion was on PBS member station WTTW "during an airing of the first part of the Doctor Who serial Horror of Fang Rock", "at about 11:20 p.m."
- [2] "...an episode of the British sci-fi series Doctor Who" "at 11:15pm" (so the video says "two hours later", not a clock time)

**8. "This time with sound. About 90 seconds of distorted, bizarre nonsense."**
- [1] "this time audible through distorted audio" ; "normal programming resumed after an interruption of about 90 seconds."
- [3] "The hijacking lasted about 90 seconds before it shut off on its own."
- [2] "launched into an eccentric diatribe in a highly distorted voice"; [1] "a quick series of brief and seemingly unrelated comments and cultural references"
- The crude content is deliberately not described or shown.

**9. "And nobody could stop him. No engineers were on duty at the tower."**
- [1] "Technicians at WTTW's studios could not counteract the signal takeover because there were no engineers on duty at that hour at the Sears Tower..."
- [2] "With no engineers on location at the transmission tower, WTTW employees looked on helplessly as the intruder seized control of their broadcast."

**10. "The FCC investigated. To this day... nobody knows who was under the mask."**
- [1] "A criminal investigation conducted by the Federal Communications Commission (FCC) in the immediate aftermath of the intrusion could not find the people responsible..." ; "the perpetrators have yet to be identified." ; "No one has ever claimed responsibility for the stunt."
- [2] "The Federal Communications Commission and the FBI sprang into action, launching independent pirate-hunting squads..."
