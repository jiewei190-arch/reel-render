## Visual gags (each one tied to a sourced fact)
1. **Yawning welcome signs** (the towns' names, made literal): Boring yawns, Dull catches it.
2. **The motto** "The most exciting place to live" on a banner over the Boring sign; its eyelids sink while crickets chirp.
3. **The vote**: a planning-group meeting where every hand rises slowly and in sync, one member asleep (fact: 38 members, unanimous "aye").
4. **"A pair for the ages"**: the deadpan "ceremony": the two signs lean together under the banner, a limp kazoo "ta-da", three confetti.
   (A comic staging of the pairing; no real ceremony is claimed.)
5. **Party**: party hats on the signs, two balloons that slowly sag; Dull yawns on "party".
6. **Bagpipes**: a bagpiper marches past and the signs' eyes pop open... then droop again (fact: the celebration has a piper).
7. **Bland slides in**, also yawning; then capes, god rays and a low hero angle for the League of Extraordinary Communities.
8. **Payoff**: beat of silence ("Also known as..."), eyelids sink, all three fall asleep and snore: TRINITY OF TEDIUM.
