# The Dead Jockey Who Won — script
**EMOTION:** WTF
**LINE 1:** "In 1923, a jockey won a horse race after he died."
**CAPTION (top, 0-3.5 s):** "he was already DEAD"
**Frame 1:** a horse thundering over the finish line, crowd cheering, the jockey slumped strangely in the saddle. Already moving.
**Target:** ~40 s
## Narration (exact VO)
1. In 1923, a jockey won a horse race after he died.
2. Belmont Park, New York. Frank Hayes, twenty-two years old.
3. He wasn't even a real jockey. He was a horse trainer and stable hand, and he'd never won a race.
4. His horse, Sweet Kiss, was a twenty-to-one long shot.
5. But somewhere in the last part of the race, Frank had a fatal heart attack.
6. And his body stayed in the saddle...
7. Sweet Kiss crossed the finish line first. Won by a head.
8. Nobody noticed until the owner came over to congratulate him.
9. Officials made the result official anyway. He was buried three days later, in his racing silks.
10. He's the only jockey known to have won a race after death. And Sweet Kiss never raced again.
## Tone / accuracy guard
- A real young man died: no slapstick about his death. The WTF is the horse finishing; keep the fall gentle/off-screen.
