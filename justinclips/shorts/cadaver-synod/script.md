## Narration (exact VO)
1. In 897, a pope put a dead pope on trial.
2. Rome. Pope Stephen the Sixth had his predecessor, Pope Formosus, dug up.
3. Formosus had been dead for about nine months.
4. They dressed the corpse in papal robes and propped it up on a throne.
5. A deacon was assigned to answer the questions for him.
6. But here's the part that gets darker...
7. The dead pope was found guilty. His papacy was declared invalid.
8. They cut off the three fingers he used to give blessings, and later threw his body into the Tiber River.
9. Then the public turned on Stephen. He was overthrown and strangled in prison that same year.
10. And months later, a new pope reversed the whole trial and gave Formosus a proper burial.
