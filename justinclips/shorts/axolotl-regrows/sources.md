## Fixes / wording choices (also in STATUS.md)
- "Heart" is worded "parts of its heart" in line 5 (Wikipedia says "tissues of the ... heart"); line 1 keeps the brief's punchy "its heart" with Nat Geo's "hearts", and the line 1 claim "even parts of its brain" follows both sources.
- "Brain" is always "parts of its brain" (Wikipedia: "non-vital parts"). The insert card says "ITS BRAIN (parts of it)".
- "Lives only near Mexico City" in the brief → "down to a few canals near Mexico City" (Nat Geo's wording); no claim that they live nowhere else.
- No regeneration count or size figure is used (the sources disagree on size: Wikipedia 15–45 cm vs Nat Geo "up to 12 inches").
- The hero is the pink (leucistic) pet/lab colour. Not loaded from the cloud: IUCN Red List page (403), Science 2022 brain-regeneration paper (403), NHM (404).
