# axolotl-regrows narration (spoken = numbered lines)
1. This salamander regrows its legs, its heart, even parts of its brain.
2. Meet the axolotl, the salamander that never grows up.
3. It keeps its larval body for life, frilly gills and all.
4. Lose a leg? A new one grows back within months.
5. But that's just the start. Its tail, its spinal cord, even parts of its heart grow back, without scars.
6. But here's the wild part. It can rebuild parts of its brain.
7. Scientists study it, hoping humans could one day heal like this.
8. But in the wild, the axolotl is critically endangered.
9. It's down to a few canals near Mexico City, with maybe 50 to 1,000 adults left.
10. It can regrow parts of its brain... but not its home.
