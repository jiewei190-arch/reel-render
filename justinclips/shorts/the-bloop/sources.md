## Notes and wording choices
- The brief's "louder than anything we knew" is not supported: no source says it was the loudest sound. The script says "extremely loud" (NOAA,
  Popular Science) and "Reportedly, ... far louder than a blue whale" (Popular Science; Wikipedia quotes the writer David Wolman — an inference, not a NOAA finding).
- Distance: Wikipedia's text also says "heard on multiple sensors at a range of over 5,000 km", NOAA says its hydrophones were "placed more than
  3,219 kilometers apart", Wolman/Popular Science/Gizmodo say about 3,000 miles. The script uses "about 3,000 miles apart", which the most sources share.
- Pitch: Wikipedia says it "rose" in frequency; Popular Science says it "rose and fell". The script avoids the direction ("ultra-low ... about a minute long").
- Year of the answer: NOAA says 2005; Wikipedia says "by 2012" for NOAA's icequake description. The script uses NOAA's own year.
- The sounds in the video are original syntheses (make_sfx.py), not the NOAA recording. The H.P. Lovecraft link and the "May 1997" date were not used (not in the sources).
