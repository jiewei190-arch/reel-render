# Sources: Switzerland Made Owning One Guinea Pig Illegal

**The rule:** Switzerland's Animal Protection Ordinance (Tierschutzverordnung, TSchV; in force since September 2008) treats some species as social animals. Guinea pigs, mice, rats, gerbils, degus, chinchillas, parrots, lovebirds and others must have company of their own species, so in practice you keep at least two.

## Every claim, sourced
- **"Owning one guinea pig is *basically* illegal": guinea pigs must be kept in groups of at least two. FSVO quote: "Guinea pigs are very social animals and may not be kept on their own." You can still *buy* a single guinea pig; keeping it alone is what breaks the ordinance.** Source: swissinfo.ch fact check, "Loo-flushing, explosives, gold: fact-check lonely guinea pigs and other quirky Swiss rumours": https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/culture/loo-flushing-explosives-gold_fact-check-lonely-guinea-pigs-and-other-quirky-swiss-rumours/45067078
- **The 2008 law; social pets (canaries, parrots, rats, mice, guinea pigs) may no longer be kept singly; rent-a-guinea-pig services for owners whose pig loses its partner (Priska Küng's service in Hinwil, near Zürich).** Source: swissinfo.ch (2008), "Don't let your guinea pig get lonely": https://www.swissinfo.ch/eng/rent-a-rodent_don-t-let-your-guinea-pig-get-lonely/965212
- **Article 13 TSchV: social animals must be accompanied by another animal of the same species; when one of a pair dies, the owner has to find a companion.** Source: The Local Switzerland, "Most people get pets to counter their own loneliness – but what happens if the pets themselves get lonely?": https://apiwp.thelocal.com/?p=692019 (The Local's own link to the same article)
- Background on the rental service: PRI/The World, "Rent a Guinea Pig in Switzerland" (2011): https://theworld.org/stories/2011-09-21/rent-guinea-pig-switzerland
