|---|---|---|

| "The water weighs tons" / "over 8 tons press on every square inch" | [EarthDate (UT Austin): The Deepest Deep](https://www.earthdate.org/episodes/the-deepest-deep) · [Live Science](https://www.livescience.com/how-deep-is-the-mariana-trench) | ~16,000 psi ≈ 8 tons/in², ~1,000x sea level. Rounded, as TOPICS says. |

| "The light dies fast." | [NOAA Ocean Service: How far does light travel in the ocean?](https://oceanservice.noaa.gov/facts/light_travel.html) | Sunlight fades within the top ~200 m; essentially none below ~1,000 m. |
| "Drop Mount Everest in... and there's still over a mile of water on top." (caption: "Everest would fit inside with room to spare") | NOAA depth above + Everest 8,849 m ([Britannica](https://www.britannica.com/place/Mount-Everest)) · [EarthDate](https://www.earthdate.org/episodes/the-deepest-deep) | *(computed)* 10,935 − 8,849 ≈ 2,090 m ≈ 1.3 miles. |
| "Only a handful of people have ever been down there." | [Wikipedia: List of people who descended to Challenger Deep](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_people_who_descended_to_Challenger_Deep) · [Guinness: most visits to the Challenger Deep](https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/629183-most-times-to-visit-the-challenger-deep-by-an-individual) | 27 people as of 2022 (first: Piccard and Walsh, Trieste, 1960). "A handful" kept from TOPICS (no number on screen since the count changes). |
