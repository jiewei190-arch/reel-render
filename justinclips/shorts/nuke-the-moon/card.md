The Air Force Planned to Nuke the Moon
 TARGET EMOTION: WTF
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: a 1950s general pointing a stick at a giant Moon with a mushroom cloud on it. Caption:
 "project A119". First line: "In 1958 the U.S. Air Force had a real plan to detonate a nuclear bomb on
 the Moon, just to show off."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 Project A119 was a top-secret 1958 U.S. Air Force study into detonating a nuclear bomb on the
 Moon. The flash would have been faintly visible from Earth with the naked eye, which was the
 point: a show of force to boost American morale after the Soviet Union's early lead in the Space
 Race. The plan was abandoned, and the Moon remains unnuked.

 Nuke the Moon" (youtube.com/watch?v=fbpS_Hp6S8c), but coverage was thin in the results
 checked.
