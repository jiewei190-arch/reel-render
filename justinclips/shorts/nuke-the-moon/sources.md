# Sources — Nuke the Moon (Project A119)

Checked 20 Nov 2026 against the card's source: Wikipedia, "Project A119" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_A119
(fetched 20 Nov 2026, revision oldid 1386496123). Quotes below are verbatim from that article.

|---|---|

| 3. "The Soviet Union had taken an early lead in the Space Race." | "…a boost that was needed after the Soviet Union took an early lead in the Space Race." |

## Other facts on screen
- Folder "A Study of Lunar Research Flights · U.S. AIR FORCE · 1958" — report title and year, as above.
- Folder "MAY 1958 – JAN 1959" — "In May 1958, ARF began covertly researching…" + cancelled January 1959.
- "W25 WARHEAD · 1.7 kt · small · lightweight" — as above.

## Hedges / notes
- The purpose is a hope, not an outcome ("possible boosting of domestic morale"); the video says "the idea was".
- The card's "just to show off" (show of force) is kept in the top caption idea only; narration says "a show of force".
- Not used (article discrepancies or extra detail): the lead says the project was revealed in 2000 by Leonard Reiffel,
  the body says writer Keay Davidson found it in the mid-1990s; the Soviet "E-4" counterpart ("identical" in the lead,
  "differs" in the body); "other reports were destroyed in the 1980s by the Illinois Institute of Technology".
- Visual "SOVIET EARLY LEAD" satellite is a generic cartoon satellite (no flags or emblems).
