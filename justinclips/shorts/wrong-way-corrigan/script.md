## Narration (exact VO, 11 lines, 116 words)
1. A pilot flew to Ireland... and said he was heading for California.
2. 1938. Douglas Corrigan wanted to fly across the Atlantic. Officials said no.
3. His plane was a nine-year-old Curtiss Robin. No radio.
4. Extra fuel tanks reportedly blocked his view straight ahead.
5. So on July 17th, he filed a flight plan to California.
6. He took off from New York. California is west. He went east.
7. 28 hours later, he landed in Dublin, Ireland.
8. He said he got lost in the clouds and misread his compass.
9. But here's the part... his license was suspended only until his ship got home.
10. New York threw him a ticker-tape parade. And publicly, he never admitted a thing.
11. Subscribe for more.
