## Description
In the early 1500s, rats near Autun, France, were reportedly put on trial for eating the barley. Their lawyer, Barthélemy de Chasseneuz, argued that one summons wasn't enough and that the town's cats made the trip unsafe. The case was reportedly thrown out.
