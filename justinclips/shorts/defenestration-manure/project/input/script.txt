Three men got thrown out of a castle window.
Prague, 1618. Angry nobles stormed the castle.
They grabbed two royal governors and their secretary... and out they went.
About 70 feet down.
All three survived.
Catholics said angels caught them.
Later pamphlets said... they landed in a pile of manure.
Historians think that part was probably made up.
But the secretary got a noble title... Baron von Hohenfall.
Baron of High Fall.
