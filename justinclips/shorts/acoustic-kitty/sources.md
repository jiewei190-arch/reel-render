**Dropped on purpose (animal tone guard):** the taxi ending. Marchetti said the cat was hit by a taxi; Robert Wallace (former director of the CIA's Office of Technical Service) disputed this in 2013: "the equipment was taken out of the cat; the cat was re-sewn for a second time, and lived a long and happy life afterwards" (Wikipedia). The cat is never shown hurt; the gadgets appear only as a cartoon x-ray (no surgery).
Cartoon garnish (not claims): the embassy party, the butterfly, the fish bowl, the glass knock.
