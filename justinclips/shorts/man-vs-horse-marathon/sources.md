## Notes and changes vs the topic card
- **Distance:** the card's "22 miles" is used. W's lead also says "21 miles (34 km)", and the course has changed over the years (W: about 24 miles in 2013; some recent reports say 23.5). "22 miles" is what W's history section, AW and BR all report.
- **"Only a handful of times":** stated exactly as **5 human wins** (2004, 2007, 2022, 2023, 2025), matching W's table and the organiser's list.
- **Line 2 "argument":** the sources call it "a discussion" (W), "a chat over a pint" (O) and "a bet" (BR). The VO says "an argument", meaning two men arguing opposite sides. Nothing beyond that is claimed.
- **"Every year":** the race is held every June (W, BR), but 2020 was not held (COVID-19).
- **Not used:** the landlord's and the pub's names (the TTS would mispronounce "Llanwrtyd"; the town is shown on screen); the horses' 15-minute delayed start (W only); the jackpot's growth rate (W says £1,000 a year, the organiser says £500, so they contradict).
- **Huw Lobb:** named in the VO. On screen he's a generic toon runner with no likeness.
