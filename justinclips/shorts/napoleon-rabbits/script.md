# Napoleon Was Defeated by Rabbits (Justin clips Short #11, slug napoleon-rabbits)
Narrator: Kokoro am_fenrir, deadpan storyteller. "## beat" lines = a beat of silence before the punchline.
Legend-flagged: the whole story comes from one general's memoir, so Line 1 says "According to one general".
1. According to one general, rabbits attacked Napoleon.
2. Hundreds of them.
## beat
3. And they won.
4. It's eighteen-oh-seven. Napoleon just signed a peace treaty.
5. To celebrate, his chief of staff sets up a rabbit hunt.
6. Hundreds of rabbits. Some say three thousand.
7. The cages open...
## beat
8. and the rabbits charge. Straight at Napoleon.
9. Wait, why would rabbits charge?
10. You see, they were tame farm rabbits. They thought he was bringing dinner.
11. He swats them with his riding crop. The coachmen crack their whips.
12. More rabbits.
13. Napoleon runs for his carriage. Reportedly, some jump in after him.
## beat
14. The man who conquered Europe... defeated by bunnies.
15. Subscribe for more.
