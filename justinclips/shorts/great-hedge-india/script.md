# The Great Hedge of India — script

**EMOTION:** WTF
**LINE 1:** "Britain built a 2,500-mile wall of thorns. Then forgot it."
**CAPTION (top, 0-3.5 s):** "2,500 miles of thorns"
**Frame 1:** an endless thorn hedge to the horizon, a British officer on horseback tiny beside it, camera already flying along it.
**Target:** ~42 s

## Narration (exact VO)
1. Britain built a 2,500-mile wall of thorns. Then forgot it.
2. British-ruled India, the 1800s. The goal? Taxing salt.
3. To stop salt smugglers, they built a customs line about 2,500 miles long.
4. Hundreds of miles of it was a living hedge of thorny bushes, up to twelve feet high.
5. People compared it to the Great Wall of China.
6. At its peak, in 1872, more than fourteen thousand men guarded it.
7. But here's the twist...
8. In 1879, it was abandoned. And it was forgotten so completely, standard histories skipped it.
9. In the 1990s, a London librarian made three trips to India just to find it.
10. What he found: one small raised embankment. It may be all that's left.

## Tone / accuracy guard
- The salt tax hurt ordinary people: no jokes about the guards or villagers; the WTF is the scale and the forgetting.
- Respectful depiction of Indian villagers/workers; no caricature.
