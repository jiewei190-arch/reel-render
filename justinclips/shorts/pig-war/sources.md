# Sources: the Pig War (San Juan Island, 1859)

Checked Nov 16 2026 (web search + page fetches).

## Every claim, sourced
- **1859, San Juan Island, claimed by both the US and Britain**: National Park Service, San Juan Island NHP, "The Pig War": https://www.nps.gov/sajh/learn/historyculture/the-pig-war.htm ; Wikipedia "Pig War (1859)": https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pig_War_(1859)
- **June 15, 1859: an American settler (Lyman Cutlar) shot a pig owned by the British Hudson's Bay Company (Charles Griffin's pig) that was in his garden**: NPS (the pig was rooting in Cutlar's garden); Wikipedia; HistoryLink: https://www.historylink.org/File/5037 . The VO says "a British company's pig got into an American's garden". The well-known "potatoes" dialogue is called "likely apocryphal" by Wikipedia, so it is not used.
- **He offered $10; the British wanted $100**: Wikipedia ("Cutlar offered Griffin $10 ... Griffin demanded $100").
- **Britain threatened to arrest him, so the US sent soldiers**: Wikipedia ("When British authorities threatened to arrest Cutlar, nearby American settlers called for military protection"). NPS: Capt. George Pickett landed 64 men on July 27 (Wikipedia/HistoryLink say 66), so the VO just says "soldiers".
- **461 American troops (with 14 cannons)**: NPS ("461 Americans, protected by 14 field cannons", by Aug 31, 1859).
- **Five British warships**: Wikipedia ("five British Royal Navy warships ... carrying 2,140 men"); HistoryLink (five warships, 2,140 troops). Gun counts differ between sources (52, 70 or 167), so no gun count is used. On screen: "2,000+ men aboard".
- **Nobody fired a shot; the only casualty was the pig**: Wikipedia ("no human casualties on either side, only a pig"); HistoryLink ("without any casualties except the pig itself"). NPS: Rear Admiral Baynes refused to "involve two great nations in a war over a squabble about a pig."
- **"a 12-year standoff" (top caption)**: NPS (joint military occupation for 12 years); the dispute was settled in 1872 when arbitration under German Kaiser Wilhelm I gave the islands to the US (Wikipedia, HistoryLink).

## Kept hedges
- No exact soldier count for the first landing (64 vs 66), no gun count for the ships.
- The potato dialogue is left out (apocryphal).

## Dramatised (comedy, not claimed as fact)
The pig trotting between the two armies, the farmer's slouch hat and beard, the top-hatted company man, the coin and the $100 sign, the birds bursting from a tree, the seaweed tumbleweed, the pig as an angel. The pig is never shown being shot. All characters are original toons (no likenesses of Cutlar, Griffin, Pickett or Baynes); flags are generic toon flags.
