# Sources — One man hiccuped for 68 years

Each claim is checked against Wikipedia plus Guinness World Records. Quotes are verbatim; the Wikipedia article was read from its
wikitext (action=raw) on 2026-11-28. Claims that only Guinness gives are said as "According to Guinness" or "reportedly".

**W** Wikipedia, "Charles Osborne (farmer)" — https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charles_Osborne_(farmer)
**GN** Guinness World Records news, "Extreme measures person with 68-year attack of hiccups took to try and stop them" (11 July 2023) — https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/news/2023/7/extreme-measures-person-with-68-year-attack-of-hiccups-took-to-try-and-stop-them-754877
**GR** Guinness World Records, "Longest attack of hiccups" — https://www.guinnessworldrecords.com/world-records/67619-longest-attack-of-hiccups

| Line | Claim | Sources (quoted) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | One man hiccuped for 68 years straight | W: "He gained national attention to the American public for hiccuping for over 68 years". GN: "the victim of the longest attack of hiccups, which lasted a jaw-dropping 68 years." |
| 2 | 1922, a farmer, reportedly went down under a 350-pound hog | W: "In 1922, Osborne began hiccuping on a farm near Union, Nebraska, after a 350-pound hog collapsed on top of him". GN: "Charles, then working as a farmer, was trying to weigh a hog that he was planning to slaughter when he fell and started hiccupping back in 1922." (The two accounts differ in detail → "reportedly went down"; the 350-pound figure is W's.) |
| 3 | Then he started hiccuping and did not stop | W and GN as above. GR: "Charles Osborne (USA, 1894-1991) started hiccoughing in 1922 while attempting to weigh a hog before slaughtering it." |
| 4 | According to Guinness, 20 to 40 times a minute, day and night, for decades | GN: "he hiccupped anywhere between 20 and 40 times per minute day and night". (Rate is Guinness only → attributed in the line.) |
| 5 | Doctors tried; nothing lasted; longest break reportedly 50 hours | GN: "Many doctors tried to help Charles"; Osborne: "I made four trips to Omaha. But every time I got back home they would start again."; the longest break was "just 50 hours." GR: "He was unable to find a cure". (Guinness only → "reportedly".) |
| 6 | He lived a normal life; married twice; reportedly eight kids | GR: "led a normal life in which he had two wives and fathered eight children." GN: "He got married twice and fathered eight children, all while hiccupping." W: "Osborne was married twice." (Eight children is Guinness only → "reportedly".) |
| 7 | One doctor's guess: lifting that hog popped a tiny blood vessel in his brain | W: "It was speculated that either an abdomen muscle was pulled or a blood vessel in the brain burst". GN (Dr Terence Anthoney): "My feeling is he popped a small blood vessel at the base of his brain when he was lifting that hog." |
| 8 | Guinness: the longest attack of hiccups; over 400 million hiccups | W: "Guinness World Records listed him as the man with the "longest attack of hiccups""; "estimating over 430 million hiccups throughout his life." GN: "It's said that he hiccupped more than 420 million times in his life." ("Over 400 million" is true under both figures.) |
| 9 | Then, in 1990, after 68 years, they just stopped | W: "His hiccuping lasted for over 68 years and stopped in 1990". GR: "He continued until a morning in February 1990." |
