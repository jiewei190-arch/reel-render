## Hedges kept / wording notes
- **"reportedly"** in line 1 and **"Reported in more than 30 places"** keep the card's reported framing; "an estimated 40 to 100 miles" keeps the article's "estimated".
- **"One February night"**: the article says the night of 8–9 February 1855 "and one or two later nights". The video says the prints were found after one snowy night (the main night) and does not claim the whole trail was made in one night. The card's "One snowy night in 1855" was shortened to fit the 12-word hook.
- **"Straight over houses"**: the article's wording ("travelled straight over"); the video shows the prints on roofs, as the article describes ("on the tops of snow-covered roofs and high walls").
- **Suspects**: said as "Others blamed..." — proposals, not findings. The kangaroo story was later admitted invented by Musgrove; that detail is in this file, not in the 43 s video.
- **"Satan himself"**: attributed ("Some people said"), as in the article.

## Dramatised (cartoon, not claimed as fact)
The cottages, villagers and vicar, the lantern scene, the ruler, the police-style lineup, the map (stylised, not to scale; dots illustrate "30+ places" and are not plotted locations), the dashed trail line on the map, and the newspaper (an invented generic "Weekly Gazette", no real masthead).
