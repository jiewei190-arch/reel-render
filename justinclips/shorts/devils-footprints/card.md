An Estimated 100 Miles of Hoofprints Nobody Could
 Explain
 TARGET EMOTION: WTF / mystery
 3-SECOND HOOK (frame 1)
 Frame 1: a line of small hoofprints in fresh snow going straight up and over a house. Caption:
 "1855". First line: "One snowy night in 1855, something reportedly walked an estimated 40 to 100
 miles across Devon, straight over houses and rivers, and left only hoofprints."

 THE VERIFIED PAYOFF
 On the night of February 8-9, 1855, after a heavy snowfall, trails of hoof-like prints were reported
 across Devon, England, estimated at 40 to 100 miles in total and crossing more than 30 locations.
 The prints ran in single file, about 4 inches long, and were said to go straight over houses, walls,
 haystacks and frozen rivers. Explanations from escaped animals to weather balloons have been
 proposed for 170 years; none has ever been confirmed, and parts of the original reports have been
 questioned.
