## Narration (exact VO, 122 words)
1. In 1855, something reportedly walked over houses and left only hoofprints.   *(hook; "reportedly" = the card's framing)*
2. Devon, England. One February night, after heavy snow.
3. By morning, hoof-like prints ran through the snow, mostly in single file.
4. Each about 4 inches long. Reported in more than 30 places.
5. But here's the part... the trail went straight over houses, walls and haystacks.   *(the "But..." turn)*
6. Over rivers. Across snowy rooftops. Even in and out of drainpipes.
7. In total: an estimated 40 to 100 miles of tracks.
8. Some people said they were the tracks of Satan himself.
9. Others blamed badgers, wood mice, escaped kangaroos... even a runaway balloon.
10. But none of it has ever been confirmed. And some reports have been questioned.   *(second "But...")*
11. Over 170 years later, nobody knows what made the Devil's Footprints.   *(payoff, last ~2 s → loop)*

Loop: the payoff shot is the frame-1 cottage; the camera settles back on frame 1's exact framing while fresh snow starts to soften the prints, so the cut back to frame 1 (prints appearing over the roof) loops.
