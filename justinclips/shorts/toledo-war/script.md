## Narration (exact VO, 125 words)
1. Ohio and Michigan once nearly went to war... over Toledo.
2. Blame a bad map. Both claimed one strip of land, 5 to 8 miles wide.
3. In 1835, both sent militias to the Maumee River... and traded taunts.
4. The one battle? Reportedly, shots fired into the air. Nobody was hit.
5. But here's the part... the war's only injury: a deputy, hurt with a penknife.
6. By a man named Two Stickney. His brother? Reportedly named One.
7. Congress stepped in. Ohio got Toledo. Michigan got statehood... and the Upper Peninsula.
8. A consolation prize almost everyone called a worthless wilderness.
9. But in the 1840s, they found copper and iron. By the 1860s, it supplied 90% of America's copper.
10. Michigan lost Toledo... and won the jackpot.
11. Subscribe for more.

Mystery → turn → reveal: "nearly went to war" → "But here's the part..." (the war's only injury) → "But in the 1840s..." (copper) → payoff "won the jackpot".

## Visual gags (each tied to a fact)
1. Staring contest over a tiny TOLEDO strip (the dispute over a 5–8-mile-wide strip).
2. The "bad map": Lake Michigan slides to the wrong place, "BAD MAP" + "?" (the Mitchell Map put the lake's tip too far north).
3. Both riverbanks jump about yelling "NYAH!" bubbles (Wikipedia: "little interaction ... apart from mutual taunting").
4. One militiaman fires straight up: POP puffs, everyone looks up "???", then a scoreboard: BATTLES 1 / HITS 0.
5. The scoreboard adds INJURIES 1 + a penknife icon + "NOT SERIOUS" (nobody is shown hurt).
6. The brothers hold up big number cards "2" and "1" (Two Stickney / One Stickney).
7. Ohio hugs the sign with heart eyes; Michigan gets a blue "UP" ribbon rosette: CONSOLATION PRIZE; then shivers alone in the snow: WORTHLESS?
8. A pickaxe cracks a rock: glowing COPPER + IRON; counter card 90%.
9. Payoff: Michigan grins on a copper mountain, Ohio (still hugging his sign) double-takes: JACKPOT.
