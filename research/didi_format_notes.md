## Shot lists (beats, not their drawings)
**Midnight diner (51 s):**
- 0–4.8 s: wide, dark dorm at night, everyone asleep in the bunks.
- One character sneaks to a tiny hot plate; ingredients appear.
- Step-by-step cooking close-ups, about 10 shots: stirring, pouring, a top-down view of the bubbling pot.
- The payoff: eating happily and sipping a can.
- A friend peeks in.
- Last ~3 s: wide shot of the room, now glowing warm.

**Good roommate (24 s):**
- A wide, lit dorm, with each roommate busy on a quick 1–2 s close-up.
- One gets up and turns off the light; the cuts get very fast (~0.5 s) here.
- A surprised face.
- Little glowing lights float up.
- Wide, dark dorm, everyone tucked in, with soft glows.

**Roommate green flags (30.5 s):**
- Wide shot of the bunk room with a lamp on.
- One close-up per roommate, each doing a tiny cozy thing: sleeping, headphones + phone, a snack, reading.
- Wide again with the lights off.

**Always comes through (38 s):**
- A rainy night; one character sleeps badly.
- The others notice; a run of several 1–1.5 s cuts as they quietly fix things.
- Last ~4 s: a bright, warm group-hug shot.

## Patterns shared by the top reels
1. **Length 24–51 s.** Shots change every ~2–4 s, and faster (0.2–1.5 s) at the emotional turn.
2. **Hook in the first 2 s:** an establishing wide of a cozy, slightly dark room, already in motion, plus a **POV / relatable caption at the top** from frame 1, e.g. "When…", "POV:". No voice-over is needed to get it.
3. **Caption style:**
   - One line of white text with a dark outline, in the top ~8–12 % of the frame.
   - It stays on screen for the whole reel; there are no per-word subtitles.
   - Small and centred. Some reels add a second-language line above it.
4. **Story shape:** wide room → close-ups of one small cozy action → small problem or craving → a kind or comforting payoff → wide room again.
5. **Loop:** the last shot returns to the opening wide, often with the lighting changed (lit → dark, or dark → warm glow), so the replay looks seamless.
6. **Emotion:** always AW / "so true" (roommates, comfort food, small kindnesses). Never edgy.
7. **Look:** soft pastel 2D chibi animals, thick soft outlines, warm lamp light against cool night blue, light rays and glow particles.
8. **Motion is small.** Characters sway, blink, stir or pour. Most of the life comes from cutting and lighting, not big animation.
9. **Audio:** I couldn't judge this (I watched muted). From the visuals, expect a soft lo-fi or music-box bed plus close-up sfx (sizzle, pour, sip). The owner should check this with the sound on.
10. **Cadence:** about **1 reel a day**, sometimes 2: Nov 9–13 daily, Oct 30–Nov 1 daily, with small gaps. Captions are short and relatable; hashtags are minimal (#animation #friends #dorm).

## Template for our bear series (our own art, Didi's format)
| Time | Beat |

| 0–2 s | Wide of the owner's room at night, the bear already moving + one top caption ("When the midnight cravings hit…"-style line in our own words) |

| Last 3 s | Back to the opening wide with the lighting changed, for the loop |
