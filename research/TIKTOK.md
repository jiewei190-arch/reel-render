# TIKTOK: how the For You feed works, and what we change (researched Oct 28 2026, worker H2)

Scope: how TikTok ranks and spreads videos, applied to OUR account: animated 66-86 s explainers, 5 a day from Nov 1, episode-badged `_ep.mp4` copies.
Labels used below: **[OFFICIAL]** = TikTok's own pages (Newsroom, Community Guidelines, Creator Rewards announcement), quoted.
**[UNOFFICIAL]** = third-party guides, studies or creator reports. These are useful but unverified. **No numbers here are invented.** Where TikTok gives no number, we say so.

---

## 1. How it works, in plain words

**What the feed looks at [OFFICIAL]**
- TikTok's newsroom lists three groups of signals:
  - user interactions: "videos you like or share, accounts you follow, comments you post, and content you create";
  - video information: "captions, sounds, and hashtags";
  - device and account settings: language, country, device type.
- Settings carry "lower weight" than the other signals.
- The Community Guidelines (FYF standards, current version effective Sep 24 2026) add that the system looks at "what you like, share, comment on, and search for, as well as what's trending."

**Watching to the end is a strong signal [OFFICIAL]**
- Signals are weighted. TikTok's own example of a strong one: "whether a user finishes watching a longer video from beginning to end."
- The Creator Rewards formula scores "play duration", which "accounts for both watch time and finish rate".
- Completion is the number we most control, through hooks, pacing and the loop ending.

**Followers don't directly decide reach [OFFICIAL]**
- "Neither follower count nor whether the account has had previous high-performing videos are direct factors."
- A new account with zero followers can still reach the For You feed (FYF), video by video.

**Diversity on purpose [OFFICIAL]**
- Diversity is "an important and intentional component".
- The feed avoids showing the same kind of video over and over. Duplicated content, content a user has already seen, and spam are not recommended.
- Viewers can tap "Not Interested" (long-press), which steers their feed away from a video like ours.

**What gets left out of the FYF or limited [OFFICIAL]**
- Content "not suitable for a broad audience" (see the "FYF Ineligible" sections of the Community Guidelines). Examples: graphic medical procedures, regulated goods.
- Videos "just uploaded or under review".
- **Unoriginal material:** "Content is also ineligible for the FYF if it includes unoriginal or reused material without anything new" (Integrity & Authenticity section).
- **Unlabelled realistic AI:** TikTok "require[s] creators to label AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people. Unlabeled content may be removed, restricted, or labeled by our team."
- **Unverified crisis claims:** "unverified information about crises, major civic events, or content temporarily under review by fact-checkers is also ineligible for the FYF."
- TikTok also says: "If a video isn't getting many views, it also doesn't necessarily mean it broke a rule." Analytics show whether a video was made ineligible for recommendation.

**Search is a second front door [OFFICIAL]**
- Videos left out of the FYF "may still [be found] through search or by going to a creator's account."
- The Creator Rewards formula scores "search value": content "based on popular search terms."
- TikTok points creators to the **Creator Search Insights** tool to find popular search topics.

**Long videos are welcome [OFFICIAL]**
- TikTok says users spend "50% of their time on TikTok watching videos longer than one minute" (Mar 2024).
- Creator Rewards pays only for "high-quality, original content that is over a minute long."
- TikTok also says the formula rewards "content that is clear, and engaging, rather than favoring accounts with an excessive amount of videos". **Volume alone is not rewarded.**

**Creator Rewards rules [OFFICIAL unless marked]**

| Rule | Detail |
|---|---|
| Eligibility | 18+, at least 10K followers, at least 100K views in the last 30 days, personal account in good standing, in an eligible country |
| What earns | Videos over 1 minute |
| How it's scored | Four metrics: **originality** ("quality content unique to the creator, showcasing their point of view or creative thought process"), **play duration**, **search value**, **audience engagement** ("likes, comments and shares"); plus the account's ad value |
| Qualified view [UNOFFICIAL] | Several guides say: For You feed views only, watched 5 s or more, unique, not paid, promoted or fraudulent. TikTok's own definition page could not be loaded here (it is script-rendered). Treat 5 s as unconfirmed. |
| Not eligible [UNOFFICIAL] | Ads and sponsored posts, Duets and Stitches, Photo Mode, watermarked reposts |
| AI content [UNOFFICIAL] | One blog says fully AI-generated content is excluded. I found no official text saying this; open question (see action 10). |

**How a new video spreads [UNOFFICIAL]**
- Creators and agencies widely describe "first-batch testing": a new video is shown to a small group, and if completion and engagement are good, it is pushed to bigger groups in waves.
- **TikTok has not published this mechanism or any view threshold.** Treat it as a working model, not a rule.
- It agrees with the official points above: per-video judgement, completion matters, followers aren't a direct factor.

**Hashtags and sounds**
- [OFFICIAL] Hashtags, captions and sounds are part of the "video information" the system reads.
- [UNOFFICIAL] Common advice: a few tightly relevant hashtags beat long lists. TikTok reads on-screen text and transcribes speech, so caption, on-screen words and voice should agree.
- [UNOFFICIAL] Business accounts can only pick in-app sounds from the Commercial Music Library. Audio already baked into an uploaded file is not affected.
  - Our reels carry our own voice and music, so we post as **original sound**.
  - Adding a trending song under a voice-over hurts clarity and breaks the mix rule (HOOKS/rule 12). Not recommended.

**Posting frequency and time [UNOFFICIAL]**
- TikTok's old guidance is often quoted as "1-4 posts a day".
- A Buffer study of 11.4M posts is reported to show the biggest gains going from 1 a week to 2-5 a week, with diminishing returns after.
- Best times: studies disagree (morning 6-10 AM and evening 6-9 PM are common). Every guide says to use **your own follower-activity chart** in TikTok Analytics (Followers tab).
- [OFFICIAL] reminder: the rewards formula does not favour "an excessive amount of videos".

**Safe zone (where TikTok's UI covers the video) [UNOFFICIAL, approximate]**
- One site (Socialync) quotes TikTok's In-Feed ads template, scaled to 1080x1920:
  - about **240 px at the top** (Following / For You tabs);
  - about **480-660 px at the bottom** (username, caption, sound bar);
  - about **120 px on the sides**;
  - a **180 px button column** on the right from about y 840 down.
- Other checkers give smaller margins.
- **This matters for us:** our burned-in captions sit at y ≈ 1650-1800, and some badges at y 196. Both fall in zones TikTok's UI can cover (see action 4).

## 2. The 10 most important actions for OUR account

Each action is tied to a concrete change. Owner rules (HANDOFF, GROWTH.md) win where they conflict.

**1. Win the first 3 seconds, then hold to the end.**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: finishing a video is the strong signal, and play duration is a rewards metric.
- Change: keep HOOKS.md as is (12-word first line, subject moving at frame 0, 3-6 word caption, "but" by 5 s).
- Add a **mid-video re-hook check** to QA at about 25 s and 50 s. HOOKS rule 6 already says this; make it a ticked box in the NOTES checklist.
- Cut any beat over 6 s with no new visual (STYLE_FIT REJECTED: static repeats).

**2. Make the ending loop.**
- Why: completion plus rewatches. [UNOFFICIAL] rewatches are widely reported to help.
- Change: GROWTH.md §2 loop ending, enforced. The last 0.5 s dissolves toward the frame-0 image, and the question before the closing line echoes the hook.
- Do not fade to black on the TikTok copy. A black last frame breaks the loop.
  - Today faces and lock (and others built on that pattern) fade fully to black in the last ~0.4 s.
  - zombieant dims 55% and spidersilk 45%.
  - Make "fade no more than 50%" a template rule.

**3. Keep the length at 66-86 s, never under 61 s.**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: rewards pay only for videos over a minute, and long videos are half of watch time.
- Change: no short cuts for TikTok. Our closing line plus end card already keeps every reel over 1 minute.

**4. Move text into TikTok's safe zone on the `_ep` copy.**
- Why [UNOFFICIAL, approx]: TikTok's bottom UI covers roughly the lower 480-660 px, and its top tabs about 240 px.
- Change:
  - `brand.py`: restrict SPOTS to y 250 or more, and keep the badge out of the right button column.
  - QA should flag any `_ep` badge above y 240. Today match is at y 196, magnet at y 196 and ship at y 196 (sitting under the top tabs).
  - For new reels, place the burned-in caption band at about y 1150-1400 on the TikTok copy, or accept that TikTok's own caption covers it and rely on TikTok's auto-captions.
  - Simplest pipeline fix: a `cap_y` option in the style modules so `_ep` renders can lift captions. Raise this with the owner before changing templates.

**5. Treat every video as a search answer (TikTok SEO).**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: search value is a rewards metric, and search reaches FYF-ineligible videos too.
- Change:
  - Topic = a question people type (GROWTH.md §3).
  - **Say the exact search phrase in the first spoken sentence or two, and show it in the on-screen caption**, e.g. "why do cats' eyes glow".
  - Before scripting, check TikTok's search autocomplete and **Creator Search Insights** for the phrase, and log it in NOTES.md.

**6. Write the TikTok caption for search, not just for style.**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: captions and hashtags are video information.
- Change: new TikTok caption template (it differs from the Instagram one):
  - "Ep. nn · " (rule 15);
  - the search question, word for word, as the first line;
  - one plain-sentence answer teaser;
  - "Sources: ..." (short);
  - the closing line;
  - 3-5 relevant hashtags: topic, science, howitworks, plus a seasonal one (#halloween). No #fyp.
- Add `caption_tt_<n>.md` per reel, or a section in `caption_<n>.md`.

**7. Ask for the engagement TikTok counts: shares, comments, saves.**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: likes, comments and shares are engagement signals.
- Change:
  - GROWTH.md §4 one-word-answer question before the closing line (comments).
  - The pinned comment (`pinned_<n>.md`) gives a bonus fact that rewards a share ("send this to someone who...").
  - Do **not** say "share this" in the voice-over itself; keep the closing line exact.

**8. Stay clearly original.**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: unoriginal or reused material is FYF-ineligible, and originality is a rewards metric.
- Change:
  - Upload only our own masters (no re-encodes of downloads, no other platform's watermark).
  - The episode badge is our own mark, so it's fine.
  - Never upload the Instagram-downloaded file. Always upload `jobs/<n>/<n>_ep.mp4` straight from the repo.
  - Don't post the same reel twice on TikTok. Duplicates are not recommended.

**9. Post at a sustainable pace, read our own clock, and don't flood.**
- Why: [OFFICIAL] volume alone isn't rewarded. [UNOFFICIAL] gains taper after a few posts a day.
- Change:
  - Keep the 5-a-day plan (owner, GROWTH §6) but spread the slots (as planned).
  - After 2 weeks, retune the slots to the Followers → activity chart.
  - **Kill switch:** if average views per post fall for 2 weeks while volume rises, tell the owner and test 3 a day.

**10. AI disclosure.**
- Why [OFFICIAL]: the label is required for AI or edited content showing *realistic-looking* scenes or people.
- Our case:
  - Our visuals are stylised animation, so the rule as written does not require a label.
  - The voice is synthetic (Kokoro TTS) but not imitating a real person.
- Change:
  - Note "animated, not realistic; synthetic narrator voice" in NOTES.md.
  - **Ask the owner** whether to switch TikTok's "AI-generated content" toggle on anyway. Being transparent is low-risk for reach under the written rule.
  - Watch for official Creator Rewards text on AI before the account reaches 10K followers. I found none, only a third-party claim.

## 3. Metrics to watch (TikTok Studio → Analytics; read on the owner's PC with their OK)

There are no official benchmarks; "good" below is relative to our own median.

| Metric | Where | What good looks like | What bad looks like / action |
|---|---|---|---|
| **% watched / average watch time** | Video → Overview / Retention | Rising over time. Above our median per template. [UNOFFICIAL] guides call 25%+ finish on 60 s videos strong. | Retention curve drops in the first 3 s: fix the hook. Cliff mid-video: cut the static beat at that timestamp. |
| **Watched full video** (finish rate) | Video → Retention | Steady or rising as hooks improve | Low with high early retention: the middle drags or the reel is too long for the topic |
| **Retention curve shape** | Video → Retention | Shallow slope, small bump at the end (loop or rewatch) | Steep drop at 0-3 s means the hook failed. Drop at the episode badge (5 s) means the badge distracts, so test without it. |
| **Traffic source** | Video → Overview | Mostly For You; Search share growing on search-titled topics | ~0% For You for a new video could mean "ineligible for recommendation". Check the eligibility note in analytics. |
| **Search queries / search share** | Video → Viewers (search terms, where shown) | Our target phrase appears | It doesn't: rewrite caption and on-screen phrase on future reels |
| **Shares, saves, comments per 1K views** | Video → Engagement | Above our median. Shares are the best sign of "send this to a friend". | Likes only, no shares: the payoff isn't surprising enough |
| **New vs returning viewers; follows per video** | Video / Followers | Follows on the episode-badged series growing | Views without follows: strengthen the end card line ("A new 'wait, really?' every day.") |
| **Qualified views / RPM** (once in Creator Rewards) | Creator Rewards dashboard | Qualified views ≈ most FYF views | Big gap means short views (<5 s, unofficial) or non-eligible regions |
| **Views per post vs posts per day** | Weekly log (handoff/metrics.csv) | Flat or rising | Falling while volume rises: flag to the owner (action 9) |

Log weekly per GROWTH.md §6. Add a "TikTok" block per reel to STYLE_FIT's Performance column: % watched, finish %, shares per 1K, search share, follows.
