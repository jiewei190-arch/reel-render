# Sources: What If You Fell Into a Black Hole? (checked Nov 8, 2026)

Legal note (same "safe version" approach as eps 3-6): an original story and original characters (the mustard-hoodie hero, Dev, Mina, Bolt the toy robot, the ship "the Pebble"). No film, show, game or comic is named, copied or imitated; the black hole art is drawn from the published science (a dark shadow, a glowing ring, bent starlight), not from any film's design. Real people and teams appear only as plain on-screen text (Einstein; "NASA Goddard"; "EHT"; "NIST"), never as a face or figure.

Thought-experiment framing: no one can travel 1,560 or 27,000 light-years, no one has fallen into a black hole, and a person in a suit could not survive the trip; the script says so (line 59). The story beats (the ship, Bolt, Dev's and Mina's lines, letting go of the ship, the wave, the photo/window, "still waving") are the hero's story, not claims. Everything the narrator states as fact is below.

## Dropped or softened
- **"Space and time swap roles" (line 50):** it is the common teaching picture of the Schwarzschild interior (the radial coordinate becomes timelike). Some physicists stress it depends on the coordinates chosen; the physical content, that every future path inside leads to the centre, is not in doubt. Kept as the simplified wording.
- **12.8 seconds:** it is for NASA's simulated 4.3-million-solar-mass black hole and that trajectory; said with "about" and "NASA's simulation says".
- **"Sixty kilometres wide":** non-spinning (Schwarzschild) size for 9.6 M_sun; a spinning black hole's horizon is smaller. Rounded.
- **"Over three hundred times Earth's gravity":** a worked estimate for a 1.5 m robot 1,000 km out (360 g). A human-sized body would be similar.
- **"Less than a ten-thousandth of Earth's gravity":** worked estimate at the horizon of a non-spinning 4.3 M M_sun black hole.
- **"Light-years ... from Earth" for Sgr A*:** ~26,000-27,000 ly depending on the measurement (GRAVITY 2019: ~8.18 kpc ≈ 26,700 ly). "Twenty-seven thousand" matches the EHT 2022 release.
- **Gaia BH1 "closest we know of":** true at the check date; a closer one may be found later, hence "we know of".
- **Freezing "forever":** the cold open says "to them, you've stopped moving"; the image does not hang there visibly forever, it fades out (line 43). The script avoids claiming they'd watch forever.
- **Seeing the stars inside (line 57):** the view is strongly distorted, blue/red-shifted and concentrated; the script says only that you can look back and see the stars.
- **Survival:** radiation from the glowing gas, the trip length and the suit are ignored (story rule); stated as a thought experiment in line 59 and the header.
- **Cut for length:** 215 million light-years distance of AT2019qiz; Hawking radiation (black holes slowly evaporate; Hawking 1974) as a possible ending, cut so the ending stays on the "centre is a time" reveal; "drive across it in half an hour" (60 km at ~120 km/h).
