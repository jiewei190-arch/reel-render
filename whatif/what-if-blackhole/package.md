## The promise the first 30 s must deliver
The thumbnail promises: a black hole, your face in shock, and either "12.8 SECONDS", "SPAGHETTI?" or "FROZEN?". The cold open (0-20 s) says and shows all three:
- frame 1 = the episode's most dramatic image (flash-forward to line 1/3): the hero in a bubble helmet and mustard hoodie, just past a huge black hole's edge, glowing ring behind, calm; cut to the ship window where Dev and Mina see the hero frozen and red;
- "A smaller one would have stretched you like spaghetti" = a 1 s flash of Bolt stretched into a noodle (the spaghetti promise);
- "twelve point eight seconds" = a gold 12.8 s countdown chip (the 12.8 promise);
- "you've stopped moving, and you're turning red" = the frozen red hero (the FROZEN promise).
