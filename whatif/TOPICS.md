# What If channel: topic list (Oct 30)

Format that worked for ep 1: a superhuman sense or ability, 5 "rules" that teach real science, a small cast (hero, sister Mina, friends, Biscuit the cat), and a rescue or climax where the rule we learned saves the day. About 6-7 min, the locked voice (whatif/VOICE.md), and honest about what's impossible.

| # | What if you could... | The catch / story engine | Real science it teaches | Visual hook |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **hear everything within a mile?** | Silence is gone: heartbeats, fridges, whispers through walls. Mina is lost in a crowded stadium and you have to find one heartbeat. | Cochlea and hair cells, decibels, why loud sound damages hearing, how owls and bats locate sound, noise-cancelling | Sound waves drawn as colour ripples; a "hearing view" like thermal view |
| 2 | **stop time for 10 seconds?** | Air turns to syrup, light stops reaching your eyes, you can't push a door. | Inertia, why stopped light would blind you, air resistance, momentum | Frozen raindrops, people mid-sneeze, a coffee splash hanging in the air |
| 3 | **never sleep again?** | Day 1 is great; by day 3 the brain starts glitching. | What sleep does: memory, the glymphatic "rinse", microsleeps, the Randy Gardner record | A day/night counter; the world starting to "lag" |
| 4 | **smell like a dog?** | Smell 10,000x better: you can smell fear, rain coming, who sat here yesterday. | Olfactory receptors, scent trails over time, how sniffer dogs work | Scent trails as coloured smoke ribbons |
| 5 | **breathe underwater?** | Gills need a lot of water flow; cold steals heat; pressure at depth. | Dissolved oxygen, why fish gills work, pressure, the bends | Ocean layers getting darker; a "depth gauge" rule card |
| 6 | **shrink to the size of an ant?** | Water becomes sticky blobs, you could survive a fall, your voice gets squeaky, you lose heat fast. | Surface tension, square-cube law, terminal velocity | Raindrops the size of boulders; a sugar cube like a house |
| 7 | **see ultraviolet like a bee?** | Flowers have secret landing strips, sunscreen looks like black paint. | The light spectrum, how bees and birds see, UV and skin | UV view like thermal view: glowing flower patterns |
| 8 | **feel no pain?** | Sounds like a superpower until you don't notice a burn or a broken bone. | Nociceptors, why pain protects you, the real condition (CIPA) | Pain signals as alarm lights along nerves |
| 9 | **remember everything forever?** | You can't forget an embarrassing moment or a bad day; your brain overflows. | How memory forms and fades, why forgetting is useful, HSAM | A "memory library" that never stops growing |
| 10 | **run at 100 mph?** | Friction burns your shoes, bugs hit like bullets, you can't stop or turn. | Friction, air drag, reaction time, why cheetahs sprint only briefly | Speed lines, a speedometer rule card |
| 11 | **live without gravity for a day?** | Everything not bolted down floats away, including the ocean and the air. | Gravity, the atmosphere, why astronauts' muscles weaken | Floating cars and cats; the sky peeling away |
| 12 | **talk to animals?** | Biscuit the cat finally tells you what she thinks of you. | Animal communication: bee dances, whale song, prairie dog "words" | Speech bubbles from every animal (great comedy for the cast) |

## Pick for the next three
1. **Hear everything within a mile:** the closest twin to ep 1 (a sense, a "view" mode, a rescue), so the kit, sets and cast reuse well.
2. **Talk to animals:** the most fun for the cast (Biscuit gets lines), very shareable.
3. **Stop time for 10 seconds:** a huge, popular premise with strong visuals.
