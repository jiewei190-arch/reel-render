## Facts tried and left out (not verified)
- Owl eyes (tubular, fixed in sockets, head turns up to 270 degrees): only low-quality pages surfaced; no authoritative source.
- "Eyes cannot see infrared because of water in the eye": no source supported the water-absorption framing.
- Pit organ sensitivity of 0.003 or 0.001 degrees Celsius: sources disagree and 0.003 is partly an apparatus limit.
- Cat tapetum "fifty percent more light": single pet-site claim.
- Exact detection rate in Tinsley et al.: not confirmed.
- Mantis shrimp: not researched.
