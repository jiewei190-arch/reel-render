## Description
You wake in a blackout and realise you can see everything. Not because of light, but because everything warm is glowing. This animated What If follows five rules of life with heat vision: why eyes fail in the dark, how thermal infrared works, why heat has no colour, why bright light becomes the enemy, and what happens when the person you must find is cold. We compare it to real animals and technology, and we are honest about what is impossible.

Real science in this video: the rod and cone count of the human retina and dark adaptation taking about twenty to thirty minutes (Scientific American; AMS Glossary); single-photon detection by humans (Tinsley et al., Nature Communications, 2016); pit vipers sensing heat with the TRPA1 channel (Gracheva et al., Nature, 2010); colour vision at moonlight levels in helmet geckos (Roth and Kelber, Proc.
