## Hook
"The whole street goes dark at once, and you just keep walking. Round the coffee table, over the shoe, past the door that always eats your knee."
Then Mina: "How are you DOING this?" The question is raised in the first 15 seconds, and line 4 promises the payoff: five rules, and the fifth decides whether you find her.

## What changed and why
- Fragments merged into spoken sentences with connectors ("so", "which sounds fine, until", "easy: nothing has to shine"). Contractions throughout.
- Rule titles are now said in a natural way ("Rule one. Before any new powers, your old eyes quit first.", "Which brings us to rule five.") instead of announced headings.
- Jokes sharpened: the cat "like you've insulted her", the torch-in-the-face analogy kept, the snake/remote line, the window "ten out of ten", the menu line split so it lands alone.
- Mina hand-warmer plant (line 20) now has "Hold on to that." Light foreshadowing in line 28 ("You're about to need that"). Payoff in lines 34-38.
- Climax (lines 29-38) re-paced: short lines 34-35 give space for the wait, the spark gets a build, and line 38 closes with Mina's joke after a quiet moment.
- Kept every real example: rods and cones, dark adaptation, cat, single photons, infrared, pit viper and TRPA1, glass, night-vision goggles, helmet geckos.
- Each rule still has story, science, an animal or tech example, and an upside/downside where it fits (rule 1 and 3 explicit; rule 2 is the window, rule 4 the blindness).
- Only two CAPS words: DOING, SEE.
- "One photon. One." replaced with a plain statement. "Photons" is used once and defined in the same sentence.

## Fact hedges kept
All figures and hedges stay within sources.md: "about" for rods, cones and 9.5 micrometres; "roughly a sixth" for cats; "a little better than chance" for the 2016 photon study; "one reason" for warm eyes; "about moonlight levels" for geckos.
