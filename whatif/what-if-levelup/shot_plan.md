# Shot plan: What If You Could Level Up Your Strength Every Day? (animation directions)

Same classic kit and quality bar as eps 1-4 (aitrends-2d-studio, QUALITY_BAR.md; owner decision Oct 31: the rig5/town5 art upgrade is shelved): **nothing static**. Every shot has a moving camera, characters acting on the word beats, atmosphere and parallax. Timing comes from the recorded voice (Fenrir, 418.97 s; `timeline/words.json`, onsets corrected from the audio). The full shot list is `projects/level-up/story/shots.json` v1: **135 shots**, cut 0.15 s before a spoken word, batches A-E (see the project HANDOFF, Stage B).

**Legal (every shot, STYLE.md DON'T list):** a homage to the feel of leveling anime, never a copy. No series, character or place names, no catchphrases, no "System / Player / Quest / Hunter / Rank / Arise" or letter ranks on screen, no gates, portals, statues or shadow army, no copied blue window frame or square brackets, no long dark coat, hood, gloves or daggers, and the hero's eyes never glow. Our own Stat Card, our own Zip, our own music.

## Recurring devices (build once, reuse)
- **The Zip** (`statcard.js zip`): a cereal-bowl-wide zipper tear in the air with gold teeth and sparks; only ever releases the Stat Card, then zips shut. Opens over the cereal every morning; a tiny one opens over Biscuit at the end.
- **The Stat Card** (`statCard`, `morningPop`): rounded glass card, gold level badge, cyan dumbbell + STR in kg, chevron XP bar. STR = 40 x 2^(level/3): LV 1 50 kg, LV 6 160, LV 11 508, LV 12 640, LV 16 1,613, LV 19 3,225, LV 23 8,127, LV 30 40,960. Coral one-liners only from the script ("Bones: not included.", "GRIP: LV 1"), "Bones: upgraded." in gold, the back side "Trial over. Levels returned.", then STR 44.
- **The level-up recipe** (every morning): breakfast hush, the Zip on the cut, card unfolds, bar snaps, badge flips, freeze + white-gold flash + 3 px shake, gold "LEVEL UP!" snap at 1.2 s, then the comic payoff (bent spoon, snapped cup handle, Biscuit). Bigger every time: slow-mo push-ins from level 16, the biggest pop on level 30.
- **Chapter chips** (hud chip, gold): LEVEL 1: THE BAGS, LEVEL 6: THE SOFA, LEVEL 11: THE RECORD, LEVEL 16: THE CAR, LEVEL 19: NIGHT TRAINING, LEVEL 23: THE TRUCK, LEVEL 30: THE LAST MORNING.
- **fx5**: low angles up at every lift, speed lines in cyan/gold (never across a person), slow-mo + streaks on the car lift, soil sink + ground crack on the lawn, tile crack on the patio, the steam aura with bloom + ink-navy night grade in the park.
- **Blueprints** (bp board, cream on navy, gold numbers): one per science explanation (the rule on the card; the wiring + 1979; the record; tendon/bone + 2 tonnes + the race + the gap; Newton 3 + pressure + 5x + planks; food -> 25% movement / heat -> steam + the apple bite + calories; friction + μ + 50 kg; how strength really grows; square-cube).
- **Rule cards:** hud ruleCard over a dimmed version of the current set, exact titles from the script.
- **Giant faces** on the reaction beats (7, never two in a row). Dev's four quoted lines are the only lip-sync.

## Sound
Playful for the cold open, title and level 1; wonder under each science block; hype (action) on every level-up morning and the big lifts; tension for "Bones: not included", the sinking car and the stuck truck; epic for the night training and the steam aura; hopeful building through the rescue into triumph as the truck crawls out; action again for the last morning; a calm drop for "Trial over"; warm hopeful for the 44 kg twist, Biscuit's level one and the ending. SFX: the Zip "zzzip" (swish + blip), the level-up ka-chunk + rising three-note arpeggio + powerup + flash (never a single ding), stinger on each rule card, low whooshes and slow-mo for lifts, hit + swish for sinking, hit + impact for the tile crack, rubber blip squeaks, rain + hit for the puddle, wind + shimmer for steam, counter on every count. All original; no sound-alikes.
