# Shot plan: What If Every Day, Your Jump Doubled? (animation directions)

Same kit and quality bar as ep 1 (aitrends-2d-studio, QUALITY_BAR.md): **nothing static**. Every shot has a moving camera, characters acting on the word beats, atmosphere and parallax. Final shot timing comes from the recorded voice (transcribe.py + beats.py), so treat shot counts as targets. About 105-120 shots.

## Recurring devices (build once, reuse)
- **DAY counter:** a split-flap sign ("DAY 01") that flips on each "Day ___" line with a tick SFX. It opens and closes the video (loop).
- **Height meter:** a vertical gauge on the right edge (inside the safe area) with the jump height in gold. It rises with the jump and shows landmarks as tick marks (hoop, high-jump bar, roof, Niagara, Burj, Everest, jets, 100 km line, ISS).
- **Jump grammar:** crouch (anticipation, 0.2 s), launch (squash and stretch plus a dust ring), rise (motion trail, camera tilts up with a slight lag), apex (hang time, everything slows, hoodie flutters), fall (speed lines, wind), landing (impact frame, shake, dust/splash, a reaction).
- **Rule cards:** ruleCard (as ep 1) over a dimmed version of the current set.
- **Giant faces** on every reaction beat (about 1 per 8 shots).

A light playful score for days 1-5, building tension from day 6, awe and near-silence in space, a heartbeat and swell for the last jump. SFX on every launch (whoosh), landing (thud/splash/boing), counter flip (tick), dunk (swish and rim rattle).
