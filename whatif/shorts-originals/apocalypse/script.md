# What If You Were in an Apocalypse With Zero Experience? (Short, script v1, Oct 30)

Format: standalone YouTube Short, vertical 9:16, under 80 s. Narrator: the locked What If voice (whatif/VOICE.md: Kokoro am_fenrir, speed 0.95), second person, calm, warm, funny. Captions ON (burned in, 3 words at a time). 168 spoken words (~67 s at 150 wpm, ~75 s with pauses).

Cast: HERO ("you"), MINA (little sister, red scarf), DEV (best friend, orange beanie), BISCUIT (the cat).

Look: a quiet, overgrown hometown after "the power's been out for weeks and everyone left town". Weeds through the sidewalk, a supermarket with empty shelves, a backyard camp with a tarp roof. Comedy, not horror: no zombies, no monsters, no gore, no violence, no weapons. Original world, no nods to any existing show, film or game. Any boiling shot shows a camp stove with an adult nearby, never a kid alone at a flame.

## HOOK
1. The world ends on a Tuesday. Your only survival skill is... ordering takeout.

## BEAT 1: THE SNACK RUN
2. The power's been out for weeks. Everyone left town.
3. You stock up on what's left: forty bags of chips.
4. Mina shakes an empty bottle. "We're out of water."

## BEAT 2: THE PUDDLE
5. You find a puddle. Dev just says, "No."
6. Biscuit won't even sniff it.

## BEAT 3: THE BACKYARD
7. Night one, you camp outside. It rains.
8. Soaked. Shivering. On a mountain of chips.

## RULE CARD: DRY FIRST. SAFE WATER NEXT. SNACKS LAST.
9. Rough rule of thumb: the rule of threes.
10. Minutes without air. Hours without shelter, in harsh weather. Days without water. Weeks without food.
11. Hypothermia can hit above forty degrees Fahrenheit, if you're wet.
12. Dry and warm comes first.
13. Then safe water. Strain cloudy water through a clean cloth. With a grown-up, bring it to a rolling boil for one minute. Three, high in the mountains.
14. Food? That can wait.

## TWIST ENDING
15. Day thirty. Tarp roof. Clean water. Thirty-nine bags of chips.
16. Then the lights flick back on.
17. Your phone buzzes. Your day-one takeout... is arriving.
18. Full What If stories on the channel.
