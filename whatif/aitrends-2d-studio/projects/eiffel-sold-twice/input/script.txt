A con man sold the Eiffel Tower. Twice.
In 1925, Victor Lustig forged government letterhead.
He told 6 scrap dealers the tower was being torn down.
Andre Poisson paid, plus a bribe. Lustig fled the country.
Poisson was too embarrassed to call the cops.
So Lustig came back, and reportedly sold it... AGAIN.
He did end up in Alcatraz. Not for the tower. For counterfeiting.
