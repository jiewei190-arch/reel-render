What if the Earth stopped spinning for one second?
Right now, the ground under you is racing east, and you can't feel it.
Then it stops. Instantly.
But you don't. Neither does the air. Neither do the oceans.
So how fast are you still going?
At the equator, about sixteen hundred seventy kilometers an hour.
Faster than a jet. Everything not bolted to bedrock flies east.
