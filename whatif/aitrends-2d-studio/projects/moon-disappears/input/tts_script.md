# moon-disappears narration (spoken = numbered lines; numbers spelled out for the TTS)
1. If the Moon vanished tonight, tides would shrink to about a third.
2. Relax, it's only a thought experiment. Here's what scientists say would happen.
3. First, nights get darker, like a new moon every single night.
4. But the tides are the big one. The Moon does most of the pulling.
5. The Sun makes tides too, but it pulls less than half as hard.
6. So every tide would be roughly a third of today's.
7. But animals would get confused. Some dung beetles steer by moonlight.
8. They'd have to switch to steering by the Milky Way instead.
9. But the strangest part is slow. Over a few hundred thousand years, Earth's tilt could wobble.
10. Mars has no big moon... and its tilt swings by up to sixty degrees.
