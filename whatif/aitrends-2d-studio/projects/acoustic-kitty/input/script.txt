The CIA built a spy cat.
In the 1960s, they hid a microphone in its ear and an antenna in its fur.
Reportedly, it cost millions of dollars.
First mission: spy on two men in a park.
The cat had other plans... it kept getting distracted. And hungry.
The CIA's memo: it "would not lend itself" to their "highly specialized needs."
Translation... you can't train a cat.
