# naked-mole-rat narration (spoken = numbered lines)
1. This rodent barely ages... and almost never gets cancer.
2. Meet the naked mole-rat: pink, wrinkly, nearly hairless, and all teeth.
3. A house mouse this size lives four years, tops. This one? Over 30.
4. But here's the crazy part: its risk of dying doesn't go up with age.
5. And big colonies watched for years? Not a single case of cancer.
6. But it gets weirder. They live like ants, with one queen in charge.
7. Only the queen has babies. The rest dig tunnels, guard, and babysit.
8. And when the air runs out? It survives 18 minutes with zero oxygen.
9. Its body switches to burning fructose, a sugar, instead.
10. Scientists now study it for clues to aging... in us.
