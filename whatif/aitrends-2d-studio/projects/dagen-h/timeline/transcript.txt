At 5 AM, a whole country switched road sides. Every driver. Same minute. It's Sweden, 1967. Swedes drive on the left. Every neighbour drives on the right. In a vote, 83% of Swedes said: keep the left. The government switched anyway. Sunday, September 3rd. At 4:50, every car in the country stops. Wait... how do you change sides? You see, you just... carefully drive across. About 350,000 road signs had to be swapped. Everyone expected chaos. Instead, accidents went down. For a few weeks, anyway. Subscribe for more.
