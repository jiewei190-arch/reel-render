A newspaper said bat-people lived on the Moon.
And people bought it.
New York, August 1835.
The Sun runs a front-page scoop.
It says the famous astronomer John Herschel has seen life on the Moon.
Through a giant new telescope.
First, animals like bison.
Then, unicorns.
Then beavers, walking on two legs, carrying their babies.
And finally...
people.
With bat wings.
Wait.
Did Herschel really see that?
You see, Herschel was in South Africa, and had no idea.
A writer at the Sun made it up.
To poke fun at people, and to sell papers.
According to one account, the story sold 60,000 copies as a pamphlet.
And the Sun?
It never admitted it was fake.
