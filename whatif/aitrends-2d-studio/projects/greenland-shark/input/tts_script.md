# greenland-shark narration (spoken = numbered lines)
1. This shark may live around 400 years.
2. Meet the Greenland shark, cruising the cold, dark North Atlantic and Arctic.
3. But how do you find out a shark's age? You look inside its eyes.
4. A 2016 study carbon-dated the lenses in sharks' eyes.
5. The biggest one, five meters long, came out at about 392 years.
6. Give or take 120 years... and it's still the longest-living vertebrate known.
7. It grows less than one centimeter a year.
8. And it isn't ready to have babies until it's about 150.
9. But it gets stranger: it cruises slower than you walk.
10. So the oldest one they found was likely born before the United States existed.
