What if you shrank to the size of an ant?
Grass becomes a forest.
Then a gust throws you off a skyscraper.
A hundred floors. No parachute.
Full size, you'd hit at around two hundred kilometres an hour.
But tiny bodies barely fall. The air holds you.
You land at walking speed, and walk away.
Then one raindrop grabs you, and won't let go.
For you, water is scarier than gravity.
Would you risk it?
