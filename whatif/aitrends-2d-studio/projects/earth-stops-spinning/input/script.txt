If Earth stopped spinning for one second, everything would fly sideways.
Relax. It's a thought experiment, but scientists have worked out what happens.
At the equator, the ground spins east at over 1,600 kilometers an hour.
You, the air, the oceans, and that cow are all moving with it.
But hit the brakes for one second, and only the ground stops.
Everything not bolted down keeps going, sliding east at full speed.
But it gets worse. The oceans would slosh right over the land.
But stand on the North Pole, and you'd barely move at all.
The good news? NASA says stopping Earth would take a miracle.
It is slowing down... by about 2 milliseconds per century.
