He survived Hiroshima.
Then he went home to Nagasaki.
Three days later, it happened again.
His name was Tsutomu Yamaguchi, a ship designer from Nagasaki.
In August 1945, he was in Hiroshima on a business trip.
On August 6, the first atomic bomb exploded about 3 km away.
He was badly burned.
But he survived.
The next day he went home to Nagasaki.
On August 9, he was at work, telling his boss what he had seen.
Then the second bomb fell on Nagasaki.
He survived again.
For decades, his papers only listed Nagasaki.
In his later years, he spoke out against nuclear weapons.
In 2009, at 93, Japan officially recognized him as a survivor of both.
The first and so far only person to get that recognition.
