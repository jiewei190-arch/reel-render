What if you fell into a black hole?
At first, nothing. You just fall.
But gravity pulls your feet harder than your head.
And it gets worse.
Near a small black hole, that pull stretches you thin like spaghetti.
Scientists really call it spaghettification.
But fall into a giant one, millions of suns heavy, and you'd cross the point of no return without even noticing.
Which one would you pick?
