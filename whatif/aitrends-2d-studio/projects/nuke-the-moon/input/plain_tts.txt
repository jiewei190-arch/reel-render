In 1958, the U.S. Air Force planned to nuke the Moon.
A top-secret study, called Project A one-nineteen.
The Soviet Union had taken an early lead in the Space Race.
So the idea was a show of force, to boost morale back home.
Fire a small nuclear warhead at the Moon... and set it off on impact.
The flash would have been faintly visible from Earth, with the naked eye.
But here's the wild part... a young Carl Sagan was on the team.
His job: working out how the dust cloud would spread.
A hydrogen bomb was too heavy, so they picked a 1.7 kiloton warhead.
In January 1959, the Air Force canceled it. The risks outweighed the benefits.
The reports were destroyed in 1987... and the Moon remains un-nuked.
