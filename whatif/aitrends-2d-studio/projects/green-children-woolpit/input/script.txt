A medieval legend says two green-skinned children appeared in an English village.
Woolpit, Suffolk. The twelfth century.
At harvest time, villagers found a brother and sister beside a wolf pit.
Their skin was green. They spoke a language nobody knew.
For days they refused all food... except raw broad beans.
Slowly they learned to eat, and the green began to fade.
Sadly, the boy was sickly, and died around the time of his baptism.
But his sister learned English... and explained where they came from.
A land where the sun never shone, the light was like twilight... and everything was green.
Historians call it folklore, or a garbled real event.
So where did the green children come from? Nobody knows.
