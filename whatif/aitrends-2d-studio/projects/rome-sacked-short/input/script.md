# The Day Rome Got Sacked (Toon 3D test Short)
1. August 24th, 410.
2. Nobody has broken into Rome in almost eight hundred years.
3. So this guard is very relaxed.
4. Then the Salarian Gate opens.
5. Some say, from the inside.
6. The Visigoths are in.

8. Two years earlier, Rome paid them off with three thousand pounds of pepper.
9. This time, pepper won't fix it.
10. And the emperor? He's not even in Rome. He's in Ravenna.
11. Told that Rome has perished, he panics. About his chicken. Named Roma.
12. Or so the story goes.
