Residents of this Honduran town say it rains fish. Every year.
It's Yoro. Locals call it the Lluvia de Peces, the rain of fish.
In the mid-year rainy season, a big storm rolls in...
and when it clears, there are fish lying on the ground.
Residents say it's happened for over a hundred years.
Since 1998, there's even a yearly festival for it, with a parade.
But here's the strange part. The fish don't appear to fall from the sky.
One theory is waterspouts. But the nearest ocean is about 45 miles away.
Another: fish from a nearby river live underground, and heavy rains wash them up.
And a local legend says a priest prayed to feed the poor... and fish fell.
Nobody has confirmed how they get there. So Yoro waits for the next storm.
