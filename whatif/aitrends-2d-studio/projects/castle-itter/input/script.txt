Five days after Hitler died, Americans and Germans fought on the same side.
May fifth, 1945. Itter Castle, Austria.
Inside: famous French prisoners, including two former prime ministers.
Outside: Waffen-SS troops, coming to attack.
Defending the castle? A small group of American tank soldiers...
German army soldiers who had joined the resistance...
and the French prisoners themselves, picking up rifles.
But here's the part that sounds made up...
The German commander, Major Sepp Gangl, was killed by a sniper while trying to get a former French prime minister out of the line of fire.
The castle held until help arrived.
It's one of only two known times in the war that Americans and Germans fought side by side.
