Every year in Wales, people race horses... on foot.
It started in 1980, with an argument in a Welsh pub.
Over a long distance, could a human keep up with a horse?
The landlord overheard... and set up a race to find out.
22 miles over the Welsh hills: runners versus horses and riders.
For 25 years, the horses won. Every. Single. Time.
But here's the part... in 2004, a runner named Huw Lobb beat them all.
By about 2 minutes. And he took home a 25,000 pound prize.
Since then, humans have won just 4 more times, most recently in 2025.
Humans: 5 wins. Horses: pretty much all the rest.
