# pando-one-tree narration (spoken = numbered lines)

1. This entire forest is actually one tree. Every single trunk.
2. It's called Pando, a quaking aspen in Utah, and every trunk grows from one shared root system.
3. That's about forty-seven thousand trunks, all genetically identical.
4. But here's the wild part: together, it weighs about six thousand tonnes.
5. That may make it the heaviest living thing on Earth.
6. Its age? Many articles said eighty thousand years. That's now considered way too old.
7. Newer estimates say thousands of years, maybe nine to sixteen thousand.
8. But Pando is in trouble. It's shrinking.
9. Deer and elk eat the young shoots before they can grow, so old trunks die with nothing to replace them.
10. Fences now protect most of it... but some deer just jump over.
11. Subscribe for more.
