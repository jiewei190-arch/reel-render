This entire forest is actually one tree. Every single trunk.
It's called Pando, a quaking aspen in Utah, and every trunk grows from one shared root system.
That's about 47,000 trunks, all genetically identical.
But here's the wild part: together, it weighs about 6,000 tonnes.
That may make it the heaviest living thing on Earth.
Its age? Many articles said 80,000 years. That's now considered way too old.
Newer estimates say thousands of years, maybe 9,000 to 16,000.
But Pando is in trouble. It's shrinking.
Deer and elk eat the young shoots before they can grow, so old trunks die with nothing to replace them.
Fences now protect most of it... but some deer just jump over.
