Canada and Denmark fought a war with whisky.
For almost 50 years.
Over a rock.
Hans Island.
Just over one square kilometer, between Canada and Greenland.
In 1973, the two countries drew a border...
and left the rock out.
In 1984, Denmark's minister for Greenland left a bottle on it.
"Welcome to the Danish island."
Then Canadian soldiers showed up.
With a flag.
And Canadian whisky.
Wait.
How is this a war?
You see, nobody fired a shot.
They just took turns.
Plant a flag, leave a bottle.
For decades.
In 2022, they finally made peace.
They split the island in two...
and swapped bottles.
