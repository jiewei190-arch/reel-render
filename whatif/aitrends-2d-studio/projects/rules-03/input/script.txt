Three a.m. Every phone on your street screams at once. Emergency alert. Three rules.
Rule one. Stay inside. Cover every window.
Rule two. If it looks like morning outside, it isn't. Don't look.
Rule three. If someone knocks, count your family before you answer.
You tape cardboard over the windows. Your little brother falls asleep on the couch.
Four a.m. Light pours in under the front door. White and warm, like sunrise.
Then a knock.
Sweetheart? It's Mom. The sun's up. Come and see.
Mom is asleep upstairs. You're almost sure.
So you count. Mom, upstairs. Dad, upstairs. Your brother, on the couch. You.
Four.
Five.
