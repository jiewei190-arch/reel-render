This shrimp punches so fast, the water around it briefly boils.
Meet the peacock mantis shrimp. Small, but it hits with thousands of times its body weight.
Its club fires at 83 kilometers per hour.
Scientists compare it to a bullet fired from a handgun.
But here's the weird part. The strike is so fast, the pressure around it crashes.
The water turns into vapor bubbles. Like boiling, but from pressure, not heat.
Then the bubbles collapse... and hit the target a second time. One punch, two hits.
But it gets stranger. The collapsing bubbles give off a tiny flash of light.
Snail shells crack like eggs. And the biggest ones can even break aquarium glass.
So whatever you do... never tap on its tank.
