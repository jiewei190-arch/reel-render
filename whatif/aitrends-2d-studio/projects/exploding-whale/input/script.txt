In 1970, Oregon removed a dead whale... with dynamite.
A 45-foot sperm whale had washed up on the beach in Florence, Oregon.
8 tons, rotting, and the state highway department got the job.
The plan: blow it to bits, and let the seagulls clean up.
So they used half a ton of dynamite. A crowd gathered to watch.
3... 2... 1...
But here's the part... the whale came back down.
Chunks of blubber rained on the crowd. One crushed a brand-new car.
Nobody was seriously hurt. The seagulls? Scared off. And most of the whale... was still there.
50 years later, the town named a park after it: Exploding Whale Memorial Park.
