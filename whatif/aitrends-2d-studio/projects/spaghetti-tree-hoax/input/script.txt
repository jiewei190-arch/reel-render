In 1957, British TV convinced viewers that spaghetti grows on trees.
April 1st. A serious British TV show aired a report from Switzerland.
A Swiss family... harvesting spaghetti, straight off the trees.
The report even said the dreaded spaghetti weevil had virtually disappeared.
And it was narrated by one of Britain's most trusted broadcasters.
Back then, pasta was an unusual dish in Britain.
But here's the part... the next day, viewers called in.
Some wanted to know how to grow their own spaghetti tree.
It was an April Fools' joke, of course.
The reported advice? Place a sprig of spaghetti in a tin of tomato sauce... and hope for the best.
