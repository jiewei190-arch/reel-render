In 2004, Paris police stumbled onto a secret movie theater underground.
Deep in the catacombs, beneath the Trocadéro.
The catacombs hold the remains of more than six million people.
And down there, they found a giant cinema screen, seats for an audience, and projection equipment.
Film reels of recent thrillers and film noir classics.
But here's the part that gets weirder...
A fully stocked bar. And a complete restaurant, with tables and chairs.
Electricity and a phone line had been brought in from an unknown location.
The security system? A recording of barking dogs.
A secretive group called the U.X. took responsibility.
When police came back to investigate, it had all been dismantled. Left behind, a note... "Do not search."
