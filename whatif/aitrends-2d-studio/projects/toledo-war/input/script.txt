Ohio and Michigan once nearly went to war... over Toledo.
Blame a bad map. Both claimed one strip of land, 5 to 8 miles wide.
In 1835, both sent militias to the Maumee River... and traded taunts.
The one battle? Reportedly, shots fired into the air. Nobody was hit.
But here's the part... the war's only injury: a deputy, hurt with a penknife.
By a man named Two Stickney. His brother? Reportedly named One.
Congress stepped in. Ohio got Toledo. Michigan got statehood... and the Upper Peninsula.
A consolation prize almost everyone called a worthless wilderness.
But in the 1840s, they found copper and iron. By the 1860s, it supplied 90% of America's copper.
Michigan lost Toledo... and won the jackpot.
