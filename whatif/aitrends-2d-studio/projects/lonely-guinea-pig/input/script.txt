In Switzerland, owning one guinea pig is basically illegal.
Not because they're loud. Because guinea pigs get lonely...
and Switzerland decided loneliness is animal cruelty.
The law says social pets must live in pairs. Parrots, even lovebirds.
If it needs a friend, you are legally required to provide one.
But what happens when one of your pair dies?
Congrats. You're now a criminal with a grieving rodent.
Enter the most Swiss solution ever: rent-a-guinea-pig services.
You lease a temporary companion for your widowed pet.
Somewhere in Zurich right now, a guinea pig is on a blind date...
arranged by the government.
