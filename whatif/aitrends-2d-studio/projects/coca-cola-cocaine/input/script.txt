Coca-Cola really had cocaine in it.
That's not a rumor.
In 1886, a morphine-addicted pharmacist named John Pemberton invented it, with coca leaves, the plant cocaine comes from.
It was sold as medicine.
A temperance drink that supposedly fixed everything.
Then America panicked about cocaine, and around 1903, the cocaine was out.
But here's the part nobody knows: Coke reportedly STILL uses coca leaves today.
One company in New Jersey legally imports coca leaves, strips the cocaine out, and Coke uses what's left for flavor.
Coca-Cola: still made with coca. Just... the legal part.
