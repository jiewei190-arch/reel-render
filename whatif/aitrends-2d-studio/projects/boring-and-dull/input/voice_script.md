# Boring and Dull (Justin clips Short, Chatterbox D2)

1. A town called Boring teamed up with a village called Dull.
2. These are real places. Boring is in Oregon. Its motto: the most exciting place to live.
3. Dull is in Scotland. Population: about 84.
4. In 2012, a Scottish woman on a cycling holiday rode through Boring... and had an idea.
5. Boring's planning group voted. Unanimous. Boring and Dull, a pair for the ages.
6. But here's the part... now they throw a party. Boring and Dull Day, every August ninth.

8. Then, in 2013, a third town joined. Bland. As in, Bland, Australia.
9. Together, they're the League of Extraordinary Communities.
10. Also known as... the Trinity of Tedium.
