A town called Boring teamed up with a village called Dull.
These are real places. Boring is in Oregon. Its motto: the most exciting place to live.
Dull is in Scotland. Population: about 84.
In 2012, a Scottish woman on a cycling holiday rode through Boring... and had an idea.
Boring's planning group voted. Unanimous. Boring and Dull, a pair for the ages.
But here's the part... now they throw a party. Boring and Dull Day, every August 9th.
Yes. With bagpipes.
Then, in 2013, a third town joined. Bland. As in, Bland, Australia.
Together, they're the League of Extraordinary Communities.
Also known as... the Trinity of Tedium.
