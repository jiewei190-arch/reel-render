1. In nineteen seventy-seven, a telescope caught a signal so weird someone wrote, "Wow!"
2. Ohio's Big Ear radio telescope was listening for alien signals.
3. Its printout was code: numbers meant weak, letters meant strong.
4. Then, on August fifteenth, it printed: six, E, Q, U, J, five.
5. That U was about thirty standard deviations above the noise.
6. Days later, Jerry Eeman circled it and wrote: "Wow!"
7. It lasted seventy-two seconds, exactly as long as Big Ear could watch one spot.
8. They listened again for decades. Nothing. Comets got blamed. Astronomers said nope.
9. But here's the part... a twenty twenty-four study says a cold hydrogen cloud may have suddenly flared.
