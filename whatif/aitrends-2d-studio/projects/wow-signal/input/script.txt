In 1977, a telescope caught a signal so weird someone wrote "Wow!"
Ohio's Big Ear radio telescope was listening for alien signals.
Its printout was code: numbers meant weak, letters meant strong.
Then, on August 15, it printed: 6, E, Q, U, J, 5.
That U was about 30 standard deviations above the noise.
Days later, Jerry Ehman circled it and wrote: "Wow!"
It lasted 72 seconds, exactly as long as Big Ear could watch one spot.
They listened again for decades. Nothing. Comets got blamed. Astronomers said nope.
But here's the part... a 2024 study says a cold hydrogen cloud may have suddenly flared.
Still unconfirmed. But in 2012, someone replied anyway... with over 10,000 tweets.
