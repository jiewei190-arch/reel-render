One man hiccuped for 68 years... straight.
In 1922, farmer Charles Osborne reportedly went down... under a 350-pound hog.
Then he started hiccuping. And he did not... stop.
According to Guinness, 20 to 40 times a minute... day and night, for decades.
Doctors tried. Nothing lasted. His longest break? Reportedly... 50 hours.
But here's the part... he lived a normal life anyway. He married twice, and reportedly had eight kids.
One doctor's guess? Lifting that hog popped a tiny blood vessel in his brain.
Guinness called it the longest attack of hiccups ever. Over 400 million hiccups.
Then, in 1990, after 68 years... they just stopped.
