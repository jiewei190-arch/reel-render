In 1962, laughing shut down 14 schools.
January thirtieth. A girls' boarding school in Kashasha, in what is now Tanzania.
Three students started laughing... and couldn't stop.
It spread to 95 of the school's 159 students. Some attacks lasted hours. Some lasted days.
In March, the school had to close.
But it didn't stop there...
It spread to a village where several of the girls lived. 217 people were affected.
Then more villages. More schools. About 1,000 people in total. 14 schools shut down.
It wasn't funny. The laughing came with crying, fainting and pain. Doctors called it mass hysteria.
It took about 18 months to finally fade away.
