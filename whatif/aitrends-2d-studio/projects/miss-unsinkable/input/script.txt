This woman survived the Titanic. And both its sister ships.
1911: Violet Jessop's ship, the Olympic, hit a warship.
Her next ship... the Titanic.
1912: an officer reportedly handed her a baby in a lifeboat.
1916: the Britannic hit a mine and sank in 55 minutes.
The propellers were still turning. She jumped clear.
Three ships. Three disasters. They called her Miss Unsinkable.
