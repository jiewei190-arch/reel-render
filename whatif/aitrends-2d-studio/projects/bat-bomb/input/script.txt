The U.S. military built a bomb out of bats.
World War Two. Project X-Ray.
The plan: a million bats, each carrying a tiny timed firebomb.
Drop them over Japan. They'd roost in attics... and then ignite.
In May 1943, armed bats escaped during a test...
and set their own air base on fire.
About two years. About 2 million dollars.
It was canceled in 1944. The atomic bomb project took priority.
The bat bomb was never used in combat.
