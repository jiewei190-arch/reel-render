In 1876, it rained meat in Kentucky.
Out of a clear blue sky.
A woman was outside, making soap, when chunks of meat started falling all around her.
Some pieces were 4 inches wide.
So two men did the obvious thing.
They tasted it.
Verdict: mutton.
Or maybe venison.
It made The New York Times.
Scientists found lung, muscle and cartilage.
Wait, how does meat fall out of a clear sky?
You see, the best guess is vultures.
Scared vultures throw up, to fly away lighter.
And when one does it... the whole flock joins in.
So that clear blue sky... wasn't empty.
