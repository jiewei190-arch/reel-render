### Rules for shot writers
- Write ONLY your own file: `scenes/shots/<BATCH>_*.js` (e.g. `A_cold_open.js`, `E_last_run.js`) registering exactly your batch's ids from shots.json. Do not edit shots.json, sets/, cast.js, config, tools, engine or another batch's files. Need a prop (staircase, family photo wall, clipboard, smartwatch, scale)? Draw it locally in your shot file. `scenes/shots/DEMO_cast.js` binds s001 until batch A lands: batch A deletes nothing; the lead removes it.
- Helper names in the scene texts are the Stage A names (train.js pushUp/kneePushUp/sitUp/squat/runCycle/repCounter/stopwatch/backpack/pillow/punchPose + the Biscuit-on-the-back helper; park.js `park(g,t,cam,{season, mood, rain, snow, leaves})` + `parkFg`; body.js blueprints; home3.js bathroom/kitchen/bedroom + `timeSkip(g,t,label)`, HAIR LOG, Dev phone UI). If a worker shipped a different name or option, use theirs and follow its DEMO file.
- Hero look in every shot: BIG DARK CURLS (never bald, never thinner on screen, even in the hair-scare beats), green hoodie, white sweatband with a green stripe, grey trousers, grey running shoes. No jumpsuit, cape, gloves, names or catchphrases (the anime nod is only the routine, the hair joke and the pillow punch).
- Devices on every beat: a `timeSkip` card flips (tick SFX) on each Day/Week/Month/Year jump: s010 (riffle to 1,095), s012 DAY 1, s017 DAY 2, s027 WEEK 1, s029 MONTH 1, s038 MONTH 3, s053 DAY 1? Rule cards: s020, s041, s062, s078, s098 (exact titles from script.md, already in the scene texts).
- Seasons on the park runs: spring (day 1, title), summer (year 1 run), autumn + rain (shin splints, walking days), winter + snow (year 2-3), winter -> spring wipe (curls back, s093), spring dawn again for DAY 1,095 (full circle).
- Lip-sync only the quoted lines: MINA s006 ("Everyone says" only), s007, s019, s061 ("For your own good." only), s094 (mouths "Unchanged."); DEV s008 ("It's a known thing." only), s053 ("Wear this." only), s077, s114 ("Okay." only), s115. The narrator and the HERO are never lip-synced.

Adjust `at` only if you change the beat (tell the lead).
