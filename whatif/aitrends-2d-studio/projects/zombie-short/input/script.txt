The zombie apocalypse starts on a Tuesday. Your only survival skill is ordering takeout.
Day one. You barricade the door with a beanbag.
The zombies push it over. Gently.
Day two. You sneak past them in squeaky shoes.
Squeak. Squeak. Forty heads turn.
You run. Mina runs faster. Biscuit is already on the roof.
Then your phone buzzes. Your takeout, from Tuesday, is finally here.
The scooter pulls up, and every zombie on the street stops. And sniffs.
Turns out zombies don't want brains. They want fries.
So you do the one thing you're good at. You order more.
Day thirty. You run the only delivery service in the apocalypse. Five stars.
The zombies always tip. In shoes.
Would you survive? Tell me your one skill.
