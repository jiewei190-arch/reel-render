Day one of the zombie apocalypse. No weapons. No plan. No experience.
The power's out. The whole city is dark.
You hide in the supermarket. Lights off. Doors locked.
Mistake number one. You turn on your flashlight.
Something hits the front glass. Then another. Then a hundred.
Mistake number two. You run to the back, into the freezer room, and pull the door shut.
Cold. Dark. Silent. Safe.
Mistake number three. The door only opens from the outside.
Hours pass. Your phone dies.
Then, a knock. Slow. Patient.
Hey. It's me. Open up.
It's Dev. But Dev didn't make it out on day one.
Would you open the door?
