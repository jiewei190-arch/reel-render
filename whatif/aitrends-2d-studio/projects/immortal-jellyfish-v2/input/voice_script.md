# Immortal Jellyfish v2 ("jail break" re-cut). EMOTION: WOW

1. This jellyfish can't die of old age.
2. Injured, or starving? It doesn't die. It hits reset.
3. It melts into a blob, sinks, and turns back into a baby.
4. Then grows up again. And again.
5. It's smaller than your pinky nail, and in theory, it can do this forever.
6. The catch? Fish and turtles eat it. Immortal, but also... lunch.
