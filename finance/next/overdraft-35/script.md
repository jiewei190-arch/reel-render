# overdraft-35: script (draft for MAIN; not voiced)

- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (split: same coffee, same bank)
- **Hook text (frame 0, ≤ 5 words):** $35 FOR A $5 COFFEE?
- **Frame 0:** CLOSE: a $5 coffee cup slams onto a counter; a card reader flashes red DECLINED... then APPROVED, and a $35 tag stamps onto the cup (impact SFX at 0.00 s). Split screen from 1.0 s: Spender (top, balance $0) vs Saver (bottom, a $100 cushion).
- **Two-person race:** Two counters: SPENDER PAID vs SAVER PAID per coffee ($5 vs $40, then $15 vs $120 after three).
- **Open loop:** "But the fee isn't even the worst part..." (~45 %): a second and third coffee slide onto the counter, covered tags trembling.
- **Twist:** "It hits every time... a hundred and five dollars in fees" (~70 %): three $35 tags stamp in a row; Spender's counter jumps to $120.
- **Loop:** Last line "...Spender's cost forty." → frame 0, the $35 tag stamping onto the cup.
- **Easter egg / double payoff:** the blocky cat steals a coin out of the tip jar; double payoff: a 4th coffee slides in as the loop starts.
- **Rules checked:** line 1 has no character names (they come in after 2 s); "you" in line 1; ~72 words (aim 75–85 at a natural pace, 25–29 s); numbers spoken the way the HUD shows them; hedged ("made-up", "about", "at many big banks").

## VO (one line per row; `script.txt` is the same text)
1. Your bank can charge you thirty-five dollars for a five dollar coffee.
2. Same coffee, same bank. Spender's balance is zero. Saver keeps a hundred dollar cushion.
3. Spender's card goes through anyway. Overdraft: a thirty-five dollar fee.
4. But the fee isn't even the worst part...
5. It hits every time. Three coffees before payday: a hundred and five dollars in fees, at many big banks.
6. Saver's coffee cost five dollars. Spender's cost forty.
