## The 10 things these Shorts do that a basic blocky finance Short probably doesn't
These come from 8 Shorts, so check them against your own retention data before making any a rule.

1. **Two-layer captions.** A tiny full-sentence line near the top (~5-12% down) sits above a big bold 1-3 word chunk in the lower third (~65-80% down). The small line is for readers who skim and the big chunk keeps the beat. Example: Zack Splinters 1.5 s (`https://www.youtube.com/shorts/yGw0nhlUtkQ`); Tyler 7f9Ze66WlPw at 1.5 s.
2. **One accent colour on the one word that matters.** Only one word per chunk is coloured: the subject, the number or the emotion. Example: "this man ADOPTED" with ADOPTED in green at 0.05 s (`nOiaaf60W0U`); "were PRISONERS" in orange at 1.5 s (`7f9Ze66WlPw`). For Block Money: colour the money word (red for Spender losses, green for Saver gains).
3. **Caption already running at frame 0.** There is no blank "title card" beat. The first word is on screen at 0.05 s in Tyler x2, Qubik, cannonanimates and McFlurry.
4. **Open on an extreme close-up, then reveal wide within ~1.5 s.** Tyler `nOiaaf60W0U` goes from a face filling the frame at 0.05 s to a 10-kid group shot at 1.5 s. The close-up carries the emotion and the wide shot delivers the number or setup.
5. **Subject plus number in the first three words.** "This man adopted 10 kids", "these 13 kids were prisoners". The hook is a specific noun and a number. For Block Money: "Spender blew $500..." in the first caption chunk.
6. **Cut cadence matched to the content type.** The scan found about 5 cuts per 10 s on Tyler and about 9.5 per 10 s on cannonanimates (upper bound), against roughly 0 on Zack's macro explainers. A story with dialogue needs a cut about every 1-2 s. A single process needs one continuous camera move. A basic blocky Short that holds one static angle for 5 s or more is out of both bands.
7. **Camera never fully static.** Zack's Stitches `ttuKeS5k4Uo` is one slow continuous push or drift for 31 s. The Roblox Shorts have handheld-style sway between cuts (cannonanimates `8WEm8GKMT4w`, 0-3.6 s). Add a slow push-in or drift to every shot, including holds.
8. **Populated backgrounds.** cannonanimates has other avatars on the playground frame behind the swing (`8WEm8GKMT4w`, 0.1 s). Tyler's group shot has 10 distinct outfits (`nOiaaf60W0U`, 1.5 s). Background characters and props give the eye something to explore on replay.
9. **A designed loop seam.** Balloon Toons ends on exactly the pose it starts on (`ISDcNOtk8b0`, 10.06-11.4 s vs 0.05 s), so replays are seamless. Qubik appears to end mid-sentence into its opening word (`XIaeEg3A4vM`, 28.2 s "ON" into 0.05 s "SPOT"). That is unconfirmed without audio. Write the last caption word so it grammatically feeds the first word of the video.
10. **Proof or reveal image in the last second.** Tyler's stories end by cutting to real photographs plus a subscribe chip (`nOiaaf60W0U`, 23.7-25.2 s). The ending is a different visual register from the animation. For Block Money: a closing "receipt" or "balance" card in a different visual style (a real-looking statement, a bar chart) acts as the proof beat, and the Spender/Saver gag can sit one beat before it as the double payoff.

## Numbers to copy (n is small, so treat as starting points)
- **Length:** median 27.6 s (n=8). In-scope story/explainer Shorts sit at 25-31 s, with cannonanimates at 47 s. Keep your ~30 s.
- **Cut rate:** Roblox-style dialogue stories about 5-9.5 cuts per 10 s (automated, upper bound); macro explainers about 0-1 per 10 s. For a two-character money story, aim for about 5-6 cuts per 10 s (one every ~1.7 s) as a starting point.
- **Caption size and placement (from 164x268 px crops):** big layer cap height about 5-6% of frame height, centred, with its baseline about 70-80% down. Small sentence layer cap height about 2-3%, near the top (~5-12% down), left- or centre-aligned, often on a dark rounded box (Zack) or plain white (Tyler, Qubik). The big layer is white bold with a dark outline, and each chunk is 1-3 words.
- **SFX per 10 s:** not measured. Needs a listening pass or an audio-enabled tool.
- **Voice:** not measured. Same caveat.
