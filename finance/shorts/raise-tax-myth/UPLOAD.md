**Title (46 chars):** You Turned Down a $10K Raise Because of Taxes?

**Description:**
Example: a single filer at $100,000 taxable income gets a $10,000 raise. With 2025 federal brackets, only the dollars above $103,350 are taxed at 24%; everything below is taxed exactly as before. The extra $10,000 costs about $2,333 in federal income tax and $765 in payroll tax, so about $6,902 is kept. Over 5 years (no further raises) that's about $34,510. State and local taxes, credits and deductions are not included.
