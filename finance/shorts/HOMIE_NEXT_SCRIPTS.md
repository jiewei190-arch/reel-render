# My Homie Wildin: next episodes (drafts v3: curses + emotions, under 40 s each)

Same cast, sets and look as ep1/ep2. Narrator "me" + "homie" (ep2: Gerald + Tyler, ElevenLabs).
About 55-62 spoken words each, about 33-38 s. No whisper/soft tags, no second-person demands.

How the emotions work:
- VOICE: an ElevenLabs delivery tag per line, e.g. [annoyed], [angry], [shouting], [shocked], [laughing], [deadpan],
  [disgusted]. Tags are performed, not spoken.
- FACE + BODY: the speaker's face changes ON the key word, not once per line (e.g. annoyed -> furious on "FUCK").
  Body beats on every word, bigger on the caps words. The listener reacts in the cut-aways. Mouths open on
  shouted lines (static open mouth; no lip-sync).

END: homie licks his lips, smug half-lid, slow push, cut to black.
