- **Eggs / double payoff:** cat + sticky note; finalGag catCoin (the cat walks off with a coin on the loop).
- **Rules checked:** line 1 has no names and has "you" plus a dollar figure (8 words); hedged ("In this example", "about", "nearly"); no app, chain or brand names; no advice or CTA.
