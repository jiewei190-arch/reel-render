## Hook candidates (first line: a shocking number or a direct "you" claim, under 8 words)

1. **This penny beats a million dollars.** (6 words) ← PICK: a concrete, checkable claim that sounds impossible; the prop is in the shot.
2. You'd take the million. You'd lose. (6)
3. One penny. Thirty days. Five million dollars. (7)
4. A million dollars is the wrong choice. (7)
5. Take a penny, not a million. (6)

Why the pick: it is a paradox the viewer must watch to resolve, and it loops: "…every single time, / this penny beats a million dollars."

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "Which is why, every single time, / This penny beats a million dollars."
- **Twist (~70%):** day 30 = $5.3 million, and half of it arrives on the very last day.
- **No dead air:** every line is a new day/number (a question) or its payoff; "Still losing" sets up the turn.
- **Counter (every shot):** a big holo counter over the set, above the caption band: `DAY nn · $x` ticking up with the doubling (DAY 1 · $0.01 → DAY 30 · $5,368,709.12), red while it is behind $1,000,000, green once it passes.
