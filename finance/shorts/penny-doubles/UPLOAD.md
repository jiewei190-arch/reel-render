**Title (44 chars):** A Penny vs $1,000,000: Which Would You Take?

**Description:**
One penny, doubled every day for 30 days. On day 20 it's only about $5,000... then the doubling takes over.
A thought experiment about exponential growth (nothing real doubles every day). Day n = $0.01 × 2^(n−1).
