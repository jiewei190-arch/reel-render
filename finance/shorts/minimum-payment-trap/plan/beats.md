## FORMAT v1 beat sheet

1. **Want:** Spender wants a big TV, right now.
2. **Trouble:** he puts $5,000 on a card at 22 % and pays only the minimum ("Easy, right?"). Most of each payment is interest, so the money he owes barely moves.
3. **Twist:** nineteen years and over $8,000 extra. Saver pays $200 a month and is done in under three years.

- **Hook text on frame 0 (6 words):** 19 YEARS TO PAY OFF A TV
- **Frame 0 (mid-action):** a big TV is mid-fall onto the store counter, and Spender cheers with both arms up. This is hook A v1, the owner's pick.
- **Stakes by ~1.5 s:** "…paying for it for nineteen years" is spoken by ~3 s. The hook text states the stake from frame 0.
- **"You":** line 7 "So if you only pay the minimum, you end up like Spender." The line before it is a plain-word stake: "the money he owes barely moves".
- **Loop:** "…you end up like Spender." / "Spender just bought a TV." On the last shot a new TV is coming down from above, so frame 0 (the TV mid-fall) follows on.
- **Music and SFX:** the toon3d `upbeat` bed from 0 s, ducked under the voice. SFX hit every impact: the TV landing, the card beep, the calendar burst, the price-tag thud, the 22 % stamp, the "Wrong" buzz, the coin rush, the jar crash, Saver's punch and burst, and the DONE stamp.
- **Voice:** D2 Chatterbox at exaggeration 0.55, cfg 0.3, normal speed. Best of 2 seeds (see `vo/takes.md`).
