# minimum-payment-trap

- **Spoken:** 73 words, voice 26.89 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S19 ("Because otherwise...") cuts back to the S01 lens: Spender picks up the red $5,000 block again at the start of the long road, counters reset; the loop lands on "Your minimum payment can take nineteen years."

## Hook candidates

1. **Your minimum payment can take nineteen years.** (7 words) ← PICK: a direct "you" claim with a shocking number; "can" keeps it honest (the example is stated next).
2. Your credit card wants you to pay minimums. (8)
3. Nineteen years to pay off five thousand. (7)
4. The minimum payment is a trap. (6, opinion, no number)
5. Five thousand dollars, eight thousand in interest. (7)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "Because otherwise... / Your minimum payment can take nineteen years."
- **Twist (~70%):** a flat two hundred a month: under three years instead of nineteen.
- **Counter (every shot):** two counters on a toll-booth display, above the caption band: `BALANCE $5,000 ↓` and `INTEREST PAID $0 ↑` (to $8,099 on the minimum route), plus a `YEAR nn` clock; the flat-$200 lane gets its own green counter.
