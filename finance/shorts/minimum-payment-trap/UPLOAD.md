**Title (43 chars):** Why a $5,000 Card Balance Can Take 19 Years

**Description:**
Example: $5,000 at 22% APR, paying only the minimum (interest + 1% of the balance, $25 floor), no new charges:
about 19 years and over $8,000 in interest. A flat $200 a month: under 3 years. Your own statement's minimum payment warning shows your real numbers.
