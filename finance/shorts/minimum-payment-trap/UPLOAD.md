# UPLOAD: minimum-payment-trap (FORMAT v1)

**Title:** He'll Pay for This TV for 19 Years

**Description:**
Example: a $5,000 TV on a card at 22% APR, paying only the minimum (interest plus 1% of the balance). That's about 19 years and over $8,000 in interest. Paying a flat $200 a month instead clears it in under 3 years. Your own card statement shows a minimum payment warning with your real payoff time.

Sources:
- Federal Reserve G.19, credit card interest rates: https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/g19/current/default.htm
- CFPB, Understanding minimum payments: https://files.consumerfinance.gov/f/documents/cfpb_building_block_activities_understanding-minimum-payments_guide.pdf
- CFPB, Regulation Z §1026.7 (the minimum payment warning): https://www.consumerfinance.gov/rules-policy/regulations/1026/7/
