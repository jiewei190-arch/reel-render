# juicero: fact check

Checked 2026-11-23 with live web reads. The Bloomberg article at the centre of the story (19 Apr 2017, "Silicon
Valley's $400 Juicer May Be Feeling the Squeeze") could not be opened from the build machine, so its finding is taken
from same-day reports by CBS News and Fortune. The company name is spoken and shown as plain text only. No logo and no
drawing of the real machine: the props are the kit's generic bottle ("JUICE"), bag ("JUICE PACK"), a plain box and two
paper cups. Executives are generic and faceless; nobody is named.
