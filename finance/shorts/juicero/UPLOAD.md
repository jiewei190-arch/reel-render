## Description
This juicer raised about a hundred twenty million dollars. Your hands worked just as well. Juicero launched in 2016: a Wi-Fi juice press for 699 dollars. It squeezed packs of produce with four tons of force, its founder said. Then, in 2017, Bloomberg reporters squeezed a pack by hand. Same amount of juice. Sometimes faster. The company offered refunds. Within five months, it stopped selling. The lesson: build something the customer cannot do alone.
