**Title (25 chars):** A $5 Coffee That Cost $40

**Description:**
Example: a $5 coffee on a $0 balance. The card goes through anyway, and many big banks charge a $35 overdraft fee. Three coffees before payday = $105 in fees: $120 paid for $15 of coffee. Fees differ by bank; some charge less or nothing. For one-time debit card and ATM overdrafts, banks need your opt-in before charging a fee.
