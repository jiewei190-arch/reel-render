Who decided rent should be 30 percent?
It started with a 1969 law for public housing.
It capped rent at 25 percent of a tenant's income.
A limit to protect tenants, not a spending target.
In 1981, Congress raised that cap to 30 percent.
Twist: a public housing cap became everyone's rule of thumb.
On 4,000 dollars a month, that's 1,200 for rent.
