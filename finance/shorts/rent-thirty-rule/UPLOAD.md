**Title:** Where the 30% Rent Rule Came From

**Description:**
The "spend 30% of income on rent" rule started as a rent cap. In 1969, Congress limited public housing rent to 25% of a tenant's income. In 1981 the cap was raised to 30%. That cap became the most common rule of thumb for affordability. Example: on $4,000 a month, 30% is $1,200. It's a rough guide, not a law of nature.
