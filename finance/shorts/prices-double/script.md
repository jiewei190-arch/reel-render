# prices-double

- **Spoken:** 64 words, voice 25.63 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S18 ("That's how") returns to the S01 lens: Penny looks up at the $1 price block that starts to double again; the loop lands on "Prices double faster than you think."

## Hook candidates

1. **Prices double faster than you think.** (6 words) ← PICK: a direct claim the Short proves; the last line ("That's how") flows into it.
2. At 4 percent, prices double in 18 years. (8, spoils the payoff)
3. Your money halves every 24 years. (6, only at 3 %)
4. Inflation has a doubling clock. (5)
5. The Rule of 72 works backwards too. (7)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "Small yearly numbers, huge long-run change. That's how / prices double faster than you think."
- **Twist (~70%):** since 1970 U.S. prices doubled roughly every 18 years, three times over (CPI-U).
- **Counter (every shot):** `72 ÷ INFLATION = YEARS` flipping per rate, then `1970 → 2026: ×8.7 (3 DOUBLINGS)`.
