**Title (35 chars):** Prices Double Faster Than You Think

**Description:**
The Rule of 72 works on inflation: at 2% prices double in about 36 years, at 3% about 24, at 4% about 18. Since 1970, U.S. consumer prices rose about 8.7× (CPI-U): roughly 4% a year, a doubling about every 18 years.
