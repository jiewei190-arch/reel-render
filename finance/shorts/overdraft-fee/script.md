# overdraft-fee

- **Spoken:** 65 words, voice 28.07 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S19 ("You can still") returns to the S01 lens: Spender swipes again at the terminal, the $24 sandwich tag and the red $58 receipt pop; the loop lands on "Swipe 24 dollars, pay 58."

## Hook candidates

1. **Swipe 24 dollars, pay 58.** (5 words) ← PICK: two numbers that don't add up; the last line ("Opted in? You can still") runs into it.
2. A 34 dollar fee on 24 dollars. (7)
3. Your bank's loan at 17,000 percent. (6, gives away the twist)
4. The overdraft that costs more than lunch. (7, vague)
5. Three days, 34 dollars. (4)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "Opted in? You can still / swipe 24 dollars, pay 58."
- **Twist (~68%):** "Twist: as a loan, that's an A.P.R. of about 17,000 percent." ($34 to borrow $24 for three days, the CFPB's own comparison).
- **Counter (every shot):** `$24 SWIPE` → `+ $34 FEE` → `= $58`; on the twist `APR ≈17,000%`. VO rounding matches the HUD ("about 17,000" ↔ "≈17,000%").
