**Title:** Swipe $24, Pay $58: The Overdraft Math

**Description:**
In the CFPB's 2014 overdraft study, the median debit card transaction that triggered an overdraft fee was $24, and the median fee was $34. More than half of overdraft episodes ended within three days. Borrowing $24 for three days for a $34 charge works out to an APR of about 17,000%. Under federal rules, overdraft fees on ATM and one-time debit card transactions need your opt-in.
