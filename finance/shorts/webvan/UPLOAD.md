## Description
Webvan burned about eight hundred million dollars delivering groceries. It launched in 1999 with a simple promise: order online, get your food at the door. One month in, it signed a one-billion-dollar deal for up to twenty-six giant warehouses. On its first day of trading, it was valued at about eight billion dollars. Less than two years later, the stock closed at six cents. Two thousand workers lost their jobs, and Webvan filed for bankruptcy. The lesson: prove the model, then scale it.
