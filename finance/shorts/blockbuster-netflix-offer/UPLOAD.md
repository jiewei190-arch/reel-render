## Description
Netflix once offered itself to Blockbuster for fifty million dollars. It was 2000. Netflix mailed DVDs, and it was losing millions. Its founders flew to Dallas to pitch the deal. One founder says Blockbuster's boss struggled not to laugh. The answer was no. That year, Blockbuster collected nearly 800 million dollars in late fees. Ten years later, it filed for bankruptcy, and the whole company sold at auction for about 320 million. Netflix today is worth about 240 billion (market value on Nov 23, 2026). Blockbuster's former CEO disputes the founders' account of the meeting.
