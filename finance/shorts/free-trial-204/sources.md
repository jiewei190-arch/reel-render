# Sources and maths: free-trial-204 (for MAIN QA)

**Hypothetical example, not advice.** $17 a month is an illustrative price; real trials and prices vary.

- **"$17 a month" / "about $204 a year":** CNET subscription survey 2025 (YouGov, 2,440 US adults, fielded Apr 28-30, 2025): subscribers spend an average of **$205 a year on subscriptions they rarely or never use**, about **$17 a month**; total subscription spend about $90 a month.
  - (CNET survey, as reported by) https://thedesk.net/2025/05/cnet-subscription-survey-2025/
  - https://cordcuttersnews.com/americans-now-pay-over-200-in-subscriptions-they-dont-use/
  - Newer (2026) CNET survey, reported by local news: average spend **$111 a month**, about **$252 a year** wasted on unused subscriptions. Not used in the VO because I could not reach CNET's own methodology; it makes the VO figure conservative.
    https://www.wfsb.com/2026/07/16/average-american-now-spending-111-month-subscriptions-study-finds/ ·
    https://www.abc15.com/dont-waste-your-money/subscription-creep-average-family-wastes-252-a-year-on-unused-services
- **"Almost half of people in one survey forgot a trial at least once":** CNET survey (2024): **48 %** of US adults said they signed up for a free trial of a paid subscription and then forgot to cancel it (Millennials 65 %, Gen Z 59 %).
  - https://9to5mac.com/2024/10/18/half-of-americans-have-forgotten-to-cancel-a-trial-subscription/
- **Context (not in the VO):** C+R Research (2022): people guessed $86 a month on subscriptions; itemised it was $219; 74 % said recurring charges are easy to forget. https://www.fool.com/the-ascent/personal-finance/articles/survey-finds-americans-spend-133-more-than-they-realize-on-monthly-subscription-fees/
- **Context (not in the VO):** The FTC's "click to cancel" rule was vacated by the 8th Circuit on July 8, 2025; the older Negative Option rule and ROSCA still apply. https://www.morganlewis.com/pubs/2025/07/ftcs-click-to-cancel-rule-vacated-ahead-of-planned-july-14-effective-date

## Maths
```
monthly charge (example)      = $17          (CNET 2025: $205 / 12 = $17.08 unused per month)
charges in one year           = 12
Spender, one year             = 12 x $17 = $204   -> "about two hundred and four dollars" / hook "$200"
counter ticks on screen       : month 1 = $17, month 6 = 6 x 17 = $102, month 12 = $204
Saver (cancelled before day 8)= $0
```
