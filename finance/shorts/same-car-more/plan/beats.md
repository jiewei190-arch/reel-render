## FORMAT v1 beat sheet (style S4 WOULD-YOU-RATHER)

1. **Want:** the viewer picks one: $926 a month or just $453 for the same $30,000 car at the same 7 %. Spender picks $453: "Easy choice, right?"
2. **Trouble:** $453 means seven years, not three. Behind the shed door, something is eating his money.
3. **Twist:** the door bursts open on the INTEREST furnace: seven years costs $8,034 in interest, three years $3,347. The cheap payment burns about $4,700 extra.

- **Hook text on frame 0 (6 words):** $926 OR $453 A MONTH?
- **Frame 0 (mid-action):** two identical red cars. The $926/MO board has landed and the $453/MO board is mid-drop.
- **Open loop (56 %, 15.4 s):** "And behind that door, something is eating his money..." The shed door rattles, its sign is covered ("? ? ?") and orange glow leaks out underneath (S08, S19, S21).
- **Answer withheld until the twist (70 %, 19.4 s of 27.72 s):** the door bursts open on "8,034". The furnace roars and the $8,034 vs $3,347 towers rise, matching the VO. The payoff line "about 4,700 extra" follows at 92 %.
- **"You":** "Would you rather pay…" (line 1, and again on the loop).
- **Loop:** "…4,700 extra." → frame 0, "Would you rather pay 926 a month, or just 453?" There is no closing line; the boards drop again on the S01 lens.
- **Music and SFX:** the toon3d `upbeat` bed. SFX: ding, slide, tick, success, laugh, stamp, a rumble and knock at the door, crash and boom on the burst, impact on the tower, fire, fail, whoosh.
