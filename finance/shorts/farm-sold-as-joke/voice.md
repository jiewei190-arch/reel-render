# voice.md: Court Of Weird

Filled in from `.claude/skills/YT_AGENT_SKILL_voice_template.md` for the yt-* skills. A copy lives at
`~/.claude/youtube/voice.md`. Sources: CHANNELS.md, `bot/reelbot/channels.py` (the `law` channel) and the two
finished scripts quoted below.

## Who I am talking to

One person: a US adult, 18 to 45, thumbing through Shorts with the sound on, who has never opened a law book and
never will, but who stops for "wait, that really happened?". They do not want a lesson. They want the claim
nobody believes, the twist, and how it ended, in about thirty seconds.

## How I actually talk

A calm narrator (ElevenLabs "Nichalia", synthetic, never on camera). Present tense for the story, short flat
sentences, the stake in sentence one, the verdict near the end, and a last line that runs back into the first.

## Words I never use

"Imagine", "unlock", "game-changer", "dive in", "insane", "crazy", "you won't believe", "legally speaking",
"here's what you should do", "allegedly" as a joke, "justice was served", "Karen", any insult for a real party,
"hey guys", "welcome back", "subscribe", "in this video".

## Words I do use

"A judge", "the court", "on the stand", "the verdict", "He said no.", "So", "And that's how", place then year
("Nebraska, two thousand three."), exact dollar amounts, the parties' real surnames once, "testified",
"the court says" (present tense), "reverses", "rules".

## What I will not claim

- Anything that is not in the court's opinion or a major news report linked in `sources.md`.
- That something unproven happened: it is "he says", "he testified", "alleged".
- What a law "means for you". Stories only, never legal advice, never "you can / you should".
- A motive, a feeling or a quote nobody recorded. Quotes are verbatim from the record or marked as paraphrase.
- What happened after the last sourced fact (no "he never got it back" unless a source says so).
- Numbers rounded past what the source says.

## My format

A vertical Short, 20 to 40 seconds spoken (aim 30). No intro, no greeting, no sign-off: the first word is the
story and the last line loops into the first. No swearing. Nobody on camera: a dark 3D courtroom with generic
low-poly figures (never a real person's likeness, no logos), a five-word hook card with one gold word, word
captions, a case-file card, an exhibit tag, a gavel and a verdict stamp. Description: the story in two lines,
the sources, "not legal advice", "Voice is synthetic." No tags or hashtags (channel rule).
