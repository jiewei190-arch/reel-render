What if a joke you wrote on a restaurant check cost you your farm? It happened.
Virginia, nineteen fifty-two. Two men share a bottle of whiskey.
One bets the other won't sell his farm for fifty thousand dollars.
So the owner writes it on a restaurant check. His wife signs too.
In court, he calls it a bluff. He says he was "high as a Georgia pine."
The first judge sides with him. But Virginia's highest court reverses.
It reads what he wrote, not what he secretly meant, and orders the sale.
