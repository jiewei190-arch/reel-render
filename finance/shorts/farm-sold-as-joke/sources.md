# farm-sold-as-joke: sources and claim table

**Case:** *Lucy v. Zehmer*, 196 Va. 493, 84 S.E.2d 516 (Virginia's highest court, decided November 22, 1954).
Opinion by Justice Buchanan. Complainants: W. O. Lucy and J. C. Lucy. Defendants: A. H. Zehmer and Ida S. Zehmer.

## Left out on purpose

- The parties' full names are not spoken; surnames appear only on the case card and folder (kit rule: generic characters).
- The restaurant's name: it is not in the opinion, so it is not used.
- "Unanimous": Wikipedia says so, the opinion itself does not; not used.
- An earlier $20,000 offer, the $5 Lucy offered to bind the bargain, the "forty minutes or more" of discussion: true but cut for length.
