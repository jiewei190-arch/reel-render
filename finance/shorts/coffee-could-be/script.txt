Same five dollars a day. Spender buys a coffee. Saver saves it.
Saver invests his at a made-up, steady seven percent.
One year: Spender has 365 cups. Saver has saved 1,825 dollars.

Ten years: Saver has about 26,000. Spender shrugs. That's it?
Thirty years: about 186,000.
He only put in 54,750. Time did the rest. So what's your five dollars doing?
