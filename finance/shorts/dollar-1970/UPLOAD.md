**Title (45 chars):** Your Dollar Is Worth Under 12¢ in 1970 Money

**Description:**
By the government's consumer price index (CPI-U), average prices are about 8.7× their 1970 level: what cost $1 then costs about $8.70 now.
That's roughly 4% a year for 56 years. Averages: some prices rose more, some less.

Sources:
