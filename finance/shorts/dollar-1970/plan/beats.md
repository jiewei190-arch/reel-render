## Hook candidates

1. **Your dollar is worth under twelve cents.** (7 words) ← PICK: a direct "you" claim with a shocking number; line 2 ("In 1970 money.") pays it off at once.
2. A 1970 dollar is worth eight seventy. (7)
3. Prices went up almost nine times. (6)
4. Your money shrinks while you sleep. (6, no number)
5. Inflation took eighty-eight cents of your dollar. (7)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "Nobody notices a single year. That's exactly why / Your dollar is worth under twelve cents."
- **Twist (~70%):** it's only roughly four percent a year, quietly, for over fifty years.
- **Counter (every shot):** a big price-tag counter `$1 (1970) = $x.xx NOW` rolling from $1.00 to $8.70, and in the NOW shots `YOUR $1 = xx¢` falling from 100¢ to 11.5¢.
