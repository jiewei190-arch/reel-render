# interest-only

- **Spoken:** 70 words, voice 30.59 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S18 ("Interest-only can mean") returns to the S01 lens: Spender beside the untouched OWE $20,000 block after year 5; the loop lands on "five years of payments. Still owe everything."

## Hook candidates

1. **Five years of payments. Still owe everything.** (7 words) ← PICK: a shocking result; the last line ("Interest-only can mean") runs into it.
2. You paid 8,000 and owe 20,000. (6)
3. The cheapest payment cost the most. (6)
4. 133 dollars a month, forever. (5, overstates)
5. Paying every month, owing the same. (6)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "A small payment isn't always cheap. Interest-only can mean / five years of payments. Still owe everything."
- **Twist (~70%):** the full payment (about $406) costs more each month but clears the loan in five years with about $4,332 of interest, vs $8,000 of interest and $20,000 still owed.
- **Counter (every shot):** two lanes: `INTEREST-ONLY $133/MO · PAID $x · OWE $20,000` vs `FULL $406/MO · OWE $y`. VO rounding matches the HUD ($133, $406, $8,000, $4,332).
