# UPLOAD: interest-only

**Title:** 5 Years of Payments, Still Owe It All

**Description:**
Example: borrow $20,000 at a made-up 8%. Interest-only payments are about $133 a month. After five years you've paid $8,000 and still owe all $20,000. The full payment is about $406 a month. That's more each month, but after five years you owe nothing, with about $4,332 of interest in total. A small payment isn't always cheap.
