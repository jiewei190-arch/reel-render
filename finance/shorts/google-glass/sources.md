# google-glass: fact check

Checked 2026-11-23 with live web reads. Company and product names are spoken and shown as plain text only. No logo,
no product artwork and no drawing of the real device: the hero prop is the kit's generic camera labelled "WEARABLE".
Executives are generic and faceless, not likenesses; nobody is named in the voice.
