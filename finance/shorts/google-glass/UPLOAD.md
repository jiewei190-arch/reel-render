## Thumbnail text
THE $1,500 GLASSES

## Description
Google's fifteen-hundred-dollar glasses lasted under two years on sale. In 2012, Google launched them with skydivers landing on the roof of its own conference. Time magazine listed them among the best inventions of the year. Then people noticed the camera. A Seattle bar banned them, and wearers got a nickname. In January 2015, Google stopped selling them to the public. Its lab chief later admitted they encouraged too much attention. A business version lasted until 2023.
