**Title (35 chars):** Why $60 Short Cost $200 in Interest

**Description:**
Example: a $1,200 couch on a "no interest if paid in full in 12 months" store card. Pay $95 a month and you're $60 short at the end, so about $200 of interest from day one (at about 30% APR) comes due at once.
That's deferred interest, not true 0% APR. Terms vary by card.
