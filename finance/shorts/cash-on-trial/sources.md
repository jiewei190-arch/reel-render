## Hedges and limits
- The appeals court **reversed and remanded**; it did not itself order forfeiture. The on-screen stamp says REVERSED, not FORFEITED.
- What happened on remand could not be confirmed, so the video never says the money was kept for good.
- "Never charged" is limited to drug charges, which is all the sources support.
- The government's side (not in the 35 s script, add to the description): the court pointed to the foil-wrapped bundles hidden in a cooler, the dog alert, a rental car in someone else's name, and that he lied to the troopers about carrying cash.
