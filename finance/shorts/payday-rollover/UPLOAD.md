**Title (31 chars):** The $400 Loan That Never Ends

**Description:**
Example: a $400 payday loan at a typical $15 per $100 costs $60 every two weeks. Pay only the fee and roll it over, and after about five months that's $600 in fees with the full $400 still owed.
Fees and rules vary by state; some states cap or ban these loans.
