POV: your boss just offered you 1,500 dollars of free money.
It's a match: save some of your pay, and they add more.
Say you earn 50,000. They match half, up to six percent.
You put in 3,000 a year. Spender, at the next desk, skips it.
Then your first statement arrives...
1,500 extra, from your employer. Fifty cents on every dollar you saved.
Plans differ, and some make you wait to keep it. Skip it, and that 1,500 just isn't yours.
