**Title (25 chars):** Some Jobs Pay You to Save

**Description:**
Example: you earn $50,000 and your employer matches 50% of your contributions up to 6% of pay. You put in $3,000, they add $1,500. Plans differ (match formulas, vesting), and the 2026 employee limit is $24,500.
