# friendster-google-offer: fact check

Checked 2026-11-23 with live web reads. The main source is Gary Rivlin's New York Times article "Wallflower at the Web
Party" (15 Oct 2006), read through the NYT News Service reprint in the Taipei Times because nytimes.com could not be
opened from the build machine: **check the quotes against the NYT original before upload.** Company names are spoken
and shown as plain text only; no logos or site artwork (the hero prop is the kit's generic laptop labelled "FRIENDS").
Executives are generic and faceless; nobody is named in the voice.
