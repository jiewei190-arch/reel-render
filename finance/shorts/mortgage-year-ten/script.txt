You borrow four hundred thousand dollars for a house.
Same house. Spender takes thirty years at about seven percent. Saver takes fifteen.
Spender's first payment: about twenty-seven hundred dollars. Only three hundred twenty pays off the house.
But wait till you see year ten...
Ten years in, Spender has paid three hundred twenty-three thousand... and still owes three hundred forty-four thousand.
Saver paid more monthly, and owes under half.
In this example, Spender's house costs almost a million dollars.
