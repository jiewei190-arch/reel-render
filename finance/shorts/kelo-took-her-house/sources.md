## Hedges, limits and fairness
- **"Still empty" is dated and attributed** ("a report found", tag "2025 REPORT"). Reason is an opinion-leaning outlet; the factual sentence is consistent across three of its articles (2023, 2024, 2025). The 2025 article says a "government-funded recreation center" was under construction near the site, and the 2024 article reports a developer's plan for 500 apartments. The description says the status may have changed. No major wire or newspaper article could be opened from this container to double-check.
- **Not claimed, because only Wikipedia supports it here:** that Pfizer left New London (2009-2010), that Kelo's house was moved to another street (one Reason article says the houses were torn down), the $78 million public cost, the job numbers after the fact, and the count of states that changed their laws (45 or 47, sources differ).
- **"Pink house":** not in the opinion, so not in the script. The slug and title avoid it.
- **The city's side is in the script** (line 4: the jobs; line 5: the majority's reasoning) and in the description.
- "Pfizer", "Motel Six" and "Ritz-Carlton" are spoken or shown as plain text only (the last two are the dissent's own words). The signs on screen say "BUDGET MOTEL" and "LUXURY HOTEL": generic, no logos.
- The framed picture at frame 0 is the kit's generic building photo, not Ms. Kelo's house. The second robed figure is a generic judge, not a likeness of any justice.
