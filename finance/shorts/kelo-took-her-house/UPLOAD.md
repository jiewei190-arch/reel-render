**Description:**
Kelo v. City of New London (2005): the U.S. Supreme Court ruled 5-4 that a city could take homes for an economic development plan. Twenty years later, a report found the lot where Susette Kelo's house stood was still empty.
The plan covered about 90 acres beside a new $300 million Pfizer research facility and was projected to create more than 1,000 jobs.

What the 36 seconds leave out: the city's side was that the area needed jobs and tax revenue, and the majority said promoting economic development is a long-accepted job of government. Nine owners with fifteen properties sued; none of the homes was said to be run down. "Still empty" comes from 2023-2025 reporting about the parcels where the homes stood; a recreation center was reported under construction nearby in 2025, and a developer has announced housing for the area. The status today may have changed.
