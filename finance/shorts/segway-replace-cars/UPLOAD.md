## Description
The Segway cost a hundred million dollars to develop. It was supposed to replace cars. It arrived in 2001. Its inventor said it would do to the car what the car did to the horse and buggy. One investor predicted the fastest climb to a billion dollars in sales, ever. The factory was built for forty thousand a month. In nearly twenty years, about a hundred forty thousand were sold. Production ended in 2020. The lesson: hype is not demand.
