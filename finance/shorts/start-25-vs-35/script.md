# start-25-vs-35: script (draft for MAIN; not voiced)

- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (split: same savings, ten years apart)
- **Hook text (frame 0, ≤ 5 words):** 10 YEARS LATE: $280K LESS
- **Frame 0:** CLOSE: a birthday cake with a '25' candle; a coin drops into a jar beside it with a clink-impact at 0.00 s. Split screen from 1.0 s: Saver at 25 (top) vs Spender at 35 (bottom), the same jar.
- **Two-person race:** Two counters + AGE calendars racing 25→65 and 35→65; the jars fill at different speeds.
- **Open loop:** "...so how bad can it be?" (~55 %): Spender shrugs; the two jars are covered as the calendars flip.
- **Twist:** "about five hundred twenty-eight thousand ... two hundred forty-five thousand" (~80 %): the covers drop; Saver's jar towers over Spender's.
- **Loop:** Last number → frame 0, the '25' candle and the first coin.
- **Easter egg / double payoff:** the blocky cat naps on Spender's jar; double payoff: a '65' candle pops onto the cake.
- **Rules checked:** line 1 has no character names (they come in after 2 s); "you" in line 1; ~77 words (aim 75–85 at a natural pace, 25–29 s); numbers spoken the way the HUD shows them; hedged ("made-up", "about", "at many big banks").

## VO (one line per row; `script.txt` is the same text)
1. Starting ten years late could cost you about two hundred eighty thousand dollars.
2. Saver starts at twenty-five: two hundred dollars a month. Spender waits until thirty-five, then saves the same.
3. At a made-up, steady seven percent, they both stop at sixty-five.
4. Spender only missed ten years of deposits... so how bad can it be?
5. Saver put in ninety-six thousand. Spender, seventy-two thousand.
6. At sixty-five, Saver has about five hundred twenty-eight thousand. Spender, about two hundred forty-five thousand.
