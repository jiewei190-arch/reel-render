# Sources and maths: four-money-minds (MAIN-written)
**Hypothetical example, not advice.** All rates are illustrative assumptions; investments can lose money.
- Saver: $1,000 at an example 4% APY for 1 year = $1,040 (+$40). High-yield savings rates vary.
- Investor: example 7% for 1 year = $1,070 (+$70); shown with a dip because one-year stock returns can be negative.
- Debt Killer: paying off a $1,000 card balance at an example 22% APR avoids about $220 of interest over a year
  (simple, 1000 x 0.22; monthly compounding would be ~$244, so "+$220" is conservative).
- Spender: spends it, $0.
- "The boring one won": in this example, a guaranteed 22% saved beats 4% and an uncertain 7%.
