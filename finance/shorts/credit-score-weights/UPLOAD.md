**Title:** The 5 Slices of a Credit Score

**Description:**
A widely used credit score publishes its weights: payment history 35%, amounts owed 30%, length of credit history 15%, new credit 10%, credit mix 10%. The second-biggest slice isn't paying on time. It's how much of your available credit you use. A high reported balance can count even if you pay on time. The weights vary by person and by scoring model.
