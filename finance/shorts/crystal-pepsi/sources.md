# crystal-pepsi: fact check

Checked 2026-11-23 with live web reads. Brand names are spoken and shown as plain text only. No logos, no label
artwork, no trade dress: the hero props are the kit's generic cans in plain pale grey, labelled "CLEAR" and
"CLEAR TOO". Executives are generic and faceless; nobody is named in the voice.
