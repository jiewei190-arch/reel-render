## Description
Pepsi's forty-million-dollar clear cola was gone in about a year. That was the reported marketing budget for Crystal Pepsi, which hit the Super Bowl in 1993. Then Coca-Cola launched a clear cola of its own: Tab Clear. The Coke marketer behind it later called it a suicidal mission, meant to take Crystal Pepsi down with it. Both drinks disappeared. Crystal Pepsi's creator called it the best idea he ever had, and the worst executed. His lesson: listen to people before you launch.
