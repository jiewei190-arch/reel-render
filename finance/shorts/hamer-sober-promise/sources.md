## Hedges, limits and fairness
- **Who won:** the nephew had assigned the claim (to his wife, then on to Louisa W. Hamer), so the script says "the claim wins", never "the nephew got paid". The description explains it.
- **The uncle is not the villain:** the script says the money was "still in his hands"; the description adds that he held it by agreement, on interest. He never refused.
- **"To stay sober"** in line 1 is shorthand; line 3 gives all four conditions. "No gambling" = "playing cards or billiards for money".
- **The stamp says ENFORCED**, a plain-English label for "judgment of Special Term affirmed"; the exact disposition is in this file and the date on the stamp is the decision date.
- **Not claimed:** the nephew's age at the time of the promise (not stated in the opinion text read), the amount finally paid with interest, and anything about what contract law says today.
- One birthday reference in the opinion body reads "1845"; the head matter and every other reference say 1875 (scan or printing error).
