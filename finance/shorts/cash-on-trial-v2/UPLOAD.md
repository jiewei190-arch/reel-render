**Description**
A 2003 traffic stop on a Nebraska highway. A cooler on the back seat. Seven foil-wrapped bundles: $124,700. No drugs
were found and no drug charges were brought, so the government sued the money itself. That is a real case:
United States v. $124,700 in U.S. Currency (8th Cir. 2006).

Both sides: the appeals court pointed to the foil-wrapped bundles hidden in a cooler, a drug dog's alert to the car and
the cash, a rental car in another person's name, and that the driver first told the troopers he had no cash. The driver
said three people had pooled the money to buy a refrigerated truck for a produce business, and the trial judge ruled
for him. On Aug 18, 2006 the appeals court reversed, 2 to 1, and sent the case back.

Source: the court's opinion, No. 05-3295, 458 F.3d 822. This is a story about a real case, not legal advice.
