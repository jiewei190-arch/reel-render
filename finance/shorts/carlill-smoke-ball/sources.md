## Hedges, limits and fairness
- **Dates:** only the year 1892 (the appeal) is confirmed from the judgment page. The commonly given dates (ad of 13 November 1891, use from 20 November 1891 to 17 January 1892, judgment of 7 December 1892) appear on Wikipedia but not in the text read, so the voice says "Victorian England" and no day or month is shown.
- **Not claimed, for the same reason:** her first name, the 10-shilling price, "Regent Street", what a smoke ball contained, the 1889-90 pandemic, and the often-repeated story of how long she lived.
- **"PAY HER"** is a plain-English stamp for "the defendants must perform their promise" / "Appeal dismissed"; the description gives the exact wording.
- **The company's side** is line 6 and most of the description. Nothing in the video says the product was a fraud; it says what the ad promised and what the court held.
- The evidence box labelled "SMOKE BALL" is a generic kit prop, not a depiction of the product; no trade dress or logo is shown.
