Seventy-two tells you when your money doubles.
Divide that by the yearly interest rate.
Six percent? About twelve years.
Eight percent? About nine.
Twelve percent? About six.
It's a shortcut, not a promise. Real returns go up and down.
Now flip it.
A card charging twenty-four percent can double what you owe in about three years.
Same maths, wrong side. So remember...
