**Title (48 chars):** The One Number That Tells You When Money Doubles

**Description:**
Divide 72 by the yearly rate and you get roughly how many years it takes to double: 6% ≈ 12 years, 8% ≈ 9, 12% ≈ 6.
Flip it: a 24% card balance with no payments can double in about 3 years. A shortcut, not a promise; real returns vary.
