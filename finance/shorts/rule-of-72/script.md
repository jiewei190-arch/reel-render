# rule-of-72

- **Spoken:** 81 words, voice 29.00 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.55, cfg 0.3; voiced on the PC GPU).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S16 on "That's why you should know these": the S01 lens on Spender with his card again; the loop lands on "Three things that double." at frame 0.

## Hook candidates

1. **Seventy-two tells you when your money doubles.** (7 words) ← PICK: one number + "your money", and the last line ("So remember...") flows into it.
2. Your money doubles on a schedule. (6)
3. Divide by this number. Know when you're rich. (8, and drifts into a get-rich promise: rejected)
4. One number predicts when money doubles. (6)
5. Your card debt can double in three years. (8, strong but spoils the twist)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "Same maths, wrong side. So remember... / Seventy-two tells you when your money doubles."
- **Twist (~70%):** "Now flip it": the same rule doubles card debt in about three years.
- **Counter (every shot):** a big flip-counter `72 ÷ RATE = YEARS` (e.g. `72 ÷ 6 = 12`), its numbers flipping on each rate; red in the debt half.
