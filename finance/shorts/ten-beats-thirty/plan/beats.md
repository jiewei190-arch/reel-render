## Hook candidates

1. **Ten years of saving can beat thirty.** (7 words) ← PICK: a shocking claim with numbers; "can" keeps it honest; the last line ("That's how") flows into it.
2. You can save less and end with more. (8)
3. Twenty-four thousand beats seventy-two thousand. (5)
4. Starting late costs you more than money. (7, vague)
5. Your thirties can beat your whole career. (7, over-claims)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "But time did the heavy lifting. That's how / Ten years of saving can beat thirty."
- **Twist (~70%):** at 65, Saver (put in $24,000) has about $280,000; Spender (put in $72,000) has $245,000.
- **Counter (every shot):** an `AGE nn` sign over each plot and a balance counter on each jar (green for Saver, red for Spender) ticking up; a small `EXAMPLE 7%/YR` badge stays on screen.
