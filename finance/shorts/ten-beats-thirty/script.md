# ten-beats-thirty

- **Spoken:** 74 words, voice 29.84 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S23 ("That's how") returns to the S01 lens: both characters at their empty jars at the start, Saver already dropping the first coin; the loop lands on "Ten years".

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "But time did the heavy lifting. That's how / Ten years of saving can beat thirty."
- **Twist (~70%):** at 65, Saver (put in $24,000) has about $280,000; Spender (put in $72,000) has $245,000.
- **Counter (every shot):** an `AGE nn` sign over each plot and a balance counter on each jar (green for Saver, red for Spender) ticking up; a small `EXAMPLE 7%/YR` badge stays on screen.
