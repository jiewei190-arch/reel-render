**Title (34 chars):** How 10 Years of Saving Can Beat 30

**Description:**
Hypothetical: $200/month from 25 to 35, then stop, vs $200/month from 35 to 65, both at a steady 7%/yr (an example rate, not a prediction).
$24,000 in grows to ≈ $282,600; $72,000 in grows to ≈ $245,400. Real returns aren't steady and nothing is guaranteed; the point is time.
