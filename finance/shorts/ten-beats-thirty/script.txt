Ten years of saving can beat thirty.
Saver puts away two hundred a month from twenty-five to thirty-five, then stops.
Spender saves the same two hundred from thirty-five to sixty-five.
Saver put in twenty-four thousand. Spender, seventy-two thousand.
Assume a steady seven percent a year, just as an example.
At sixty-five, Saver has about two hundred eighty thousand. Spender, about two hundred forty-five.
Real returns aren't steady, and nothing is guaranteed. But time did the heavy lifting.
