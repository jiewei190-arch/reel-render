# same-raise: script (draft for MAIN; not voiced)

- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (split: the same raise, two choices)
- **Hook text (frame 0, ≤ 5 words):** SAME RAISE. $100K APART.
- **Frame 0:** CLOSE: a paycheck slams onto a desk with '+$400/MO RAISE' stamped on it (stamp-impact at 0.00 s); two hands grab for it. Split screen from 1.0 s: Spender (top) vs Saver (bottom), the same desk.
- **Two-person race:** Two counters: SPENDER: SPENT vs SAVER: INVESTED (value), side by side for 20 years.
- **Open loop:** "Nice, but not life-changing... yet." (~55 %): a covered YEAR 20 board wobbles.
- **Twist:** "After twenty years: about a hundred and five thousand" (~75 %): the cover flies off; Saver's coin pile erupts.
- **Loop:** "...still has a car payment." → frame 0, the raise paycheck slamming down.
- **Easter egg / double payoff:** a 'BLOCK MONEY' mug on the desk; double payoff: Spender's new car keys fall into the coin pile.
- **Rules checked:** line 1 has no character names (they come in after 2 s); "you" in line 1; ~75 words (aim 75–85 at a natural pace, 25–29 s); numbers spoken the way the HUD shows them; hedged ("made-up", "about", "at many big banks").

## VO (one line per row; `script.txt` is the same text)
1. You and your coworker get the same four hundred dollar a month raise.
2. Spender spends all of it on a bigger car payment. Saver spends half, and invests two hundred a month.
3. At a made-up, steady seven percent, after ten years Saver has about thirty-five thousand.
4. Nice, but not life-changing... yet.
5. After twenty years: about a hundred and five thousand. And he still enjoyed half the raise.
6. Same raise. Spender still has a car payment.
