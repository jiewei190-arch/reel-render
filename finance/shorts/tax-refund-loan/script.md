# tax-refund-loan

- **Spoken:** 75 words, voice 27.08 s (D2 Chatterbox, exaggeration 0.3, cfg 0.3).
- **Hook:** line 1, spoken from 0.0 s, no intro.
- **Ending:** stops mid-thought, no call to action; the last line runs straight into line 1 when the Short loops.
- **Loop:** S19 ("But every spring,") returns to the S01 lens: Teller slides the REFUND $3,366 case to Saver again; the loop lands on "your tax refund is a free loan."

## Hook candidates

1. **Your tax refund is a free loan.** (7 words) ← PICK: a "you" claim that flips a happy thing; the last line ("But every spring,") runs into it.
2. The government borrowed 3,366 dollars from you. (7, not true for everyone)
3. Your refund isn't a bonus. (5, gives away the twist)
4. 280 dollars a month, zero interest. (6)
5. Who pays you interest on a refund? (7)

## Beats
- **Loop sentence:** "A refund can still be a fine choice. But every spring, / your tax refund is a free loan."
- **Twist (~70%, line 6, after the W-4 line):** a refund isn't a bonus: it's your own withheld pay, sent back late.
- **Counter (every shot):** `AVG REFUND $3,366` → `÷ 12 = $280/MO` → `INTEREST $0`. VO says "3,366" and "About 280" (280.50), HUD shows $3,366 and ≈$280.
