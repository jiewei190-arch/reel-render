**Title:** Your Tax Refund Is a Free Loan

**Description:**
IRS filing season data to Oct. 16, 2026: the average refund was $3,366, about $280 a month. Money withheld from your pay comes back without interest when it's refunded. A refund isn't a bonus. Much of it is your own pay, returned later. Your W-4 sets your withholding, and the IRS has a free estimator to check it. A refund can still be a reasonable choice.

Sources:
- IRS, Filing season statistics (week ending Oct. 16, 2026): https://www.irs.gov/node/167596
- IRS, Tax Withholding Estimator: https://www.irs.gov/individuals/tax-withholding-estimator
- IRS, About Form W-4: https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-w-4
