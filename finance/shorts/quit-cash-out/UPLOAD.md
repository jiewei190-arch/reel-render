**Title (36 chars):** You Quit. Your $10,000 Became $7,800

**Description:**
Example: two people leave a job at 30 with $10,000 in a 401(k). One cashes out: a 10% early-withdrawal tax plus income tax (12% in this example) leaves about $7,800. The other rolls it over; at about 7% a year it grows to over $100,000 by 65. About a third of people who leave a job cash out. Hypothetical; taxes and returns vary.
