**Description:**
Katko v. Briney, the Iowa "spring gun" case: a man broke into an empty farmhouse to steal old jars, was shot by a wired shotgun, sued the owners, and a jury awarded him $30,000.
The Iowa Supreme Court upheld the verdict in 1971, with one justice dissenting.

What the 37 seconds leave out: the Brineys had boarded the house up and posted "no trespass" signs after about ten years of break-ins, but nothing warned of the gun. Marvin Katko pleaded guilty to larceny, was fined $50 and paroled from a 60-day sentence. The jury gave $20,000 in actual damages and $10,000 in punitive damages, and the court said it was not deciding whether punitive damages are allowed in a case like this, because the point was not properly raised at trial.
