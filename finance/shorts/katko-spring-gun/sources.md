## Hedges, limits and fairness
- **No crime label.** The script says "he pleaded guilty to larceny", which is what the opinion records. It never calls him a burglar.
- **The owners' side is in the script:** ten years of break-ins, a boarded-up house, and the gun lowered to the legs. The description adds that they had posted "no trespass" signs "several years before 1967", that no sign warned of the gun, and that one justice dissented.
- **Injury detail kept out of the voice:** the opinion says "Much of his leg, including part of the tibia, was blown away" and that he wore a cast "for approximately a year". The script only gives the hospital stay.
- **Punitive damages:** the majority wrote "We express no opinion as to whether punitive damages are allowable in this type of case" (the point was not raised properly at trial). The description says so.
- **Not claimed:** what the Brineys did to pay the judgment (Wikipedia says they sold 80 of their 120 acres; not verified from a primary source or news report, so it is not in the video), and anything about later Iowa law. No legal advice: the Short tells what one court decided in 1971.
- The boxes labelled "OLD JARS" and "SHOTGUN" are generic evidence boxes, an illustration, not a depiction of the real exhibits.
