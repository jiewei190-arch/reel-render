## Hedges, limits and fairness
- **"By their account"** marks the whisper as the Zehmers' testimony, not a finding.
- **"He sold his farm"** in line 1 is the outcome the court reached; the script then gives his defence in his own words (line 7) and says the court rejected it. The description gives the trial court's opposite ruling.
- **No "drunk" label in the title or thumbnail:** the court found he was not too intoxicated to contract, so the title "A Drunk Joke Cost Him His 471-Acre Farm" (the linter's top score) was rejected.
- **Not claimed:** whether the sale later closed, what the farm was really worth (the opinion mentions an $11,000 purchase price and a $6,300 assessment), anything about contract law today.
- The papers on the desk carry generic titles ("GUEST CHECK", "AGREE TO SELL", "TITLE REPORT"); they illustrate, they are not copies of the exhibits.
