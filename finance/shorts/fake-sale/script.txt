Your hundred dollar deal was probably never a hundred dollars.
In this example: same jacket, same store. Spender sees "was one hundred, now sixty!" and grabs it.
Saver checks back every week. The price never moves.
But that's not even the sneaky part...
Consumer watchdogs tracked twenty-five big chains for six months.
At nineteen of them, items were "on sale" about seventy-six percent of the time.
The sixty dollar price wasn't a sale. It was just the price.
