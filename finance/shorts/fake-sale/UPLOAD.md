**Title (24 chars):** Your "Deal" Wasn't a Deal

**Description:**
Example: "was $100, now $60". A consumer watchdog checked prices at 25 national chains every week for 6 months. At 19 of them, items were labelled "on sale" about 76% of the time, so the "sale" price was often just the usual price. Results differ by store.

Sources:
- Consumers' Checkbook, "Sale Fail" (Nov 2025): https://www.checkbook.org/national/sale-fail/
- Harvard Business School Working Knowledge, fake original prices and what shoppers pay: https://www.library.hbs.edu/working-knowledge/bargain-hunters-beware-a-store-s-original-price-might-not-be-after-all
