# fake-sale: script (voiced: Tyler, 29.5 s)

- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (same jacket, same store: Spender grabs the "deal", Saver watches the price for weeks)
- **Hook text (frame 0, 4 words):** IT WAS NEVER $100
- **Frame 0:** hookZoom on Spender's grinning face; a red "SALE $60" tag slams onto his chest (impact + cash at 0.00 s); joy → shock at 0.7 s.
- **Beat 2 (~1.5 s):** slamZoom on a "WAS $100" board; a NEVER stamp hits it on "never".
- **Race:** split screen SAVER PAID vs SPENDER PAID; Spender celebrates "NOW $60!" and swipes his card; Saver's WEEK counter runs 1→6 next to "STILL $60".
- **Open loop (~45 %, "But that's not even the sneaky part..."):** a clothing rack with covered "SALE? $??" tags, trembling.
- **Mechanism:** "25 CHAINS" block drops, a "TRACKED 6 MONTHS" calendar flies in.
- **Twist (~80 %, "seventy-six percent"):** "ON SALE" board counts 0 → 76 % OF THE TIME, flash + shake, Spender shock.
- **Ending:** split: both paid $60. "It was just the price." → loop to frame 0 "Your hundred dollar deal..." with the SALE tag dropping again.
- **Eggs / double payoff:** cat + sticky note; finalGag catCoin.
- **Rules checked:** line 1: no names, "you" + $ (10 words); hedged ("probably", "In this example", "about"); no store names (the study names chains; the VO and screen don't); no advice or CTA.

## VO (= script.txt)
1. Your hundred dollar deal was probably never a hundred dollars.
2. In this example: same jacket, same store. Spender sees "was one hundred, now sixty!" and grabs it.
3. Saver checks back every week. The price never moves.
4. But that's not even the sneaky part...
5. Consumer watchdogs tracked twenty-five big chains for six months.
6. At nineteen of them, items were "on sale" about seventy-six percent of the time.
7. The sixty dollar price wasn't a sale. It was just the price.
