# credit-score-car: script (draft for MAIN; not voiced)

- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (split: same car, two credit scores)
- **Hook text (frame 0, ≤ 5 words):** SAME CAR. $6,000 MORE?
- **Frame 0:** CLOSE: two identical car keys slam onto a desk side by side; one tag reads 6.5%, the other 13% (impact SFX at 0.00 s). Split screen from 1.0 s: Saver (top) vs Spender (bottom) at the same dealer desk.
- **Two-person race:** Two counters: INTEREST PAID for each buyer, ticking up month by month (to ≈$5,200 vs ≈$11,300).
- **Open loop:** "Both payments look fine at first... but watch the totals." (~50 %): two covered TOTAL boards; the month counters start ticking.
- **Twist:** "about five thousand in interest, versus eleven thousand" (~80 %): the covers fly off; Spender's interest pile is twice as tall.
- **Loop:** "...Same car." → frame 0, the two keys slamming down.
- **Easter egg / double payoff:** the 19-year TV from the TV Short in the dealer's back office; double payoff: Spender's key ring snaps.
- **Rules checked:** line 1 has no character names (they come in after 2 s); "you" in line 1; ~71 words (aim 75–85 at a natural pace, 25–29 s); numbers spoken the way the HUD shows them; hedged ("made-up", "about", "at many big banks").

## VO (one line per row; `script.txt` is the same text)
1. Same thirty thousand dollar car, same five years, and one buyer pays about six thousand dollars more.
2. The only difference? Their credit scores.
3. Saver's prime score gets about six and a half percent. Spender's subprime score gets about thirteen.
4. Both payments look fine at first... but watch the totals.
5. Five hundred eighty-seven a month, versus six hundred eighty-eight.
6. After five years: about five thousand in interest, versus eleven thousand. Same car.
