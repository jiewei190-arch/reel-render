One man got Google and Facebook to wire him over $100 million.
He didn't hack them. He sent emails.
Both giants did business with a real hardware maker in Taiwan.
So he set up a company in Latvia with the exact same name.
Then came fake emails, fake invoices, even fake corporate stamps.
From 2013 to 2015, they paid. Over $120 million in total.
The money scattered fast: Cyprus, Slovakia, Hungary, Hong Kong.
But wires leave a trail. In 2017, Lithuanian authorities arrested him.
Extradited to New York. Five years in prison.
