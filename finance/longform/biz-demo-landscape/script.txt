Blockbuster could have bought Netflix for fifty million dollars.
It was 2000. Netflix mailed DVDs, and it was losing money.
Its founders flew to Dallas and offered to sell.
By one founder's account, Blockbuster's boss struggled not to laugh.
The answer was no. Blockbuster ruled the video store.
By 2004 it had about nine thousand stores, and late fees poured in.
Then Netflix went streaming, and the stores went dark, one by one.
In 2010, Blockbuster filed for bankruptcy, with over a billion dollars in debt.
Netflix today? Worth over two hundred fifty billion dollars.
