# wedding-or-keys: script (draft for MAIN)

- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (split: the same $34,200, a one-night party vs a pile of savings)
- **Voice:** Christopher
- **Hook text (frame 0, ≤ 5 words):** $34,000 IN ONE NIGHT
- **Frame 0:** CLOSE on a plain blocky face (Spender, unnamed until line 2) in party mode, confetti already falling; a "$34,200" cash block drops and slams (impact at 0.00 s), a clock behind reads 7:00 PM.
- **Two-person race:** split from line 2. Top: Spender's party, a counter LEFT draining from $34,200 (≈ $114 a minute, sped up). Bottom: Saver's pile of the same $34,200, untouched, a covered sign beside it.
- **Mechanism visual (line 3-4):** guests walk in; each one knocks a "$292" block off Spender's stack; a ticking clock and the minute counter.
- **Open loop (≈ 58-65 %):** "But Saver's pile hides a bigger number..." Saver's covered sign trembles.
- **Twist (≈ 67-83 %):** the sign uncovers: a house with "DOWN PAYMENT $37,500"; Saver's pile fills a bar to 91 %. Top half: Spender's counter hits $0 as the clock hits midnight.
- **Payoff / loop:** "ninety percent of a down payment, gone by midnight." Clock strikes 12, the last confetti falls → loop to frame 0's cash slam.
- **Eggs:** sticky note on the cake: "PLUS 1 = +$292"; the cat walks off with a coin from the gift table. Final gag: catCoin.
- **Rules checked:** line 1 has "you", a dollar figure and no names (9 words); 78 words; hedged ("about", "in this example", "typical"); no judgement line, no advice, no brands, no CTA. Weddings are a choice; the Short shows the size of the number, not what to do.
