## Why these 2
- **quit-cash-out:** strongest pain (a moment everyone faces, and a third of people make the costly choice), a two-person race is natural (same balance, two choices), and the twist number is ~14x the cash in hand. Facts come straight from the IRS.
- **fee-one-percent:** the "tiny number, huge cost" contrast is the shock, and the twist "the fee took more than you ever put in" is concrete and checkable. Mechanism sourced from SEC/Investor.gov. Different from the taken topics (rule-of-72, start-25-vs-35 are about time, not cost).
- Both are S2 races (the format with our best hold, 85 %), on different sets (office vs street) and different opening actions.
