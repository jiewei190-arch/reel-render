## Picks and why
1. **delivery-markup** (Christopher): the most-argued everyday spend right now (viral $25k story, Gen Z delivery-debt coverage), a sharp sourced number ($12 meal → about $21; +$9.30 an order) and a natural two-person race (walk vs app). Twist: twice a week = nearly $1,000 a year just for the door.
2. **fake-sale** (Tyler): betrayal pain ("my deal wasn't a deal") plus a watchdog number (on sale ~76 % of the time). It lands in Black Friday season. A clean race: Spender grabs the "deal", Saver watches the price for weeks and it never moves.

Both avoid brands in the VO (no chain or app names), give no advice and are hedged ("in this example", "about").
