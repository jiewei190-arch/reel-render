# Lane E research: BIG LIFE MONEY (2026-11-19)

Method: web searches for viral mortgage, student-loan, wedding and housing money clips (TikTok and news write-ups of them), Reddit and forum threads, plus the newest official data. View counts were rarely visible in the cloud (TikTok and Reddit pages don't render here); where a write-up exists I cite it. Treat "viral" as "covered by several outlets", not a measured count.

## Picks and why

1. **mortgage-year-ten** (Tyler). The strongest relatable pain in the lane, and the most shocking true number I found: after **10 years** of payments on a $400K loan at today's 7.11 %, you've paid **$323K and still owe $344K**. You've paid more than you owe and still owe almost as much as you paid. It's simple maths from an official rate, easy to show (two counters), and the 15- vs 30-year split gives a fair S2 race with its trade-off shown, with no advice.
2. **wedding-or-keys** (Christopher). The biggest comment-war topic in the lane, and a clean S2 split: the same $34,200 as one night vs a down-payment pile. Shocking but true: one average wedding is about **90 %** of a typical first-time down payment (9 % of the median home), and it's about **$114 a minute** over a five-hour party (an assumption flagged as "in this example"). We show the size, not a verdict; viewers argue in the comments.

Rejected for now: student loans (too many plan-specific caveats for a 29-s honest example; good for a later S1 with a single named plan), rent and cars (overlap with taken topics).
