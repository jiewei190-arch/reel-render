- **Style:** S2 RICH-VS-POOR (split: same couch, same store card, $100 vs $95 a month)
- **Voice:** Christopher (ElevenLabs, eleven_v4), 475 chars, 29.5 s (time-stretched x1.13 to fit)
- **Hook text (frame 0, 4 words):** 0% ISN'T ALWAYS 0%
- **Frame 0:** hookZoom close-up, a grinning face (joy → curious at 0.7 s) with a green "0% FOR 1 YEAR" sign slammed onto the chest; impact + ding at 0.00 s.
- **First 3 s:** cut at "couch" to a slam-zoom on the "$1,200" tag over the sofa; cut at "no" to a close-up with a NO INTEREST* sign flying in.
- **Race:** split screen, cards in hand; $100/MO (Saver) vs $95/MO (Spender); at "Twelve months later" two LEFT TO PAY counters run $1,200 → $0 (Saver celebrates) and $1,200 → $60. "$60 SHORT", shrug on "Close enough?".

- **Mechanism + twist (~62-87 %):** an x-ray-style red INTEREST jar filling with coins "from day one", a HIDDEN INTEREST counter $0 → $203, "~30% APR"; then the slam "~$200 INTEREST" (flash) on "two hundred".

- **Eggs:** tv19 + cat (home set); **double payoff:** tvFall.
- **Rules:** line 1 = "Your" + $ figure + a time, 11 words, no names; hedged ("about thirty percent", "about two hundred", tag "EXAMPLE · DEFERRED INTEREST"); no store names, no CTA, no advice.
