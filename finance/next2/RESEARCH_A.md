# Lane A research: DEBT TRAPS (2026-11-19)

Goal: the 2 topics with the strongest relatable PAIN plus a shocking but TRUE number.
Method: web search (news, CFPB/Fed/Pew, Reddit coverage). **Limits:** no direct TikTok/YouTube view-count scraping in this session. I did not use the vidIQ connector, because it spends the owner's credits. View counts below are the ones the cited articles report.

## Why these 2
- **Payday rollover:** the cleanest "true but unbelievable" maths in the lane: **$600 paid on a $400 loan and still owing all $400**. That's simple arithmetic from CFPB's typical fee and Pew's average time in debt. The pain is relatable (a car repair, a short paycheck). It builds into a ticking counter, and S2 adds a clean foil: Saver pays $400 once.
- **Deferred interest:** a counter-intuitive twist that rewards the watch: **$60 short → ~$200 interest charged back to day one**. The trap is "almost doing it right", which is more relatable than reckless spending. CFPB itself publishes the mechanism with a worked example. Retail-card APRs at record highs make it current (2024-25 data).
- Both avoid brands, use numbers from CFPB/Fed/Pew, and pay the hook off without bait.
