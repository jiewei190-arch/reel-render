## Why these 2
- **free-trial-204:** universal and relatable (almost half admit it), clean race (reminder vs "I'll remember"), a ticking counter (day 1 -> 8, month 2 -> 12),
  and the twist number ($204) is the survey's own average ($205 a year, ~$17 a month), so the example is not cherry-picked.
- **atm-fee-twenty:** pure "your bank charges you for being broke" pain (FORMAT_V1 day-1 fix 6), one hard number from the newest study ($4.86, Sept 2025),
  and a shock ratio a viewer can feel: about a quarter of the cash. The yearly total ($505) is the twist. Different set (street/bank) from overdraft-35.
